Measurement of transference number (tLi+)  
The tLi+ of the electrolytes was determined using Li//electrolyte//Li symmetric cell. A positive 
voltage of 10 mV was applied between the electrodes of the symmetric cells, and the steady-state 
current (Iss) and initial current (Io) were recorded via chronoamperometry. The EIS spectra of the 
symmetric cells were also obtained before and after polarization to measure the initial resistance 
(Ro) and steady-state resistance (Rss). Finally, the tLi+ of the electrolytes was calculated using the 
following equation (S1).
