EXPERIMENTAL SECTION 
Materials. Elemental sulfur, 1,3-dioxolane (DOL), dimethyl ether (DME), pyridine (PN), 3-
fluoropyridine (3-FPN), dimethyl sulfoxide (DMSO), lithium bis(trifluoromethane) 
sulfonamide (LiTFSI), Polytetrafluoroethylene (PTFE), and poly(vinylidene difluoride) 
(PVDF, Mw = 560000) were purchased from Sigma-Aldrich, USA. N-methyl-2-pyrrolidone 
(NMP) was purchased from Junsei Chemical, Japan. All materials were used without further 
purification. 
 
Synthesis of S/C composite. Elemental sulfur and activated carbon in a mass ratio of 7:3 were 
homogenously grounded and charged in a pyrex ampoule, which was purged with Ar and kept 
under a vacuum while being sealed. The ampoule was transferred into a box furnace and heated 
at 160 °C for 12 h with a ramping rate of 1 °C min–1 to allow the sulfur to melt and 
homogeneously infiltrate into activated carbon. The ampoule was then cooled down to room 
temperature and opened. The resulting S/C composite was grounded before further analysis. 
Physicochemical characterization. 
UV-vis spectroscopy. A solution of Li2S6 (0.1M) was prepared by dissolving stoichiometric 
amounts of Li2S and elemental sulfur in each solvent and stirred at room temperature overnight. 
The concentration of Li2S6 was then diluted to 1 mM for the UV-vis spectroscopy analysis. 
The UV quartz cuvettes were filled with the solutions in an Ar-filled glovebox. Ultraviolet-
visible (UV-vis) spectroscopy analysis was carried out using a Lambda 25 UV/vis 
Spectrophotometer (Perkin Elmer, Switzerland).  
X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS). The surface chemistries of the Li symmetric cells 
were analyzed after one cycle (current density: 0.5 mA cm−2; areal capacity: 0.5 mAh cm−2) 
using X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS). XPS analysis was performed by using an Axis-

Supra (KRATOS, United Kingdom) spectrometer. For the XPS measurements, the Li

Field emission scanning electron microscopy (FE-SEM). The morphological analysis was 
performed by using VGCF as the conducting agent for the 16 π cathode and 30 µL catholyte 
comprising 0.08 M Li2S6 and 1 M LiTFSI in (a) DOL/DME and (b) 3-FPN solvents. The 
surface morphologies of the Li2S deposits on VGCF electrodes were studied after discharge to 
1.8 V at 0.03C. FE-SEM analysis was conducted on a JSM-7800F Prime instrument (JEOL 
Ltd, Japan) at the National Centre for Inter-university Research Facilities (NCIRF) at Seoul 
National University. For the FE-SEM measurements, the electrodes were collected and washed 
with the respective electrolytes (DOL/DME, PN, and 3-FPN) in an Ar-filled glove box. 
 
Electrolyte preparation. The corresponding electrolytes were prepared by dissolving lithium 
bis(trifluoromethane)sulfonimide (LiTFSI) in the respective solvents for 6 h in Ar-filled glove 
box and are referred to as 1 M LiTFSI-PN, 1 M LiTFSI-3-FPN and 1 M LiTFSI-DOL/DME.  
Coin cell preparation. All electrochemical tests were performed by fabricating CR2032-type 
coin cells. The electrode material composed of S/C composite, carbon black and PVDF in a 
mass ratio of 7:2:1 was dispersed in N-methyl-2-pyrrolidone to form a slurry, which was then 
cast onto aluminum foil (20 µm, Hohsen, Japan) using the doctor blade technique. The 
electrodes were dried in a convection oven at 60 °C overnight. The areal sulfur loadings were 
fixed at 1.0 and 2.5 mgsulfur cm−2 and the area of cathode discs was fixed to 16 π. For the sulfur 
loadings of 8.0 and 10.0 mgsulfur cm–2, 12 π cathode composed of S/C composite, carbon black, 
and PTFE in a mass ratio of 6:2:2 were pressed into a pellet. Coin cells were fabricated in an 
Ar-filled glove box by adopting 16 π Li metal disc as the counter electrode and 18 π 
polypropylene membrane (Celgard 2400) as a separator. Electrolyte-to-sulfur ratio was fixed

Electrochemical characterization. Galvanostatic discharge-charge tests were performed at 
various scan rates in the potential range of 1.8–2.7 V vs Li/Li+ using PEBC050.01 battery 
cycler (PNE solution, South Korea). The cells were cycled at 0.1C after one pre-cycling step 
at 0.03C. For Li symmetric cell tests, the current density was 0.5 mA cm−2 with an areal 
capacity of 0.5 mAh cm−2. Linear sweep voltammetry (LSV) was performed using an 
aluminum current collector with a scan rate of 0.1 mV s−1. EIS analysis was conducted in the 
frequency range of 1 MHz–0.1 Hz with an amplitude of 10 mV at the open circuit potential 
using a VSP instrument (Bio-Logic, France). 16 π of S/C electrode, 16 π of Li-metal counter 
electrode, and 18 π polypropylene membrane (Celgard 2400) separator were used for EIS 
measurements. S/C electrodes after 10 cycles were charged to 2.7 V prior to EIS analysis. The 
current densities and specific capacities were all calculated based on the sulfur weight. The 
potentiostatic reaction tests, cells were galvanostatically discharged at 0.1C to 2.10 V, followed 
by a potential shift to 2.05 V at which the output current was monitored during the polysulfides 
conversion reaction by using a WBCS 3000 battery cycler (Wonatech, South Korea).
