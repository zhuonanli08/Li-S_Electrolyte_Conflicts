Figure S11. CV curves from 0.1 to 0.5 mV s−1, CV curves of Li−SPAN batteries (a) with 
Li2Se and (b) without Li2Se in the voltage range of 1.0-3.0 V; (c) Linear fit profiles 
obtained from oxidation peaks of the CV curves; (d) Linear fit profiles obtained from 
reduction peaks of the CV curves.
Lithium-ion (Li+) diﬀusion coefficients for Li−SPAN batteries with/without Li2Se were 
calculated by a series of cyclic voltammograms with various scan rates and were analyzed 
with the Randles-Sevick equation: Ip =2.69 × 105 n1.5 A DLi+ 0.5 CLi+ v0.5. In the equation, Ip 
represented the peak current (A), n was the number of electrons transformed in the reaction 
(2 for Li−SPAN battery), CLi+ represented the Li+ concentration (mol L−1) and v referred 
to the scanning rate (V s−1). A was the area of the cathode (cm−2). DLi+ stood for Li+ 
diﬀusion coefficient (cm−2 s−1).
