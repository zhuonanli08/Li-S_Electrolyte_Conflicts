Figure S12. (a) The exchange reaction mechanism of radicals in DMTS modified electrolyte during cycling. DMTS undergoes homolytic bond cleavage into radical CH3S• and CH3SS• and then radicals can couple react with each other. The recombination of radicals and sulfur atoms make the generation of CH3S4CH3. Subsequently, the successive cleavage and randomly exchange reaction between all radicals ensure the formation of CH3SnCH3 (4 ≤ n ≤ 7). The radical reaction processes were simply conveyed as CH3SnCH3 + CH3SS• ⇌ CH3Sn+1CH3 + CH3S•, 3 ≤ n ≤ 6. (b) Radical CH3Sn• (1 ≤ n ≤ 3) can react with Li+ to form CH3SnLi (1 ≤ n ≤ 3), which would change into CH3SLi step by step in the form of Li2S, without the formation of lithium polysulfides during the whole discharge process.
