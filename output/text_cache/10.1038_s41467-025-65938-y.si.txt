Supplementary Fig. 22. Characterization of the SPAN positive electrode. (a, b) SEM images of the SPAN. 
XRD pattern (c) and Raman spectrum (d) of the SPAN. 
As revealed by SEM imaging, the SPAN material exhibits a particle-like morphology with diameters ranging 
from 100 to 400 nm. The XRD pattern displays a broad diffraction peak centered at approximately 25°, 
indicative of a predominantly amorphous carbon structure with limited graphitic ordering, which originates 
from the pyrolysis of PAN. This structural feature is further corroborated by Raman spectroscopy, which 
shows pronounced D and G bands with an ID/IG ratio of ~1.15, reflecting a high degree of structural disorder 
and the presence of abundant defects within the carbon matrix. In addition, the appearance of characteristic 
C-S and S-S vibrational bands in the Raman spectra confirms the formation of chemical bonds between sulfur 
and the carbon backbone.
