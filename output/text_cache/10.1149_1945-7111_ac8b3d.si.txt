Preparation of S/C Composite Cathode 
The sulfur composite cathode was prepared by mixing 70 wt. % elemental sulfur (99.5%, Thermo 
Scientific), 20 wt. % Super P carbon black (99+% metal basis, Thermo Scientific) and 10 wt. % 
polyvinylidene fluoride (Thermo Scientific). The powder mixture is ball-milled at 300 rpm for 
approximately 90 minutes at room temperature. The resulting mixture was dispersed in N-methyl-
2-pyrrolidone (99.5% anhydrous, Sigma-Aldrich) to form a homogenous slurry. The slurry was 
coated on aluminum foil with areal loading of 1.96 mg·cm-2 and dried at 60 oC under vacuum 
overnight. Electrode samples 14 mm in diameter were punched for cell assembly. 
Preparation of electrolyte 
The electrolyte was prepared by dissolving lithium bis(trifluoromethanesulfonyl)imide (99.95% 
trace metal basis, Sigma-Aldrich) in conventional 1:1 volume ratio of 1,3-dioxolane (99.8%, 
Sigma-Aldrich) and 1,2-dimethoxyethane (99.9%, Sigma-Aldrich) with 2 wt.% lithium nitrate 
(99.99%, Sigma-Aldrich) additive. The electrolyte was prepared at concentrations of 1 and 2 M.  
Electrochemical measurements of the coin cells 
The coin cells CR2032 were assembled with the sulfur electrode, pure lithium foil as counter 
electrode and a glass fibre separator (Celgard 2325) in an argon-filled glove box (H2O < 1 ppm, 
O2 < 1 ppm). The discharge and charge measurements over a voltage range of 1.6-2.8 V versus

Electrochemical measurements of the operando lithium-sulfur cell (control experiment) 
The operando lithium sulfur cell was assembled in an argon-filled box (H2O < 1 ppm, O2 < 1 ppm). 
Sulfur electrode was placed on the cathode mount, secured with a screw-fastened thin circular 
clamp, and the arrangement is inserted into the quartz glass. Electrolyte (1 and 2 M ) is then added 
and the anode mount with lithium foil is placed on top of the electrolyte layer to seal the cell. 
Electrochemical cycling was performed on the assembled cell using a Gamry Reference 3000 
Potentiostat/Galvanostat/ZRA at a rate of C/30 (I = 80 µA) between a voltage range of 1 – 3.5 V 
(vs. Li/Li+) at room temperature. The measurement was constrained to the region of the first 
plateau of a typical lithium-sulfur cell using 1 M LiTFSI in DOL:DME (1:1, by vol.); the capacity 
is limited to ~200 mAh·g-1 where the LiPS-shuttle is of the most concern.  
We performed galvanostatic discharge for 20 minutes, paused the electrochemistry and observed 
the cell for 4 hours. Optical images of the cell were collected automatically at 10 min intervals 
using a camera.

UV-vis measurements 
The ex situ UV-vis measurement was carried out using an Agilent Cary 60 UV-vis 
Spectrophotometer which was switched on for about 20 min before the measurement. The 
measurement was taken at the end of the first discharge, and a scan rate of 60 nm min-1 was selected 
so that high-quality signals can be obtained. The range of 200 nm to 800nm was scanned in ~10 
minutes.
