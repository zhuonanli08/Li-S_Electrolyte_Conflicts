1. Experimental Details  
In the model, it has been assumed that supersaturated Li2S exists before precipitation. 
To verify the reasonability of this hypothesis, Li | S batteries of 8 μL mgS−1 E/S ratio 
were assembled and applied GITT test referred to Chen’s work.1 Batteries used for 
GITT test were assembled with the electrolyte of DOL/DME (v/v = 1:3) solvent with 
1.0 mol L−1 LiTFSI and 5.0 wt.% LiNO3, and the cathode with an areal sulfur loading 
of 4.3 ± 0.2 mgS cm−2. The GITT curves were obtained by a series of galvanostatic 
discharge of 7.5 min at 0.08 C (1 C = 1672 mA g−1) followed by 75.0 min of relaxation 
time on these Li | S cells. Combing coulometric titration and electrochemical relaxation 
measurements, this technique can be applied to determine OCP during discharge.
