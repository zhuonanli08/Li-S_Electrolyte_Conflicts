Figure S6. Lack of electrochemical contribution from precipitate. CVs perfomed with three 
different 250 mM Li2S4 catholytes. The Typical curve was obtained from a cell in which the 
catholyte used was decanted from the top of a vial to avoid the precipiate at the bottom. The PPT 
mixed curve was obtained from a cell constructed with catholyte from a vial that was agitated 
vigorously prior to cell fabrication, guaranteeing inclusion of the precipitate. The catholyte used 
in the cell for the PPT filtered curve was completely separated from the precipiate by filtration 
thorugh a 20 nm pore size filter.
