Measurements of lithium transference number (tLi+) using VLF-IS 
In this study, lithium transference number (tLi+) was estimated using very-low-frequency 
impedance spectroscopy (VLF-IS) in the same procedure reported by Wohde et al. S1 The highest, 
intermediate, and lowest frequency intercepts in the very-low-frequency impedance spectra shown in 
Figures S1a and b were assigned to the resistance for the migration of ions in the bulk of electrolyte 
(Rbulk), interfacial resistance at Li electrodes (Rinterface), and resistance for Li ion diffusion under anion-
blocking condition (Rdiffusion), respectively. Here, Rinterface is considered as the sum of charge transfer 
resistance (Rct) and solid electrolyte interphase (SEI) resistance (RSEI). These resistances were 
determined by fitting the impedance data with the equivalent circuit shown in Figure S1c and are listed 
in Table S1. The tLi+ value was estimated from the following equation (1).
