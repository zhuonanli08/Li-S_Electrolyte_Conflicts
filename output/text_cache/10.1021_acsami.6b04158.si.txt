Figure S3. The Raman spectra from DMSO solvent with different concentration of Li2S (the red and 
green lines are fitting peaks).  The Raman spectrum of pure DMSO shows two bands at 667 and 697 cm-1, 
which are assigned to the C-S-C symmetric and asymmetric stretch modes of DMSO respectively.1 With 
the increasing the dissolution of Li2S in (DMSO)10(NH4NO3) solutions, newly formed two bands (green 
peaks) at 676 and 708 cm-1 ascribes to the C-S-C symmetric and asymmetric stretch modes of DMSO 
molecules that solvate Li+ ions from Li2S.1
