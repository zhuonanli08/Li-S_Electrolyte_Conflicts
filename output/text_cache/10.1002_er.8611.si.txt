In the system-level performance model, a one-dimensional electrochemical model is used to calculate the overpotential and area specific impedance of each cell component as described below.

1-D Electrochemical Model

The electrochemical model developed in our previous works is reproduced here [1]–[3]. This model calculates the cell voltage by taking the anode, porous separator, cathode and both positive and negative current collectors into account. This concentration-independent model calculates the overpotential and ASI of the individual components at a specified degree of discharge.

Cathode:
Sulfur cathode is assumed as a porous structure consisting of sulfur, carbon, binder and electrolyte. In addition, it was assumed that there is only one reaction taking place in each discharge plateau.




For the calculation of the cathode overpotential, the porous electrode model developed by Newman and Tobias was used[4] where either Tafel or linear kinetics is applied depending on the current density.
