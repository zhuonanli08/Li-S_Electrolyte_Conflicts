Figure S7. As measured and absorption corrected Sulfur K-edge XANES of Li2S4 in DOL/DME solution. 
The absorption correction were performed using in-build module in Athena program using target 
chemical composition of the solution.  It is evident that the line width of pre-edge feature and white line 
shows minimal changes over the self-absorption correction. Hence the observed line width can be 
correlated to the presence of polysulfide cluster in the solution.
