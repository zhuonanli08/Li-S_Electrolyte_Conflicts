Fig. S10. Discharge profiles for the EIS tests at different state of discharge. (a) controlled cathode without PEO, (b) PEO@C/S cathode, (c) PEO-C/S cathode.
The three pouch cells were first subjected to three cycles at 0.1C to activate the batteries. Subsequently, the fully charged batteries were discharged at a rate of 0.5C, and their impedance spectra were recorded at voltages of 2.35, 2.16, 2.01 (2.06 for PEO@C/S), 1.85 (1.95 for PEO@C/S), and 1.70V using an electrochemical workstation. Prior to testing, the batteries were allowed to rest for 2 minutes to stabilize their voltage.

Fig. S13. Original and fitting curves for potentiostatic discharge at 2.15V for (a) W/O PEO, (b) PEO@C/S and (c) PEO-C/S cathodes.
After standing for 20 hours, the electrochemical workstation was used to perform a constant voltage discharge of 2.15V, until the current was reduced to 1%. The curve (original data in black) was fitted as the sum of two exponential functions, representing the reduction of Li2S8 and Li2S6. 

Fig. S14. Fitting of current vs time curve for potentiostatic discharge at 2.05V.
The constant voltage discharge test at 2.05V was carried out for the battery after the previous test, until the current was less than 1% of the initial value. By subtracting the contributions from LiPSs species as fitted in Fig. S8, the contribution resulting from the electrodeposition of Li2S can be obtained.
