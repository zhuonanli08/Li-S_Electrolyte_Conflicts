Supplementary experimental details
1.1 Battery materials:
The soft package batteries were assembled in argon-filled glove boxes with lithium foil (100 μm in thickness, Ganfengliye Corp., China) as anode, copper foil as the current collect of anode (Hefei Kejing Corp., China), Cellgard 2325 as the separator and the as prepared electrolytes. The package material was Al-plastic film (150 μm in thickness, Hefei Kejing Corp., China). The Ni-tab and Al-tab (Hefei Kejing Corp., China) was used to bring out the current from the soft package.
1.2 Soft package battery assembly:
Take the pouches with size of 2 cm × 2 cm for example: Firstly, the cathode with C/S composite on aluminium was cut into 2 cm × 4 cm and dried at 70 °C. Secondly, 2 cm × 0.5 cm C/S composite was removed off the cathode with knife to expose the aluminium foil. Thirdly, the exposed aluminium foil and the tab were welded under ultrasound with the ultrasonic spot welding machine (as shown in Figure S2, Shenzhen Kejing Corporation, China). This process was operated under room temperature and 80 W power. The welding frequency was usually 20 KHz. The copper foil (8 μm in thickness) was cut into 2 cm × 2 cm. The anode Ni-tab was welded onto the copper foil with the ultrasonic welding machine (Figure S2) under 120 W power. The seal of soft package battery is as follows: Firstly, two pieces of Al-plastic films were first cut into 3 cm × 3 cm. Secondly, three edges of the Al-plastic were sealed with hot pressure machine (Shenzhen Kejing Corporation, China) under 185 °C for 2 seconds. Thirdly, the lithium foil was covered with the celgard 2325 membrane (cut into 2.5 cm × 5 cm) and the cathode (welded with tab). Thirdly, the lithium, membrane and cathode were folded in the middle, with the lithium wrapped with others. Fourthly, the copper (welded with nickel tab) was put into the middle of the folded lithium. Fifthly, the copper, lithium, membrane and cathode were press together with hand and put into the soft package carefully. Sixthly, 2 ml electrolyte was added into the soft package for 6 hours and the excess electrolyte was poured out. Seventhly, the left edge of the soft package was sealed with vacuum hot pressure machine (Shenzhen Kejing Corporation, China) under 185 °C. The left edge of soft package should be pressured for several times under 185 °C, to ensure that the hot melt adhesives on the tabs were perfectly integrated with the Al-plastic film. The assembly of soft package batteries with size of 5 cm × 8 cm is similar as the 2 cm × 2 cm ones. 

Figure S1. a) The ultrasonic welding machine. b) The illustration of anode current collector with Ni-tab. c) The illustration of anode current collector with Al-tab.

As for the 4,000 mAh soft package battery assemble: first, the lithium foil with the length of 2 m was spread on to a glass plate; then, the Celgard 2325 membrane with the length of 3 m was used to swamp the lithium foil; third, dozens of the cathodes with the size of 5 cm × 8 cm was stacked between the Celgard 2325 separators and lithium folded in the Z type; the tabs of cathodes and anodes were melting together under ultrasonic spot welding machine; then, the electrodes stacks were pressed by hand and packed into aluminum plastics shells; last, the electrolyte was added into the aluminum plastics shells before sealed. As to be noted that, the soft package stacks were pressed tightly to ensure no more electrolyte was remained in the corners of the aluminium plastics shells. The appearance of the Li-S stacks is shown in Figure S2.

1.3 The detection of PS concentration 
The concise concentration detection process of PS is as follows (Figure S3): Firstly, 5.0 g polysulfide solution was dispersed in 100 g 25 wt. % KOH (Sinopharm Chemical Reagent Co., Ltd, AR) aqueous solution (yellow). Secondly, 15 ml H2O2 30% (Sinopharm Chemical Reagent Co., Ltd) was added into the above solution, stirring for about 1 hour until the solution was colourless. During this process, all the polysulfide was transferred into SO42-. Thirdly, add 1 M BaCl2 (Sinopharm Chemical Reagent Co., Ltd) aqueous solution to react with the SO42- until no more BaSO4 sediment formed. Then, the sediments were collected by centrifugation and filtration. At last, the BaSO4 was washed three times with pure water and dried in 800 °C for 5 hours. Through weighting the BaSO4 mass, we could calculate the amount of polysulfide and the concentration of polysulfide.

2.5 The DOL polymerization phenomenon in CE with and without PS. 
We prepared two bottles of electrolytes. One bottle is filled with 20 ml CE (pure DOL with 2 M LiTFSI) and the other is filled with 20 ml CE with 0.1 M polysulfide (mainly Li2S6). We added the perfluorosulfonic acid resin (H+ type Nafion 212 membrane, with size of 1cm× 1cm) catalyst into two bottles, to accelerate the DOL polymerization process. As a result, during the past 3 weeks, the CE without polysulfide turned into wax-like polymer while the CE with polysulfide is still liquid (shown in Figure S10).
