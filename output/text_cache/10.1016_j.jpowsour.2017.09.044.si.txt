Figure S1. The initial (A) discharge and (B) charge profiles of sulfur-carbon cathode obtained in the 1M LiTFSI DME/DOL electrolyte with 15 ppm, 50 ppm, 150 ppm, 250 ppm, 500 ppm and 1000 ppm H2O at a 0.15 C rate between 3.2 V and 1.8 V. The batteries exhibit the initial discharge capacity of 85075 mAh/g-sulfur (15 ppm H2O), 80062 mAh/g-sulfur (50 ppm H2O), 82881 mAh/g-sulfur (150 ppm H2O), 86770 mAh/g-sulfur (250 ppm H2O), 63548 mAh/g-sulfur (500 ppm H2O), 42555 mAh/g-sulfur (1000 ppm H2O). The capacity of Li-S cell decreases drastically when the water concentration is above 500 ppm.

Figure S4. Curve fitting for (A-B) O1s and (B) C1s XPS spectra obtained from Li metal. Li metal was immersed in electrolyte containing 1M LiTFSI DME/DOL with 15 ppm H2O (dry electrolyte) and 300 ppm H2O (wet electrolyte) for 5 h prior to XPS measurement. The Li2CO3/LiOH ratio obtained from dry electrolyte is 0.82 and the Li2CO3/LiOH ratio obtained from wet electrolyte is 0.16, suggesting that the composition of SEI film changes drastically between the dry and wet electrolytes.
