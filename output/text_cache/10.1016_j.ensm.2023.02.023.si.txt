Experimental Section
1.1 Chemicals
All reagents and starting materials were purchased from DoDoChem or Macklin, and they were used without further processing.

1.2 Electrolyte and polysulfide preparation
1.0 mol L-1 lithium bis(trifluoromethanesulfony)imide (LiTFSI) and 0.3 mol L-1 lithium nitrate (LiNO3) dissolved in 1,3-dioxetane (DOL)/1,2-diamethoxyethane (DME) (v/v=1:1) mixed solvents was adopted as the blank electrolyte. The fluorenone (FL) was added to blank electrolyte, and the electrolytes with the concentrations of 20, 50 and 100 mmol L-1 FL were prepared. The mixtures of lithium sulfides (Li2S) and sulfur (S) with a molar ratio of 1:1, 1:3, 1:5, and 1:7 were added into DOL/DME mixed solvents. Then the powder was heated under an argon (Ar) atmosphere at 60 °C for 120 h to prepare Li2S2, Li2S4, Li2S6, and Li2S8.

1.3 Preparation of cathode materials
The pyrrole was added to the ferric chloride (FeCl3) solution and stirred for 24 h to obtain poly-pyrrole. The poly-pyrrole and potassium hydroxide (KOH) were fully mixed and stirred in water according to the mass ratio of 1:4. After drying, the above mixture was heated at 800 °C for 2 h in an Ar atmosphere to obtain porous carbon (PC). After that, the PC and S8 (S) were mixed according to a mass ratio of 3:7, and then heated at 155 °C for 12 h under vacuum condition to obtain a sulfur-porous carbon composite material (S-PC). S-PC powder (80 wt%), carbon fiber (10 wt%) and poly(vinylidene fluoride) (10 wt%) as a binder were dispersed in N-methyl-2-pyrrolidone (NMP) to form a slurry, and then coated onto an aluminum (Al) foil and vacuum-dried at 60 °C for 24 h. Finally, the cathodes were formed.

1.4 Cell Assembly
Fabrication of Li | Carbon paper (CP) cells. Specifically, carbon paper was punched into circular disks with diameters of 12 mm as the cathode, and 10 µL of blank electrolyte was added into the anode side and 20 µL blank electrolyte containing 100 mmol L-1 of FL, Li2S8, or FL+Li2S8 as catholyte. 
Fabrication of Li-S. For Li-S cells, the Al foil was cut into circles with a diameter of 12 mm. The E/S ratios were about 15, 10, 8, 6 and 5 µL mg-1 for the electrodes with S loadings of 1.15, 2.6, 3.8, 5.5 and 4.6 mgs cm-2, respectively.

1.5 Electrochemical Measurements
Cyclic voltammetry (CV) test: CV were conducted by using a CHI 760e electrochemical workstation with a potential range of 1.7-2.8 V (vs Li+/Li). Blank electrolyte containing 100 mmol L-1 of FL/Li2S8 as catholyte to assemble Li|CP cells to study the redox behavior of FL and lithium polysulfides. Li-S cells with and without FL to investigate the enhancement of sulfur redox kinetics by FL.
The galvanostatic discharge/charge measurements: Li-S cells with and without FL were cycled between 1.7 and 2.8 V (vs Li+/Li), which were conducted using a Neware battery test system. The cathode specific capacity is normalized by the mass of sulfur.
Nucleation of Li2S: Li2S8 solution with and without FL were used as the catholyte. The assembled Li|CP cells were first discharged galvanostatically at 0.112 mA to 2.10 V and then discharged potentiostatically at 2.09 V for Li2S nucleation and growth.

1.6 Spectroscopic characterization
The as-prepared Li2Sn solution were diluted with DOL/DME (v/v=1:1) solution at a volume ratio of 1:300 for UV-vis spectra test, which was performed by PE Lambda 950 (PerkinElmer) spectrometer with the range of 200-800 cm-1. The Raman spectra were recorded on a Renishaw inVia at 532 nm Laser. In-situ Raman spectra with the electrochemical cells was used to record the continuous signal change on electrode. The cells used stainless steel as cathode current collector with sulfur loading of 1.0 mg cm-2, and each Raman spectrum was acquired at 100% frequency and accumulated once for 10 seconds. In-situ XRD patterns were collected on a D8 ADVANCE (Bruker) for every 30 minutes during cells discharge. Scanning electron microscopy (SEM) was performed on a JEOL JSM-7610F Plus instrument. XPS measurements were performed by AXIS SUPRA+ (Kratos). The 13C NMR spectra were conducted with a Bruker Avance III HD 500 MHz. ESR were recorded on a Bruker A300 with Ar atmosphere.
