Fig. S1 XPS spectra (N1S, O1S) measured from lithiated graphite electrode in 5 M LiTFSI-DME/DOL. Black and red lines denote experimental spectra and fitting curves, respectively. The peak at about 399 eV in N 1s spectrum derives from S-N-S part. In O 1s spectrum, various O-containing groups give a peak at almost the same binding energy in 530~534 eV and hence, no useful information was obtained.
Fig. S2 Cyclic performances of mesoporous carbon/S electrode with 5 M LiTFSI-DME/DOL at a rate of 0.2 C (1 C = 1675 mA g-1 based on sulfur cathode).
Fig. S3 Voltage profile of the mesoporous carbon/S electrode at a rate of 0.2 C (1 C = 1675 mA g-1 based on sulfur cathode).The cycling performance for the Li-S (mesoporous carbon/sulfur electrode) half battery is shown in Fig. S2. It can be observed that the Li-S half battery can deliver a high initial discharge capacity of 1286 mAh g-1, suggesting a high utilization ratio of the active material. After 100 cycles, a reversible capacity of as high as 796 mAh g-1 can still be remained. Meanwhile, the coulombic efficiency always approach nearing 100%. As seen in Fig. S3, two typical discharge and one charge potential plateaus can be apparently observed from above figures, which corresponds to the two step reaction of sulfur during the discharge process. The smaller potential separation between the charge and discharge plateaus indicates better kinetics characteristics of the Li-S half cells.( Corresponding author.
