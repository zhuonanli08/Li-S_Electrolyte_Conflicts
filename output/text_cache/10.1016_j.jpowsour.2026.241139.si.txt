Fig. S1. Calculated adsorption structures and corresponding adsorption energies (Eads, eV) of DOL and DME on (a) Li2S4, (b) Li2S6, and (c) Li2S8 with DOL, DME, and mixed DOL/DME adsorption configurations. 
Figure S1 shows structures with one electrolyte molecule (DOL or DME) adsorbed onto each of the two Li atoms in the polysulfides, resulting in two adsorbed solvent molecules per system. Three explicit solvent structures were calculated: (i) DOL molecules or (ii) DME molecules adsorbed on both Li sites, and (iii) a mixed case of one DOL molecule adsorbed on one Li site and one DME molecule on the other Li site. These configurations were used to investigate the solvent-dependent adsorption behavior of polysulfides on the Li metal anode.
