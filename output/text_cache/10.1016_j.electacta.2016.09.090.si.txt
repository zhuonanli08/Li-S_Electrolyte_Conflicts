 (G. Liu)Supplement information The polysulfide dissolution within electrolyte shown in Fig 2 at the presence of ionic liquid can be further proved 
color for polysulfide. In the 100% IL, the solution is not clear, dark sediment can be observed inside the solution. In comparison, the solution is clearer when less ionic liquid is present (0%IL clearer than 50%IL). And the polysulfide precipitation particles can be observed via optical microscope. Much less precipitated particles are observed in 50% IL than 100%IL, and none particle is observed in 0% IL. This indicates that the same amount of Li2S6 can be well dissolved in 0% IL, while obvious saturation and precipitation can be observed in 50% IL and 100% IL. This is the clear evidence of difference in solubility of polysulfide by different electrolyte. With the increase ratio of ionic liquid in the solution, the solubility of polysulfide decrease.
