Experimental Methods 
 
Synthesis of SPAN: PAN (Sigma-Aldrich, Mw=150,000) is mixed uniformly with S (Sigma-
Aldrich) by ball milling at a weight ratio of 1:3 for 2 h with ethyl alcohol. Then the dried mixture 
is annealed at 350 ℃ for 6 h with a heating rate of 5 ℃ in Ar atmosphere. 
 
Electrolyte and electrode preparation: Battery−grade LiFSI, LiTFSI, LiPF6, EC, DEC, DME 
and DOL are used as received from DodoChem. The electrolytes of 1 M LiFSI/DME, 1 M 
LiFSI/DME-EC, 1 M LiTFSI/DME, 1 M LiTFSI/DME-EC, 1 M LiTFSI/DOL-DME, 1 M 
LiTFSI/DOL-DME-EC, 1 M LiFSI/EC-DEC, 1 M LiPF6/EC-DEC and 2 M LiFSI/EC are 
prepared by dissolving the Li salts into the selected solvents. The SPAN cathodes with medium 
areal capacity of about 1.8 mAh cm−2 are prepared by slurry coating on Al foils, with SPAN, 
CNT and polyvinylidene fluoride (PVDF) in a mass ratio of 80:10:10 mixed in N-methyl 
pyrrolidinone (NMP). The SPAN cathodes with high areal capacity are comprised of SPAN, 
CNT, poly(acrylic acid) (PAA, Sigma-Aldrich, Mw=450,000), guar gum (GG, Aladdin) in a 
mass ratio of 90:5:2.5:2.5 mixed in deionized water. After drying in a vacuum oven at 60 ℃ 
overnight and calendaring three times, the SPAN cathodes are cut into 12 mm discs for coin 
cells and 43 mm×56 mm rectangles for pouch cells. 
 
Material Characterizations: The electrodes retrieved from disassembled cells for post-
analyses are rinsed by anhydrous solvents (DME or dimethyl carbonate (DMC), depending on 
the electrolyte used). Elemental analysis is done on an Elemantar Vario EL Cube. SEM and 
EDS measurements are conducted on a field emission SEM (Hitachi SU8000 FE-SEM) at 5 kV 
and 15 kV, respectively. UV−vis absorption spectra are collected on a Shimadzu UV-1800. 
HRTEM and EDS measurements are taken with a JEM-2100F. XRD patterns are recorded on a 
PANalytical X'Pert PRO. The 17O-NMR spectra are recorded on Agilent DD2-600 (600 MHz) 
at room temperature. XPS is performed on a Thermo Scientific Escalab 250Xi. FTIR spectra are 
carried out on a Thermo Scientific Nicolet iS50, which is equipped with a Universal Diamond 
Crystal ATR accessory. The ionic conductivity is measured by METTLER TOLEDO FE38-
Five Easy. Differential scanning calorimetry (DSC) was tested using a Q200 Modulated

Differential Scanning Calorimeter (TA Instruments). For Cryo-TEM characterization, Li metal 
with an areal capacity of 0.5 mAh cm−2 is deposited onto a Cu TEM grid by applying a current 
density of 1 mA cm−2. All Cryo-TEM images are obtained on a FEI Talos F200C under low 
dose mode at an accelerating voltage of 200 kV. 
 
Electrochemical measurements: 2032-type coin cells (MTI Corporation KJ Group) and 
single-layer pouch cells are assembled to test electrochemical performances. Celgard 3501 is 
used as separators in 2 M LiFSI/EC, and Celgard 2400 is used in other electrolytes. 40 μL 
electrolyte is added in each coin cell unless otherwise specified. The Li/Cu half cells are cycled 
at 0 to 2 V for five cycles for SEI formation (no Li deposition), and then a fixed amount of Li 
is plated onto the current collector at a constant current density and stripped back by a cutoff 
voltage of 1 V. Li−SPAN half cells are assembled by 12 mm Li discs with a thickness of 500 
μm and SPAN discs with a S loading of 1.2−1.5 mg cm−2. Li−SPAN full cells are assembled 
by 12 mm Li discs with a thickness of 45 μm and SPAN discs with an areal capacity of about 
1.8 mAh cm−2 or 3.6 mAh cm−2. Single-layer pouch cells are assembled by 45 mm×58 mm Li 
rectangles with a thickness of 50 μm and SPAN rectangles with an areal capacity of 4.08 mAh 
cm−2, where the E/S ratio is 10 μL mgS−1. All the Li−SPAN batteries are tested between 1 and 
3 V at 25 ℃. Li−SPAN coin cells are cycled at 0.1 C for the first cycle to activate SPAN 
cathodes. Single-layer pouch cells are cycled at 7.5 mA cm−2 for the first cycle and 25 mA cm−2 
for the subsequent cycles. An electrochemical workstation (AMETEK) is used to conduct CV 
and EIS. The CV is tested at a scan rate of 0.5 mV s−1 from 1 to 3 V. The Tafel plots are obtained 
from CV in symmetric Li/Li cells after 30 cycles with a scan rate of 1 mV s−1 from −200 to 200 
mV, where the selected voltage range from 45 to 90 mV in the linear region is used to calculate 
the I0. The EIS is characterized in a frequency range between 100 kHz and 0.01 Hz with a 
voltage amplitude of 10 mV at 25 ℃. All Li−SPAN full cells were tested at the end of the 
charged state, and the electrodes collected from the above Li−SPAN full cells are reassembled 
into Li/Li and SPAN/SPAN symmetrical cells.
