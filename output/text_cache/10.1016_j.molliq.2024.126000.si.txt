Table S2.  Force field parameters for dimethoxyethane (DME). Bonded parameters from Ref1.

Lennard-Jones parameters were taken from OPLS-AA.  1-4 scale factor is 0.5.

The partial charges carried by all the atoms were taken from density functional theory (DFT) calculation. The exchange correlation energy was described by the PW91 functional within the generalized gradient approximation (GGA-PW91). OBS method for DFT-D correction. All electron and the double-numeric basis with polarization functions (DNP) were used for all atoms. The convergence tolerance chooses the maximum force of 0.002 Hartree/Å, the maximum ionic displacement of 0.005 Å and an energy of 1.0E-5 Hartree. Electrostatic potential fitting was employed to generate the resulting partial charges for molecules shown in Table S1.

Table S3.  Force field parameters for dioxolane (DOL). Bonded parameters from Ref2.

Lennard-Jones parameters were taken from OPLS-AA.  1-4 scale factor is 0.5.

All possible torsional energy functions for the dioxolane ring, only that for Oo-Co2-Co2-Oo was retained. Urey-Bradley terms (bond bending) were introduced to remedy the missing torsion terms and to improve the frequency fitting. The bond bending here used the charmm style [Ebend= Kθ(θ-θ0)2 + KUB(S-S0)2].
