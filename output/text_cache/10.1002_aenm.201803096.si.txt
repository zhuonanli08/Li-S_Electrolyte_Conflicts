Figure S2.  Lithium metal after 1 cycle in Li-S cells with electrolytes consisting of DOL-
DME (left), DOL-DMA (center left), DOL-DMSO (center right), and DOL-MeIm (right). The 
degradation seems most severe in DOL-MeIm, followed by DOL-DMA. Lithium anodes 
cycled in electrolyte with both DMSO and DME appear relatively untarnished after 1 cycle. 
 
 
 
 
Figure S3.  Lithium metal exposed for 24 h to DOL, DME, DMA, DMSO, and MeIm 
solvents. There is significant decomposition in MeIm and DMA, while there is relatively little 
visual changes in DOL, DME, and DMSO.

Figure S6. Enlarged 1H NMR spectra of DMA exposed to lithium metal for 7 days (in D6-
DMSO). There is a new peak at 2.8 ppm between peaks corresponding to protons at site “b” 
and “c”. The cleavage of the central C-N bond may result in the formation of dimethylamine 
and 2,4 Pentanedione, along with its tautomers, whose new proton sites may account for the 
multitude of new peaks.[6] The symbol “▲” accompanies peaks corresponding to known
