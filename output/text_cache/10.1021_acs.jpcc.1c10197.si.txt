Figure S8. The first pattern of the operando XRD pattern (Iobs) of the cell ‘S-L=2.5 E/S=6’, which shows 
the initial state before cycling. Bragg reflections from α-S (ICSD collection code: 27261) are marked 
with blue peak markers. The peak at 5.48˚ is from the 002 reflection of polyimide while the broad peaks 
around 14˚, 17˚ and 18.5˚ are from the separator. 
 
Figure S9. The 396th pattern of the operando XRD pattern (Iobs) of the cell ‘S-L=2.5 E/S=6’, which shows 
the fully charged state before the 5th cycle starts. Bragg reflections from β-S (ICSD collection code: 870) 
are marked with blue peak markers. The peak at 5.48˚ is from the 002 reflection of polyimide while the 
broad peaks around 14˚, 17˚ and 18.5˚ are from the separator. A very broad peak centered around 22˚ 
is from the carbon additives. 
 
Figure S10. The 421st pattern of the operando XRD pattern (Iobs) of the cell ‘S-L=2.5 E/S=6’, which 
shows the fully discharged state in the 5th cycle. Bragg reflections from Li2S (ICSD collection code: 
196932) are marked with blue peak markers. The peak at 5.48˚ is from the 002 reflection of polyimide 
while the broad peaks around 14˚, 17˚ and 18.5˚ from the separator. A very broad peak centered around 
22˚ is from the carbon additives.
