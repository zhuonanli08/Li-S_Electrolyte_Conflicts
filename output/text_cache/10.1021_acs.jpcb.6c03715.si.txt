Geometry Benchmark
Geometry benchmarking was performed by comparing optimized DFTB2/PTBP struc-
tures with the corresponding DFT geometries for PC, DMC, EC, and DIOX and for
their one-solvent Li+ complexes. The DFT geometry optimizations were performed with
ORCA using the ωB97X-D3BJ exchange-correlation functional and the def2-TZVP basis
set. The DFTB calculations used the original PTBP parameter set, second-order SCC-
DFTB, and D3(BJ) dispersion, consistent with the production simulations. Bonded pairs
were identified from the DFT structures using a covalent-radius cutoff and then evaluated
with the same atom indices in the DFTB2/PTBP structures. Bond angles were generated
from the resulting DFT connectivity. Positive differences indicate longer bond distances
or larger angles in DFTB2/PTBP relative to DFT.

Mean-Squared Displacement
2.1
Quantitative identification of the finite-time diffusive regime
To test the long-time regime rather than relying only on visual linearity, the logarithmic
diffusion exponent was evaluated over 20–50 ps as
α = d ln⟨|r(t) −r(0)|2⟩
d ln t
.
(1)
Normal diffusion gives α = 1, whereas ballistic and subdiffusive motion give α > 1 and
α < 1, respectively. Second, linear slopes were compared over four neighboring intervals
(10–50, 15–50, 20–50, and 25–50 ps). A curve was classified as exhibiting a stable finite-
time diffusive regime when 0.9 ≤α ≤1.1 over 20–50 ps and the range of fitted diffusion
coeﬀicients across these windows was no greater than 10% of the reported mean.
All 52 species–composition curves satisfy both criteria (Table S7). Their 20–50 ps
exponents span 0.936–1.062, their linear-fit coeﬀicients of determination span 0.9974–
0.9999, and their fit-window sensitivity is 0.3–5.8%. Most importantly, the slow com-
ponents satisfy the criteria separately: for Li+, α = 0.981–1.062, R2 = 0.9988–0.9999,
and window sensitivity is 0.8–4.0%; for OTf–, the corresponding ranges are 0.981–1.042,
0.9991–0.9999, and 0.3–2.6%. Thus, the 20–50 ps interval lies beyond the initial non-
diffusive crossover even for the slowly moving ionic species. These diagnostics establish
a stable diffusive regime within the available records.

Ensemble Li–O Coordination
Li–O coordination numbers were averaged over every Li ion and every frame sampled at
0.1 ps across the complete ensemble of independent production trajectories, using a 2.6 Å
cutoff. Error bars in Figure S13 are standard deviations over the pooled Li–frame ob-
servations and describe the breadth of the instantaneous coordination distribution across
the trajectory ensemble. In all solvents, increasing salt loading replaces solvent oxygen by
triflate oxygen in the Li first shell. This ensemble result is consistent with the ensemble-
averaged ESP trend, while the displayed molecular geometries provide representative
structural motifs.

periodic boundaries and molecular center-of-mass positions were sampled every 0.1 ps.

The non-contact baselines in Figure S14 are weakly negative and close to zero (ap-
proximately −0.004 to −0.027). Small anticorrelations are plausible in a finite periodic
NV E system because of total-momentum constraints, collective backflow, and the inclu-

therefore, both the raw contact correlation and baseline are shown. Contact correlations
are substantially more positive than the baseline for all systems, while contact survival
remains finite. This behavior is consistent with transient local cooperative motion.
