Figure S4 The SEM image of S/CNT electrode after the 1st cycle in the baseline electrolyte. It 
can be seen that the S/CNT electrode still shows a homogeneous morphology with negligible 
aggregation after the 1st cycle in the baseline electrolyte. This indicates that the passivation by 
large particle aggregate is accumulated cycle by cycle during cycling and causes cell failure 
after certain cycles. This is consistent with the fast capacity decay after ~40 cycles in the 
baseline electrolyte.

Figure S8 (a) 7Li and (b) 19F MAS NMR spectra obtained from sulfur electrodes with (top) 
and without (bottom) NH4TFSI additive in the electrolytes. Both 7Li and 19F MAS NMR 
spectra show that the line width of the isotropic peak of the sample without NH4TFSI additive 
is broader than that of the sample with NH4TFSI additive. And more number of spinning 
sidebands appeared in the sample without NH4TFSI additive due to the larger chemical shift 
anisotropy. Asterisks (*) denote spinning sidebands.

The dash lines and arrows marked on the Li metal surface and cross-section in Fig. S9b-S9c 
clearly indicate heterogeneous corrosions in the baseline electrolyte, which could be the 
reflection of the large aggregation of big particles in the sulfur cathode. On the contrary, with 
the NH4TFSI additive, the sulfur cathode and Li metal anode show much-improved 
homogeneity as shown in the SEM images in Fig. S9d-S9f due to the mitigations of 
passivation on the cathode and homogeneous reactions.

decomposition of electrolyte.[2] The O 1s peak located at 532.2 eV is assigned to
