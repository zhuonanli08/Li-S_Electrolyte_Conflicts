To investigate why the number of DOL molecules is consistently larger than the number of
DME molecules around the polymer, we conducted a hydrogen bond analysis. Specifically,
we calculated the C-H. . . O interactions, where C-H from the polymer acted as a donor and O
from the solvent molecule acted as an acceptor. We performed this analysis for different molar
fractions (x = 0.25, 0.5, and 0.75) and numbers of monomers (n = 5 and 40). Subsequently,
a sensitivity analysis was performed by varying both the C-O distance between 0.25 nm and
0.32 nm and the CH-O angle between 90° and 180°, as suggested by Steiner.9 Within the
specified distance and angle, the total number of possible hydrogen bonds was calculated
and reported in Fig. S10.
