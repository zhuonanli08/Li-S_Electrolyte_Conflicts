Electrolyte properties
Measurements of ionic conductivity and viscosity at 30 °C of the two considered electrolytes DME/DOL-1/1 and HME/DME-8/2 are shown
in Fig. S3. Furthermore the polysulfide solubility was also determined at 30 °C. The polysulfide solubility of DME/DOL-1/1 is 9.5 M and of
HME/DME-8/2 it is 0.57 M.
