Figure S14. UV–vis absorption spectra of LiPS-containing DD electrolyte through immersing the 
cycled S cathode from the pouch cell with different electrolyte.
The cycled S cathodes from the pouch cells with NDD and NBME electrolytes were 
immersed in fresh DD electrolyte for 3 day. UV–vis absorption spectra of the LiPS-containing 
DD electrolyte with 50 times dilution display that a weaker LiPS signal was found in the cycled 
cathode with NBME than NDD electrolyte, demonstrating the less LiPS dissolution was formed 
in the cells with NBME during discharge/charge process.
