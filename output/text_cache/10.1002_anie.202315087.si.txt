EXPERIMENTAL PROCEDURES 
1.Materials and methods 
1.1 Materials 
Sodium bicarbonate (NaHCO3) and ammonium persulphate ((NH4)2S2O8) were purchased from Macklin 
(Shanghai, China); sublimed sulfur (S), carbon disulfide (CS2), 1,3-benzenediamine (MPD), 2-tert-
butylanthraquinon (TBAQ) and lithium nitrate (LiNO3) were purchased from Sigma Aldrich (Shanghai, 
China). Lithium sulfide (Li2S) was purchased from Aladdin (Shanghai, China). 1,3-diamethoxyethane 
(DOL), 1,2-dimethyl ether (DME), and bis(trifluoromethanesulfonimide) lithium salt (LiTFSI) were 
purchased from Suzhou Dodochem Co., Ltd, China (the water contents were below 15 ppm for solvents 
and 50 ppm for Li salt). Lithium-metal foil (thickness, 450 μm, 100 μm) was obtained from Shenzhen 
tianchenghe Technology Co., Ltd. All these materials were used without further purification. 
1.2 Preparation of S loading cathode. 
Detailed preparation process for S loading carbon material was shown previously48, and the self-
supporting cathode (SC) was obtained with a certain mechanical strength before polymer calcination. 
The SC cathode with a diameter of 0.9 cm and a thickness of 0.08 cm and a mass of 3-5 mg was 
constructed. The construction of SC/sulfur composites (SC/S) was carried out by the typical CS2 drip 
irrigation method, and the low S loading amount for general electrodes was approximately 2 mg cm-2, 
and the high S loading cathode (≈4-10 mg cm-2) were also prepared. S loading ratio (S/(C+S)) is 
controlled to be 60%-70%. 
2. Electrochemical measurement.  
2.1 Optical experiments. 
In the three-electrode system, with chronoamperometry method, the work electrodes were applied with 
2.3 or 2.2 V vs. Li/Li+ to obtain the reduced state of Li+TBAQ•− or Li+2TBAQ2-, respectively, in aprotic 
medium (DOL/DME, 1:1 vol, with 1M LiTFSI and 0.08 M TBAQ (the highest solubility of the TBAQ is 
about 1.3 M)) under an Ar atmosphere. The ionic conductivity of electrolyte with and without Li+TBAQ•− 
were measurement, which shows the similar values (9.04 vs 8.40 mS cm-1). Semiquinone (Li+TBAQ•−) 
was added into S or Li2S suspension to check their chemical reactions (excessive S or Li2S solid was 
added into the electrolyte containing Li+TBAQ•−). 
2.2 Cell assembly and electrochemical measurements. 
The electrolytes were obtained by dissolving 1.0 M L-1 LiTFSI and 2.0 wt% LiNO3 in DOL/DME (1:1 vol,) 
mixed solvents with and without 0.08 M TBAQ, respectively. The ionic conductivity of electrolytes with 
and without Li+TBAQ•− were measured by a conductivity meter (SevenCompact S230), which are 
determined to be 9.04 and 8.40 mS cm-1, respectively. The amount of electrolyte used in the button 
batteries assembled herein is 60 µL (unless otherwise specified).  
2.2.1 Electrochemical cyclic voltammetry (CV) 
The CV curves tests were carried out an electrochemical workstation (CHI 760E) between 1.7-2.8 V vs.

Li/Li+ at different scan rates at 25℃. Lithium foil were employed as both reference and counter electrodes, 
and composite electrode were used as the working electrode. Two batteries system for Li/SC cells were 
used to perform the test. 1.0 M LiTFSI dissolved in DOL/DME (1:1 vol, 2.0 wt% LiNO3) electrolytes with 
and without 60 µL 0.08 M TBAQ were used as electrolyte, respectively. All measurements were carried 
out at room temperature. 
2.2.2 Electrochemical impedance spectroscopy (EIS) 
The electrochemical impedance spectroscopy (EIS) tests were operated with an Autolab electrochemical 
workstation (Metrohm) in a frequency range of 0.01-105 Hz with an AC voltage amplitude of 0 mV. Two 
batteries system for Li/SC cells were used to perform the test. 1.0 M LiTFSI dissolved in DOL/DME (1:1 
vol, 2.0 wt% LiNO3) electrolytes with and without 60 µL 0.08 M TBAQ were used as electrolyte, 
respectively. All measurements were carried out at room temperature. 
2.2.3 Li-S batteries (CR2032) assembly 
Li-S batteries (CR2032) were assembled using different loading SC/S electrodes as the cathodes, lithium 
foil as counter electrodes, PP membrane (Celgard 2400) as separator in an Ar-filled glove box (<0.1 ppm 
of O2, <0.1 ppm of H2O), respectively. 1.0 M LiTFSI dissolved DOL/DME (1:1 vol, 2.0 wt% LiNO3) 
electrolytes with and without 60 µL 80 mmol TBAQ were denoted as W TBAQ•− and W/O TBAQ•−, 
respectively. The E/S ratios were about 30, 15-6 µL mg-1 for the electrodes with sulfur loadings of 2.0, 
4.0-10.0 mg cm-2, respectively. 
The galvanostatic discharge/charge measurements were conducted using a CT3001A (LAND Electronic 
Co., Ltd) battery test system. The cathode specific capacities were normalized only by the mass of sulfur, 
as per common practice. The charge-discharge voltage range was 1.8-2.8 V vs. Li/Li+ at room 
temperature (1 C=1675 mA g-1).  
2.2.4 Li/C batteries (CR2032) assembly 
To evaluate the electrochemical performance of Li+TBAQ•−, the cells were first assembled using C 
electrode as cathodes, lithium foil as the reference/counter electrodes, PP membrane (Celgard 2400) as 
separator, and 0.08 M TBAQ (60 μL) as active substance, in an Ar-filled glove box (<0.1 ppm of O2, <0.1 
ppm of H2O), respectively. 
2.2.5. In-situ Raman spectroscopy.  
A Li-S cells with a perforated stainless steel positive case and a quartz window was used for in situ 
Raman spectroscopy monitor under a 633 nm laser. Each spectrum was acquired for 20 s with the 
frequency of 10%. The cells were tested at a current rate of 0.5 C (sulfur loading: 1.0 mg cm-2, 200 μL 
electrolyte). 
3. Physical characterizations. 
SEM analysis was performed with a field emission scanning electron microanalyzer (Nova Nano SEM 
450). Elemental mapping was conducted using EDX detector attached on JEOL 200F. TEM was 
performed on a JEM2100F transmission electron microscope. Raman spectra were recorded on a

MicroRaman system (LabRAM HR spectrometer, Horiba) with an Olympus BX microscope and an argon 
ion laser (532 nm). Sulfur content in the composites was investigated by thermogravimetric analysis 
(TGA, Mettler Toledo TGA/DSC3) at a temperature range of 25-600°C with a ramp rate of 10°C min-1

4. Theoretical computation. 
The geometric and electronic structure of the periodic structure Li2S based on the first-principles method 
with the framework of density functional theory (DFT) was performed with VASP. The geometry 
optimization was carried out based on projected augmented wave (PAW) pseudopotentials, the SCAN 
functional49 was used for describing the exchange-correlation interactions. The kinetic-energy cutoff was 
chosen as 500 eV, the energy convergence criteria was set to be 10-5 eV/cell and force reached the 
tolerance of 0.01 eV/Å. The Brillouin zone was sampled using 11 × 11 × 11 Г-centered Monkhorst-Pack 
meshes. Considering that crystalline α-sulfur is only weakly bonded through van der Waals interactions, 
the isolated S8 molecule was used to study the electronic properties of sulfur species at the beginning of 
discharge. Our calculation suggests that cyclo-S8 exhibits a crown shape with a D4h symmetry. The 
molecules of Li+TBAQ•− and S8 were fully optimized using the ORCA software50. The hybrid nonlocal 
exchange-correlation functional of Becke and Lee, Yang and Parr (B3LYP)39 and the def2-TZVPPD basis 
set had been further employed to calculate reliably the highest occupied molecular orbital-lowest 
unoccupied molecular orbital (HOMO-LUMO).

Note S2.The detail calcuation methode of Sact, Suti and R(Pc1+Pt1)/Pc2 in the presence/absence of Li+TBAQ•−

(1) Sact is determined to be the amount ratio of actually and theoretically transferred electrons for S→Li2S4 
reaction (Sact=Eactual(S8→Li2S4)/Etheoretical(S8→Li2S4)). Etheoretical(S8→Li2S4) is calculated from the amount of S 
loading on cathode. For example, if the quantity of loading S8 (32*8=256 g mol-1) is 8 g, it equals 1/32 
mol (1/32=8/256). Moreover, the transferred electrons from S8 to Li2S4 are 4 (S8+4e-+4Li+→2Li2S4).

reaction (Suti=Eactual(S8→Li2S)/Etheorectical(S8→Li2S)). Etheoretical(S8→Li2S) is calculated from the amount of S loading
