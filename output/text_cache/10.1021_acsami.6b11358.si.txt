Figure S1. (a) 7Li and (b) 19F NMR of wet MeCN2.5−LiTFSI (>50 ppm water) prepared with as−purchased LiTFSI before and after 
exposure to Li metal. A significant shift in the resonance of both 7Li and 19F is observed after exposure to Li metal due to the 
decomposition of the electrolyte. A visible color change is observed as shown by the photograph inset in (a). The dry MeCN2.5−LiTFSI 
(<15 ppm water) prepared with dried LiTFSI does not exhibit a color change upon exposure to Li metal.

TFSI mode, a, to a new mode, mode b, due to coordinated TFSI. (b) The higher wavenumber region shows MeCN modes c, d, e, and f.

Figure S4. Raman spectra of the (a) (MeCN)2.5‒LiTFSI and (b) (MeCN)2‒LiTFSI neat and diluted with TTE at volume ratios of 2:1, 
1:1, and 1:2 (electrolyte:TTE). The spectra is fit with four component Gaussian functions to determine the relative peak area ratios 
associated with free MeCN and coordinated MeCN.  
 
 
 
Figure S5. Relative 7Li chemical shifts calculated by DFT for LiTFSI in MeCN in the dilute regime, the (MeCN)2‒LiTFSI solvate, and 
(MeCN)2‒LiTFSI diluted with TTE at a volume ratio of 1:1. As the LiTFSI concentration increases from the dilute regime to the 
(MeCN)2‒LiTFSI solvate, the 7Li chemical shift becomes more positive (deshields). Addition of TTE to the (MeCN)2‒LiTFSI solvate 
also causes the 7Li chemical shift to move to a more deshielded region. The shift to more positive ppm as the LiTFSI concentration 
increases and TTE is added is consistent with the experimental results.
