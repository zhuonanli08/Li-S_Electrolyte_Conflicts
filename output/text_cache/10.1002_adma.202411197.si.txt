1. Experimental Section 
1.1. Raw materials 
Lithium (Li) metal foils (50 μm, China Energy Lithium Co., Ltd.), 1,2-dimethoxyethane 
(DME, battery grade, DoDoChem), 1,3-dioxolane (DOL, battery grade, DoDoChem), lithium 
bis-(trifluoromethanesulfonyl) imide (LiTFSI, battery grade, DoDoChem), LiNO3 (99.99%, 
Aladdin), benzotrifluoride (BTF, Aladdin, 99% purity), deuterated dimethyl sulfoxide (DMSO-
d6, Aladdin, 99.9% purity, with 0.03% tetramethylsilane), Li2S (Alfa), elemental sulfur (S8, 
Alfa), copper (Cu) foils, nickel (Ni) foils, graphene, carbon nanotubes (CNT), 
poly(vinylidenefluoride) (PVDF), N-methylpyrrolidone (NMP), and polypropylene (PP) 
membranes (Celgard 2400) were used as purchased without further purification. 
 
1.2. Preparation of electrolyte 
The LiTFSI electrolyte was composed of 1.0 M LiTFSI in DOL/DME (1:1, by vol.). The 
Li2S8 electrolyte was prepared by mixing Li2S and S8 with a molar ratio of 8:7 and a total sulfur 
concentration of 1.0 M in DOL/DME. The Li2S8-LiTFSI electrolyte was prepared by mixing 
Li2S and S8 with a molar ratio of 8:7 and a total sulfur concentration of 1.0 M in LiTFSI 
electrolyte. The LiTFSI-LiNO3 electrolyte was composed of 1.0 M LiTFSI with 2 wt.% LiNO3 
in DOL/DME. The Li2S8-LiTFSI-LiNO3 electrolyte was prepared by mixing Li2S and S8 with 
a molar ratio of 8:7 and a total sulfur concentration of 1.0 M in LiTFSI-LiNO3 electrolyte. The 
Li2S8-LiTFSI-5LiNO3 electrolyte was prepared by mixing Li2S and S8 with a molar ratio of 8:7 
and a total sulfur concentration of 1.0 M in LiTFSI electrolyte with 5 wt.% LiNO3. The above 
electrolytes were prepared in an Ar-filled glove box with H2O and O2 contents below 0.01 ppm. 
 
1.3. Preparation of graphene cathodes and sulfur cathodes 
For preparation of the graphene cathode, 90 wt.% graphene and 10 wt.% PVDF were 
ultrasonically dispersed in NMP to form a dilute slurry. The as-prepared graphene slurry was 

<<PAGE 3>>
 
 
SI2 
subsequently coated on Al foils and dried at 60oC for 30 min. The areal loading of graphene 
was controlled as 0.20 mg cm−2. 
For preparation of the sulfur cathode, sulfur, and CNT were first mixed in a batch with a 
mass ratio of 7:3. The mixture was then ball-milled with PVDF binder at a weight ratio of 9:1 
in NMP to form a slurry. The obtained slurry was then coated on Al foils and fully dried at 60oC 
for 24 h. The sulfur content was 63 wt.% and the areal sulfur loading was 4.0 mgS cm−2. 
 
1.4. SEI composition characterization 
For analyzing the Li metal in Li | Li symmetric cells used for SEI composition 
characterization, the Li | Li cells were assembled with Li metal foils as both the cathode and 
anode with a diameter of 15.0 mm, and the amount of electrolyte was 40 μL. Each cell was 
cycled 5 times under a current density of 1.0 mA cm−2 and an areal capacity of 1.0 mAh cm−2 
to ensure continuous reaction between Li metal and electrolyte. After cycling, Li metal at the 
plated side was disassembled in the Ar-filled glove box.  
For analyzing the Li metal anode in Li–S cells used for SEI composition characterization, 
the Li–S cells were assembled with sulfur cathodes with a diameter of 13.0 mm, Li metal foils 
as the anode with a diameter of 15.0 mm, PP membranes as the separator, and LiTFSI-LiNO3 
electrolyte as the electrolyte. The electrolyte to sulfur ratio (E/S ratio) was 7.0 μL mgS−1. The 
Li–S cells were cycled at 0.05 C (1 C = 1672 mA g−1) for 1 cycle, and the Li metal anode at the 
charged state was disassembled in the Ar-filled glove box. 
The Li metal was washed with at least 2 mL pure DME to thoroughly remove the residual 
electrolyte. The plated Li was transferred for X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) and 
Time-of-flight secondary ion mass spectrometry (ToF-SIMS) characterizations.  
For analyzing the decomposition of LiTFSI, LiTFSI powder was transferred to XPS after 
being compressed in the Ar-filled glove box.

XPS measurements were conducted using Axis Supra+ by Kratos Analytical with an Al 
Kα X-ray source (1486.8 eV). The sample preparation and transfer process were carried out 
under Ar-filled protection conditions. The sputter etching was performed using Ar+ gun (5 kV)

ToF-SIMS measurements were conducted using PHI nano ToF II by ULVAC-PHI. The

vessel without being exposed to the ambient air. The sputter etching was performed using an 
Ar+ beam (3 kV, 100 nA) to obtain a depth profile. The area of analysis was 50 × 50 μm2. 
 
1.5. SEI structure characterization 
Li metal was deposited on a Ni grid with a capacity of 0.20 mAh cm−2 at 0.10 mA cm−2. 
The Ni grid was soaked by DME for 1 min to wash off the electrolyte. The structure of SEI 
under high resolution was recorded by JEM-2100plus transmission electron microscope (TEM, 
JEOL Ltd.) working at 200 kV with an Elsa cryo-transfer station. In the cryo-TEM test, Ni grid 
was directly mounted to the holder (Gatan 698, cryo-transfer holder) and maintained at −175°C. 
 
1.6. Reaction rate measurement of LiPSs and LiTFSI 
The reaction rate of LiPSs with Li metal was measured by immersing a piece of Li metal 
in 1.0 mL electrolyte with LiPS and detecting the concentration of LiPS through ultraviolet–
visible (UV–Vis) spectroscopy. Electrolytes used for reaction rate measurements were prepared 
by diluting the previously prepared electrolyte with 1.0 M[S] Li2S8, with or without 1.0 M 
LiTFSI, and with or without 2 wt.% LiNO3 50 times by DOL/DME (1:1, by vol.) to targeted 
20 mM[S] Li2S8, with or without 20 mM LiTFSI, and with or without 0.04 wt.% LiNO3 
electrolyte. LiNO3 or Li2S8–LiNO3 treated Li was prepared by immersing a piece of bare Li in 
20 mM LiTFSI and 0.04 wt.% LiNO3 in DOL/DME electrolyte or 20 mM[S] Li2S8, 20 mM

LiTFSI, and 0.04 wt.% LiNO3 in DOL/DME electrolyte for 24 h, respectively. The treated Li 
was then washed by DME to remove the residual electrolyte. 
Five sets of reaction rate measurements were carried out, which are (1) bare Li immersed 
in 20 mM[S] Li2S8 20 mM LiTFSI electrolyte, (2) LiNO3 treated Li immersed in 20 mM[S] Li2S8 
20 mM LiTFSI electrolyte, (3) Li2S8–LiNO3 treated Li immersed in 20 mM[S] Li2S8 20 mM 
LiTFSI electrolyte, (4) bare Li immersed in 20 mM[S] Li2S8 20 mM LiTFSI 0.04 wt.% LiNO3

300 μL of electrolyte was taken out and added to a glass cuvette with 1.0 mm optical path for 
characterization, and then poured back into the original solution to continue the reaction with 
Li metal. The UV–Vis spectroscopy was conducted using AvaSpec-ULS4096CL-EVO by 
Avantes. 
The reaction rate of LiTFSI with Li metal was measured by immersing a piece Li metal in 
1.0 mL electrolyte with LiTFSI and detecting the concentration of LiTFSI through 19F-NMR

previously prepared electrolyte with 1.0 M LiTFSI with or without 1.0 M[S] Li2S8 50 times by

1.7. SEI property characterizations 
Plated Li metal with specific SEI was prepared by plating Li on a nickel (Ni) electrode. 
Each Li | Ni cell was assembled with a thick Li anode (600 μm), a Ni foil, and 40 μL electrolyte. 
Two types of plated Li were prepared using LiTFSI electrolyte or Li2S8-LiTFSI electrolyte, 
respectively. During each cycle of the Li | Ni cells, a designed amount of Li (1.0 mAh cm−2) 
was plated on the Ni electrode at 0.50 mA cm−2 and then stripped until the voltage reached the 
cut-off voltage of 1.0 V vs. Li/Li+. After 5 cycles, 1.0 mAh cm−2 Li was plated at 0.50 mA cm−2 
and then disassembled for further characterization. 
The plated Li morphology was characterized by scanning electron microscope (SEM) at 
3.0 kV (FEI-APREO, Thermo Fisher Scientific). 
The impedance of the plated Li electrode was measured by reassembling the Li electrode 
with a bare Li anode as the counter electrode and 40 μL LiTFSI electrolyte. Electrochemical 
impedance spectroscopy (EIS) measurements were conducted through a Solartron 1470E 
electrochemical workstation. The frequency range was 105–10−1 Hz and the voltage amplitude 
was 10 mV. 
For each type of plated Li electrode, two kinds of reassembled cells were used for the 
calendar aging experiment. The first kind of cell used the plated Li electrode in LiTFSI 
electrolyte to clarify the remained capacity of plate Li in blank electrolyte after aging. The 
second kind of cell used the plated Li electrode in Li2S8-LiTFSI electrolyte to determine the 
remained capacity of plated Li under the existence of LiPSs. Each reassembled cell was rested 
for 20 h and then the plated Li was stripped at 0.10 mA cm−2.

1.8. Evaluation of LiNO3 protection effect 
The Li | Ni cells used for evaluation of the cycling stability of Li metal were assembled 
with Li metal foils with a diameter of 15.0 mm as the anode, Ni foils with a diameter of 16.0 
mm as cathode, and 40 μL electrolyte. The electrolyte used was Li2S8-LiTFSI electrolyte or 
Li2S8-LiTFSI-LiNO3 electrolyte. The Li | Ni cells were cycled within a voltage range of −0.5 
to 0.5 V under a current density of 0.50 mA cm−2 and an areal capacity of 1.0 mAh cm−2. 
The Li | graphene cells were assembled with Li metal foil as anode with a diameter of 15.0 
mm, graphene cathodes with a diameter of 17.0 mm, and 40 μL electrolyte. The electrolyte used 
was Li2S8-LiTFSI electrolyte or Li2S8-LiTFSI-LiNO3 electrolyte. The Li | graphene cells were 
charged potentiostatically at 2.38 V. The shuttle current was determined by the steady-state 
current.  
 
1.9. Solvation structure determination of LiPSs 
7Li NMR spectra were recorded at 400 MHz using Avance NEO 400 by Bruker at room 
temperature. Li2S8-LiTFSI electrolyte or Li2S8-LiTFSI-LiNO3 electrolyte were sealed in NMR 
tubes to perform the NMR tests. A coaxial NMR tube filled with DMSO-d6 was used to assist 
with shimming during the NMR measurements. 
 
1.10. Theoretical simulations 
Molecular dynamics (MD) simulations were performed using the LAMMPS code.[2] Two 
models with or without LiNO3 were constructed. The model without LiNO3 contained 12 Li2S8, 
481 DME, 719 DOL, and 100 LiTFSI molecules, while the model with LiNO3 had the same 
composition as the above model except contained another 29 LiNO3 molecules. The solvent 
force field parameters were generated by LigParGen web server based on OPLS-AA force 
field,[3] except for that advanced restrained electrostatic potential (RESP2) atomic partial 
charges were assigned to each atom, which was obtained based on electrostatic potential (ESP) 

<<PAGE 8>>
 
 
SI7 
charges using Multiwfn program.[4] The parameters for Li+, TFSI−, and S82− were obtained from 
Jensen et al.,[5] Koddermann et al.,[6] and Rajput et al.,[7] respectively. The initial atomic 
coordinates were generated with Packmol, and the final constructions were visualized by 
VESTA.[8] 
The time step was fixed to be 1 fs for all the simulations. All the systems were first 
equilibrated in isobaric-isothermal (NPT) ensemble using the Parrinello-Rahman barostat for 2 
ns to maintain a temperature of 298 K and a pressure of 1 atm with time constants of 0.1 and 1 
ps, respectively. Subsequently, the models were heated from 298.15 to 450.15 K within 0.5 ns 
and maintained at 450.15 K for 1 ns, followed by annealing from 450.15 to 298 K within 0.5 
ns and equilibrated at 298 K in NPT ensemble for another 4 ns. A production run of 7 ns at 298 
K in canonical (NVT) ensemble under Nose-Hoover thermostat were finally conducted. The 
last 5 ns NVT simulations were output every 1000 steps and used for solvation structure 
analyses. The radial distribution function (g(r)) was calculated based on the following formula:
