Experimental 
General: If not noted otherwise all manipulations were performed in an argon filled (99.999%) 
glovebox (GS E-Line) with < 3 ppm O2 and < 1 ppm H2O gas residual content. Accessories for 
the coin cell assembly (casings, spacers, spring from Gelon LIB Group) were washed with 
deionized water and iso-propanol, dried afterwards at 100 °C in vacuum and stored inside the 
glovebox. Whatman GF/D separators (Merck, pore size 1.6 µm, 260 µm thickness) were dried 
and stored in a similar manner. Syntheses were done with a Schlenk line under Argon. 
Solvents were dried over CaH2 followed by molecular sieve. 
Synthesis of chemicals: LiAl(Ohfip)4 was synthesized according to a previously reported 
method.[1] DMT was synthesized according to a previously reported method.[2] 
Preparation of electrolytes: Solvents were dried over CaH2 overnight and then distilled onto 
molar sieve. The needed amount of conductive salt, LiTFSI (E-lyte) or LiAl(Ohfip)4 were 
weighed into a volumetric flask and then dissolved in the solvent. After complete dissolution 
the flask was filled to the respective volume with electrolyte. Electrolytes prepared like this 
were diglyme (Fisher Scientific) 1 M LiTFSI, DMA (Fisher Scientific) 1 M LiTFSI, DME 
(Sigma/Aldrich) 1 M LiTFSI, DME (Sigma/Aldrich) 0.2 M LiTFSI, DME (Sigma/Aldrich) 0.2 M 
LiAl(Ohfip)4, DMI (Sigma/Aldrich) 1 M LiTFSI, DMSO (Carl Roth) 1 M LiTFSI, DMT 0.2 M LiTFSI, 
DMT 1 M LiTFSI, DMT 0.2 M LiAl(Ohfip)4, DOL (Chem Pur)  0.2 M LiTFSI, DOL (Chem Pur)  1 
M LiTFSI, DOL (Chem Pur) 0.2 M LiAl(Ohfip)4, DOL (Chem Pur):DME (Sigma Aldrich) 1:1 (v:v) 
1 M LiTFSI, DOL (Chem Pur):DME (Sigma Aldrich) 1:1 (v:v) 0.2 M LiAl(Ohfip)4, HME (TCI) 1 M 
LiTFSI, HME (TCI): DOL (Chem Pur) 9:1 (v:v) 1 M LiTFSI, OME1 (Carl Roth) 1 M LiTFSI, OME3 
(provided by ASG Analytik service) 1 M LiTFSI, TEGDME (Sigma/Aldrich) 1 M LiTFSI. 
TEGDME (Sigma/Aldrich) 0.2 M LiTFSI, THF (Fisher Scientific) 1 M LiTFSI, THF (Fisher 
Scientific) 0.2 M LiTFSI and TTE (ChemPur):DOL (Chem Pur)  1:1 (v:v) 1 M LiTFSI, 2-methyl 
pyrazine (Fisher Scientific) with 1.6 M LiTFSI, dimethyl pimelate (ChemPUR) with 1.1 M LiTFSI, 
ethyl -methanesulfonate (ABCR) with 1.4 M LiTFSI, ethylacetate (Fisher Scientific) 1.7 M LiTFSI, 
N-ethylmorpholine (Sigma/Aldrich) with 1.4 M LiTFSI (batch 1), N-ethylmorpholine (Fisher 
Scientific) with 1.4 M LiTFSI (batch 2), pimelonitrile (TCI) with 1.4 M LiTFSI, γ-butyrolactone 
(Sigma/Aldrich) with 1.6 M LiTFSI and γ-valerolactone (Fisher Scientific) with 1.6 M LiTFSI. 
Electrolytes that could not be prepared due to low solubility of LiTFSI were 1,3-dioxane 
(ChemPUR) with 1.7 M LiTFSI and 1,4-dioxane (ACROS) with 1.7 M LiTFSI. 
The electrolyte (ACN:LiTFSI 2:1(n:n)):TTE 1:1(v:v) was prepared by first mixing ACN (Carl 
Roth) and LiTFSI in the molar ratio of 2:1 and then mixing the resulting solution with TTE 
(ChemPur) in a ratio of 1:1 (v:v).

Electrochemistry: Shuttle current, impedance and cycling tests were done at 25°C in CR2032 
coin cells with the following configuration: negative case, Li-coin (Ø 16 mm, LTS Chem), 
separator (Whatman GF/D) impregnated with 60 µL of electrolyte, sulfur electrode 
(Nanopowder Enterprises Incoporated (NEI), Ø 14 mm, nominal capacity 875 mAh g-1), spacer 
(0.5 mm), spring and positive case. The parts were crimped the MSK-110 hydraulic crimper 
(MTI cooperation) at a pressure about 1000±50 psi (70±3 bar) for 4 s. Shuttle current and 
impedance measurement (Ivium Octostat 200) were done using the following program: 12 h 
rest, discharge at 0.05 C (compared to theoretical capacity of 1672 mAh g-1 which roughly 
equates to 0.1 C for the nominal capacity) to E<1.4 V, charge at 0.05 C to E>2.7 V, open 
current voltage (OCV) for 15 min, impedance measurement 100 kHz-0.1 kHz, OCV 1 h, 
constant voltage (CV) at E = OCV for 48 h, discharge at 0.05C to DoD = 10 and impedance 
measurement 100 kHz-0.1 kHz. Cycling (CT2001A, LAND) was done with 0.05 C (compared 
to theoretical capacity of 1672 mAh g-1 which roughly equates to 0.1 C for the nominal capacity) 
between 1.4 and 2.7 V. CV-measurements of Li against Fc and Me10Fc were done by 
dissolving Fc or Me10Fc in the electrolyte and measuring using a Pt-working electrode, a Li 
counter electrode and a Li reference electrode at a speed of 20 mV/s. CV-measurements of S 
against Li were done by using a sulfur working electrode (NEI), a Li a Li counter electrode and 
a Li reference electrode at a speed of 0.1 mV/s. 
Data Evaluation: Impedance data was evaluated using the RelaxIS 3 software package.  
PLS modeling: PLS regression was done using R[3] with the packages pls[4] and plsVarSel[5] 
using leave-one-out cross-validation (LOO-CV). Data that was not available was replaced with 
the mean of the dataset to avoid missing data. The optimal number of components was 
determined by doing an analysis of the root mean squared error of prediction (RMSEP). The 
script and output of the PLS models can be found in the supporting data, which can be 
requested under under 10.5281/zenodo.14536751.[6] 
Nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR) spectroscopy: NMR spectra were recorded in pulsed field 
gradient stimulated spin echo experiments on a BRUKER Avance II+ WB 400 MHz and a 
BRUKER Avance III HD 300 MHz at 298 K.
