1.1. General
All reagents and solvents were commercial products and used without further 
purification. iron(III) meso-tetraphenylporphyrin chloride (FeTPP) was purchased from 
Shanghai Macklin Biochemical Technology Co., Ltd. 1,2-dimethoxyethane (DME) was 
purchased from Aladdin Biochemical Technology Co., Ltd. N-methyl pyrrolidinone 
(NMP), sulfur, and lithium sulfide (Li2S) were purchased from Chengdu Colon Chemical 
Co., Ltd. The electrolyte (1.0 M LiTFSI and 2.0 wt. % LiNO3 in a volume ratio 1:1 DOL 
and DME mixed solution), Celgard2500 polypropylene separator (PP), polyvinylidene 
fluoride (PVDF, HSV-300), Super P, carbon paper (CP, TGP-H-060), aluminum (Al) foil, 
and lithium (Li) foil were supplied by Guangdong Canrd New Energy Co., Ltd.

Preparation of FeTPP electrolyte
FeTPP power was dissolved in 1 mL electrolyte containing 1,2-dimethoxyethane 
(DME)/1,3-dioxolane (DOL) solvent, 1.0 mol L1 LiTFSI and 2.0 wt. % LiNO3 to prepare 
the catalysts of FeTPP.1 The FeTPP contents were 0.1wt. %. Take the supernatant and 
dilute it 0.5 times and 1 time.
Preparation of S/C cathode
Super P and sulfur powder were mixed at a mass ratio of 1:3. The mixture was then 
sealed in glass bottles and heated at 155 °C for 6 h in an Ar atmosphere. The S/C composite 
was thoroughly ground before preparing the S/C cathode slurry. The S/C composite, Super 
P, and PVDF binder were then stirred at a mass ratio of 8:1:1 until a viscous paste was 
formed. The prepared slurry was coated on aluminum foil, dried under vacuum condition 
at 60 ℃, and then drilled into sheets with a diameter of 13 mm.
 Material characterizations
The specific structure of the sample was collected on a Bruker SMART APEX-II CCD 
detector system. FeTPP was configured and stored in a glove box filled with Ar at room 
temperature.2 UVVis spectra of FeTPP electrolyte samples placed for one month and 
newly configured FeTPP electrolyte samples were detected by a Shimadzu UV1900 
UVVis spectrometer.3 The Fourier transform infrared spectrum of FeTPP was collected 
on a Nicolet iS50 Fourier transform infrared spectrometer. The morphology of the sample 
was observed by a Zeiss sigma 500 scanning electron microscope (SEM). AFM images 
were collected on an Alysum Research Cypher ES atomic force microscope. The 
morphological evolution of the Li||Li symmetrical configuration during the lithium 
deposition process under different electrolytes was observed in a domestic optical battery 
(Beijing scistar Technology Co., Ltd.) using a YUESCOPE YM710TR optical microscope.
Shuttle current measurements

passivating the surface of the lithium anode, an electrolyte without LiNO3 additive was 
selected. Celgard 2500 membrane was used as the separator, Li as the ande, and the 
DME/DOL solution mixed with 1.0 mol L1 LiTFSI and FeTPP was used to assemble the 
battery. The assembled battery was first charged at 0.2 C to 2.8 V and held for 1000 s, then 
discharged at a constant current of 0.2 C to 2.38 V, and then the chronoamperometric

Li2S6 symmetric cell assembly and tests
Sulfur (641.3 mg, 20.0 mmol) and Li2S (183.8 mg, 4.0 mmol) were dissolved in LSB 
electrolyte (20.0 mL) to form a Li2S6 solution (0.2 mol L1). A symmetrical cell was 
assembled using commercial carbon paper (CP) as both the working and counter electrodes, 
with 20 μL of FeTPP and 0.2 mol L1 Li2S6 as the electrolyte. A control sample was 
prepared with 20 μL of Li2S6 as the electrolyte.The Cyclic voltammetry (CV) tests were 
conducted between –1 and 1 V at a scan rate of 3 mV s1.
Li2S nucleation and dissociation tests
Sulfur (903.4 mg) and Li2S (183.8 mg, 4.0 mmol) were dissolved in tetraethylene 
glycol dimethyl (20.0 mL) to form a Li2S8 solution (0.2 mol L1). CP was cut into small 
discs with a diameter of 13 mm. Super P powder was dispersed in ethanol, and then dropped 
onto CP at a dose of 0.2 mg per piece. A battery was assembled with Super P/CP as the 
cathode, lithium foil as the anode, PP as the separator and Li2S8 and FeTPP electrolyte 
were added for testing. For the blank sample, Li2S8 solution was added, and the rest of the 
operations remained the same. For Li2S nucleation, the assembled battery was discharged 
at 0.112 mA to 2.06 V firstly, and then discharged at a constant potential of 2.05 V until 
the current dropped below 0.01 mA. For Li2S decomposition, the battery was discharged 
at a constant current of 0.112 mA to 1.70 V, and then charged at a constant potential of 
2.35 V until the charging current dropped below 0.01 mA. 
Li plating/stripping tests
Li||Cu and Li||Li cells were assembled and used to perform Li plating/stripping tests. 
A Cu||Li battery was assembled with copper foil as the cathode, lithium foil as the anode, 
and the supernatant of saturated concentration FeTPP solution as the electrolyte. For the 
blank sample, a mixture of DME/DOL solution containing 1.0 mol L1 LiTFSI and 2.0 
wt. % LiNO3 was used as the blank electrolyte. The effective deposition area of lithium 
foil on copper foil was 1.91 cm2. The Cu||Li battery was discharged at a current density of 
0.5 mA cm2 and a capacity of 0.5 mA h cm2, and then stripped at a current density of 0.5 
mA cm2. A Li||Li battery was assembled with lithium foil as both the cathode and anode,

Electrochemical evaluations
Batteries were assembled using S/C cathode as the cathode, lithium as the anode, and 
different contents of FeTPP as the electrolyte. For the blank sample, a mixture of 
DME/DOL solution containing 1.0 mol L1 LiTFSI and 2.0 wt. % LiNO3 was used as the 
blank electrolyte to test the battery performance. The entire cathode sulfur content was 60 
wt. %, and the sulfur mass loading of the conventional and high-load cathodes was 1.21.5 
and 6.6 mg cm2, respectively. The electrolyte/sulfur ratios of the conventional and high-
load batteries were 15.0 and 4.5 μL mgS1 respectively.Charge-discharge curves, rate and 
cycling performance measurements were conducted in the voltage window of 2.81.7 V 
on the Neware battery measurement system. CV, linear sweep voltammetry (LSV), and 
electrochemical impedance spectroscopy (EIS) curves were recorded on a Metrohm 
Autolab G204 electrochemical workstation.
Assembly of pouch cells
The prepared cathode sheet is cut into a shape of 3.0 × 3.0 cm2. A copper foil with a 
thickness of 100.0 μm is used as the current collector for the lithium anode. Nickel tabs 
and aluminum tabs were respectively welded onto the aluminum current collector and 
copper current collector. A PP separator was used as the separator to assemble the pouch 
cell. The electrolyte/sulfur ratio (E/S) of the battery was around 12.0 μL mgS–1. The 
fabrication procedure was performed a glove box filled with a high-purity Ar.
1.3 Theoretical calculations
All calculations were performed using the Vienna Ab-initio Simulation Package 
(VASP) code with density functional theory (DFT).4,5 The Perdew-Burke-Ernzerhof (PBE) 
functional and generalized gradient approximation (GGA) were used for the electron 
exchange-correlation interaction.6,7 The cutoff energy was set to 500 eV, and electron spin 
polarization was considered in all calculations. The vacuum space is 20 A to avoid periodic 
interactions between the slab and its periodic image. For geometric relaxation, the 
convergence criteria for energy and force are less than 105 eV and 0.05 eV/ A, respectively. 
The van der Waals (vdW) correction using the Grim method (DFT-D3) involves using a

interaction between a single molecule/atom and the substrate as a control for the theoretical

Meanwhile, the binding behavior of FeTPP catalyst and LiPSs during the charging and 
discharging process was investigated by using the liquid chromatography-mass 
spectrometry (LC-MS) tests. As shown in Figure S9, the characteristic peaks at 668.95 and 
704.20 m/z correspond to the FeTPP fragment and the initial stage of FeTPP. After 
discharge, additional characteristic peaks of 591.15, 665.10, and 669.20 m/z are observed, 
and classified as chemical formulas [FePor(C6H5)3]+, [FePor(C6H5)3+C3H6O2]+, and 
[FePor(C6H5)3+Li2S2]+. FePor(C6H5)3 is a fragment of FeTPP, and C3H6O2 is the dioxane 
ring (DOL) in the electrolyte. After the charging is completed, two new peaks emerged at 
m/z values of 686.50 and 733.40, respectively attributed to the chemical formulas of 
[FePor(C6H5)3OH+Li2S2]+ and [FePor(C6H5)3+Li2S4]+. The FePor(C6H5)3 as the 
characteristic fragment of FeTPP can be observed at the end of charge stage. This result 
reveals the mechanistic pathway of FeTPP in battery system throughout the cycling process.

The chemical compositions of the cycled lithium foil in Li||Li symmetric cells were 
analyzed using XPS and elemental mapping. No Fe element is detected on the lithium 
surface by either technique in cells with FeTPP, a result identical to that observed with the 
blank electrolyte (Figure S10 and S11). This consistent lack of Fe signals clearly 
demonstrates that FeTPP is not involved in the construction of the SEI film.
