Enhancing Li Cycling Coulombic Efficiency While Mitigating “Shuttle Effect” of Li–S Battery Through Sustained Release of LiNO3

Experimental Section
Battery Assembly and Electrochemical Measurements: The standard electrolyte of LSBs is composed of 1 M lithium bis (trifluoromethane sulfonyl) imide (LiTFSI) in 1, 3-dioxolane (DOL)/1, 2-dimethoxyethane (DME) with a volume ratio of 1:1, and 2 wt% LiNO3. The fluorinated electrolyte is composed of 1 M LiTFSI in DOL/DME/TFTFE with a volume ratio of 4.5:4.5:1, and 2 wt% LiNO3. A total of four cell configurations were examined: with neither LNO-SRF nor TFTFE (denoted by R-SEI), without LNO-SRF but with TFTFE (denoted by F-SEI), with LNO-SRF but not TFTFE (denoted by N-SEI), and with both LNO-SRF and TFTFE (denoted by FN-SEI).
For Li||Li symmetrical cells, bare Li electrodes (thickness: 50 μm) were used as the working and counter electrode. The amount of standard or fluorinated electrolyte was 40 μL in each cell.
For Li||Cu cells, Cu foil electrodes were used as the working electrode, and Li foil were used as counter electrode. The amount of standard or fluorinated electrolyte also was 40 μL in each cell.
The Li plating/stripping tests were performed on the LAND CT3001A (Wuhan, China) battery test system at a current density of 1 mA cm–2 with a capacity of 1 mAh cm–2, respectively. The cyclic voltammetry (CV) curves of Li||Li cell were tested on an electrochemical station with the potential ranging from -0.2 to 0.2 V at a scan rate of 0.1 mV s−1. The exchange current density of the electrode was obtained from the CV data. Electrochemical impedance spectroscopy (EIS) measurements were conducted using an electrochemical workstation within a frequency range of 10 mHz-100 kHz under a voltage amplitude of 10 mV. The activation energy for Li+ through various SEI was obtained via EIS tests at different temperatures. At each temperature, the Li||Li cell was equilibrated for 60 min before the measurement. The Li plating/stripping CEs were tested in Li||Cu cells at a current density of 1 mA cm−2, capacity of 1 mAh cm−2.
For Li||S full cells, CNT/S cathodes (S loading 5.0 mg cm−2) were prepared according to previous reports. Specifically, 80 wt% CNT/S powders, 10 wt% Super P, and 10 wt% polyvinylidene difluorides were mixed in NMP The slurry was coated onto aluminium foils and dried in a vacuum drying oven at 60oC for 12 h. The CNT/S electrodes were used as cathodes, and bare Li electrodes (thickness: 50 μm) were used as anodes. The galvanostatic measurements of the Li||S cells were conducted on LAND systems in a voltage range of 1.7-2.6 V.
Shuttle currents of the Li||S cells with R-SEI and FN-SEI were measured. Before the shuttle current test, the cell was galvanostatically charged and discharged at 0.1 C for different cycles, and then the cells were galvanostatically charged to the 100 % state at 2.9 V, followed by discharging to different voltage stages at 2.90, 2.60, 2.45, 2.43, 2.40, 2.39, 2.38, 2.37, 2.36, 2.35, 2.34, 2.25, and 2.20 V, and then switched to potentiostatic mode. The shuttle current was a potentiostatic steady-state current at different voltage levels.

DFT calculation：The geometry of imidacloprid was optimized under the framework of density functional theory (DFT) with B3LYP functional and 6-311+g* basis set.  In order to describe the dispersion interaction, the DFT-D3 dispersion correction method was used in all calculations. All calculations were performed using Gaussian 16 program. The electrostatic surface potential (ESP) was calculated using Multiwfn program [1,2]. The visualization of the frontier molecular orbitals and mapping of electrostatic surface potential were all rendered using Visual Molecular Dynamic program (VMD) [3].
COMSOL Multiphysics Simulation Method：The length-diameter ratio of deposited Li under the R-SEI and FN-SEI was set to 2 and 1. The electrolyte conductivity was 10−2 S cm−1. The diffusion coefficient was 1.5 × 10−10 m2 s−1. The Li+ concentration was set as 1 M. The current density in the bulk electrolyte was set as 1 mA cm−2.
Characterizations：The crystalline structure was characterized by a Rigaku D/max2600 X-ray diffractometer (XRD). The morphologies of samples were observed using field-emission scanning electron microscopy (FESEM; SU70, Hitachi). The chemical compositions of SEI at different depths (determined by sputtering time) were characterized using X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS; Thermo Scientific K-Alpha+). The roughness of cycled Li electrodes was investigated using atomic force microscopy (AFM; Bruker Multimode 8). The growth behavior of Li dendrites was observed using in-situ optical microscopy (OM; Olympus Corporation). The crystalline structure of the SEI was characterized by transmission electron microscopy (TEM; FEI, Tecnai TF20). The 7Li nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR) experiments were performed using BRUKER AVANCE 400. The Li content was measured by inductively coupled plasma-optical emission spectrometry (ICP-OES; Agilent 5110). The UV-Vis absorption spectra of different electrolytes were recorded using a MAPADA P4 system. The active Li content of cycled Li anodes were tested by titration gas chromatography (TGC; GC-2014C) system.
