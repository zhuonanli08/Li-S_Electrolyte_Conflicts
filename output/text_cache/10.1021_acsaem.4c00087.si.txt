We supplemented the analysis of the sulfur cathodes before cycles. We found that the cathodes 
made from Mac possessed an abundant pore structure, which is favorable for encapsulating 
sulfur-active species during battery charging and discharging. The elements of the cathode surface 
of the cathodes before cycles are mainly composed of O, S, F, and C, where O is derived from 
contact with air, and F is derived from the PVDF.
