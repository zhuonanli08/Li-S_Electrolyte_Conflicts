Table S2. Contact ion pair formation energies ΔE and free energies ΔG for various Li/Mg-polysulfide pairs calculated at the PBE/6-31+G(d,p) 
level in different solvents. Here, S3
•- denotes the radical anion. The corresponding values for LiTFSI and MgTFSI+ are also shown. In all cases, 
the first coordination shell was modelled by explicit solvent molecules, whereas the remainder of the electrolyte has been treated by the 
implicit SMD solvation model as implemented in Gaussian. For DME, the implicit solvent has been approximated by diethyl ether. Moreover, 
the contact ion pair formation energies/free energies have been corrected by the differences between the PBE/6-31+G(d,p)-values and the 
G4MP2-values for the respective cation-anion dimers within the implicit solvent. The formal reaction equations used to calculate the contact-
ion-pair formation energies/free energies are summarized in Table S5, the corresponding optimized geometries are shown in Figures S11-S14.
