Methods Used for PCA Analysis:
Principal component analysis (PCA) was originally introduced by Pearson and later developed in a formal multivariate framework by Hotelling, where it is defined as the eigen decomposition of the covariance matrix of standardized variables. The mathematical formulation and computation of PC scores as linear combinations of standardized variables are detailed in Jolliffe (2002) and Shlens (2014), and recent overviews of PCA methodology are provided by Jolliffe & Cadima (2016).
