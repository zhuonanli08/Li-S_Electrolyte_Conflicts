Suppressing Shuttle Effect via Cobalt Phthalocyanine Mediated Dissociation of Lithium Polysulfides for Enhanced Li-S Battery Performance

Wei Zhang, Jiawen Zhu, Yadong Ye, Jiaxuan She, Xianghua Kong, Song Jin*, Zhangquan Peng*, Hengxing Ji* 

Experiment Section
In-situ Raman Experiment
Raman spectra were acquired using a customized LabRAM HR800 confocal Raman microscope (Horiba Jobin Yvon) featuring a 532 nm laser source for excitation. The three-electrode cell incorporated a quartz base allowing laser transmission. 4 mM S8 was dissolved into DOL/DME (1:1 vol.%) with 1 M LiTFSI as electrolyte. The CoPc-containing electrolyte was prepared by directly dissolving CoPc into the electrolyte and filtering it. Electrochemical measurements were conducted using a CHI 660e electrochemical station, with cyclic voltammetry (CV) applied between 1.5 V and 3.0 V at a scan rate of 5 mV s-1.

RDE Experiment
The RDE experiment was performed using an RRDE-3A (ALS, Japan) and a CHI 660e electrochemical station (CH Instruments Inc.). The RDE was housed in a custom cell with a lithium disk serving as both reference and counter electrode at the bottom. The same electrolyte utilized in the Raman experiment was employed in the RDE test. Linear sweep voltammetry (LSV) was conducted from 3 to 1.5 V at a scan rate of 20 mV s-1, while the rotation speed varied between 625, 900, 1225, 1600, and 2025 rpm. XPS Analyse
After cycled 50 times, lithium foils were disassembled from the Li║Li symmetrical batteries in an argon-filled environment. The Li foils from the cathode side, where lithium metal deposition occurred, were rinsed with DOL/DME solvent and stored in a sealed transfer box until transfer to the XPS sample warehouse (XPS, ESCALAB 250).

Li-S cell assembly and electrochemical characterization
Reduced graphene oxide powder was mixed with sulfur (sulfur content: 80 wt.%) and placed in a sealed glass tube under an Ar atmosphere. The mixture was annealed at 155 °C for 10 hours to synthesize the S@G composites. For cathode electrodes, a slurry containing 70 wt.% sulfur/graphene composites, 20 wt.% Ketjen black, and 10 wt.% PVDF in N-methyl-2-pyrrolidone (NMP) solvent was uniformly coated onto carbon-coated aluminum foil. After drying in a vacuum oven at 45 °C for over 12 hours, the coated foil was punched into 10 mm diameter disks and assembled into CR2032 coin cells with Celgard 2400 polypropylene separator and lithium foils as the anode. The electrolyte consisted of 1.0 M LiTFSI, 10 mM Li2S6 and 2 wt.% LiNO3 in a DOL/DME mixed solvent with 1 mg mL-1 CoPc, while a conventional 1.0 M LiTFSI, 2 wt.% LiNO3, and 10 mM Li2S6 in DOL/DME mixed solvent was used as the control. Galvanostatic discharge/charge measurements were conducted using a Neware battery cycler (CT-4008T-5v10Mv-164) in the voltage range of 1.8–2.7 V.
