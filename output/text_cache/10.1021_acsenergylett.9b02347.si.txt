Experimental Section
1. Preparation of Li2S/C Composites
Synthesis of the Li2S/C composites was based on a previously reported method.[1] Graphene 
nanoplatelet aggregates (GNAs) were purchased from Strem Chemicals (Newburyport, MA, 
USA). Vapor-grown carbon fibers (VGCF™) were acquired from Showa Denko K. K. (Tokyo, 
Japan). Acetylene black (AB) was obtained from Denka Co., Ltd. (Tokyo, Japan). Ketjen black 
(KB) was supplied from Lion Specialty Chemicals Co., Ltd. (Tokyo, Japan). CNovel™ 
mesoporous carbons with controlled average mesopore sizes of 30 and 50 nm (designated as 
CNovel™ 30 and 50, respectively) were purchased from Toyo Tanso (Osaka, Japan).
In a typical synthesis, GNAs (0.48 g) were first dispersed in 70 mL of an aqueous solution 
containing Li2SO4·H2O (1.60 g) via ultrasonication for 2 h. After stirring the suspension for 4 
h, ethanol (800 mL) was added dropwise. The black precipitate was collected via filtration, 
washed several times with ethanol, then dried under vacuum at 40 °C for 12 h. The isolated 
precursor was heated at 200 °C for 2 h to remove water, then calcined at 800 °C for 2 h in a 
tube furnace under an Ar atmosphere. The Li2S content in the material was determined 
gravimetrically by weighing the solid before and after Li2S removal by methanol washing.

Tetrafluoroethyl-2,2,3,3-tetrafluoropropyl ether (HFE) was obtained from Daikin (Osaka,

Li[TFSA] was mixed with G4 and SL at molar ratios of 1:1 and 1:2, respectively, to yield 
highly concentrated [Li(G4)][TFSA] and [Li(SL)2][TFSA] electrolytes. In some cases, HFE 
was added to the concentrated electrolytes to reduce viscosity and improve ionic conductivity 
while maintaining low PS solubility. All of the electrolytes were prepared in a glove box filled 
with Ar at a dew point below −80 °C and an O2 concentration below 1 ppm.
3. Preparation of Electrodes
Poly(N-vinylpyrrolidone) (PVP, MW = 1200 kDa) was purchased from Junsei Chemical Co., 
Ltd. (Tokyo, Japan). N-methyl-2-pyrrolidone (NMP) was obtained from Wako Pure Chemical 
Corp. Artificial graphite was obtained from Hitachi Chemical Co., Ltd. (Tokyo, Japan). 
Poly(vinylidene fluoride) (PVDF) was purchased from Kureha Corp. (Tokyo, Japan). An Al 
foam current collector was kindly supplied by Sumitomo Electric Industries (Osaka, Japan).
Li2S/C cathodes: The Li2S/C composite electrodes were prepared in a glove box under Ar. Each 
Li2S/C composite was mixed with an additional portion of the corresponding pristine carbon-
based material and PVP in NMP for 24 h to form a slurry. The weight ratio of C in the Li2S/C 
composite to C in the pristine material was held constant at 60:30, and PVP was added to obtain 
a final reagent ratio of 60:30:10. The obtained slurry was uniformly coated onto TGP-H-060 
carbon paper (Toray Industries, Tokyo, Japan) or the Al foam current collector. For the coin 
cell tests, the composite electrodes were cut into circular disks 15 mm in diameter and dried 
under vacuum at 80 °C for 12 h. 
Graphite anodes: Artificial graphite (90 wt.%), AB (3 wt.%), and PVDF (7 wt.%) were mixed 
in NMP to form a slurry, then coated onto a Cu foil collector. After drying in an oven at 80 °C 
for 12 h, the electrode material was cut into circular disks 16 mm in diameter. To ensure good 
electrical conductivity, the obtained electrodes were compressed and dried for an additional 24 
h under vacuum at 80 °C.

4. Characterization
Structural analysis was performed using XRD on an Ultima IV diffractometer (Rigaku Corp. 
Tokyo, Japan) equipped with a Cu Kα (λ = 0.154 nm) radiation source. The specific surface 
areas were determined by adsorption with the Brunauer–Emmett–Teller (BET) method using a 
BELSORP-miniII surface area/porosity analyzer (MicrotracBEL Corp., Osaka, Japan). TG 
measurements were carried out using a TG-DTA2020SA thermal analyzer (Bruker Japan, 
Yokohama, Japan).
5. Electrochemical Measurements
Li/Li2S half cells, Li/graphite half cells, and Li2S/graphite full cells were assembled into 2032 
coin-type cells using Celgard 3501 as a separator. Unless otherwise noted, a Li2S/C composite 
cathode made with KB was used as the Li2S electrode for the battery tests. Galvanostatic 
charge–discharge measurements were performed at 30 °C with a BTS-2004 battery testing 
system (Nagano Corp., Ichikawa, Japan). The cutoff potentials for the graphite/Li half cell tests 
were 0.001 and 2.0 V. The Li/Li2S half cells and the Li2S/graphite full cells were initially 
charged with an open-circuit voltage (OCV) of 4.4 V (half cell) or 4.2 V (full cell) to activate 
the Li2S cathode. The half cells and full cells were operated in the voltage range of 1.1–3.3 V 
during the discharge and charge steps. The 1 C rate was set at 1166 mA g−1, and the specific 
capacity of each cell was calculated based on the mass of Li2S. Unless otherwise noted, E/S 
was in the range of 5–10.
