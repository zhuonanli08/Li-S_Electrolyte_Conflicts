Supplementary experimental details 
The detection of PS concentration  
The concentration of PS was tested by transforming all the soluble sulfur species into 
insoluble sulfate salt and then weighing. Firstly, 1~2 g certain amount of PS solution was 
dispersed in 20 g 30 wt.% hot NaOH aqueous solution (orange). Some insoluble elemental S 
may precipitate out during this process but gradually disappeared due to the 
disproportionation in hot alkaline solution. Secondly, 30 wt.% H2O2 was added drop by drop 
into the above solution with continuously stirring until the solution was colorless. After that, 
all the sulfur species were transformed into SO4
2-. Thirdly, add BaCl2 to precipitate all the 
sulfate anions into BaSO4 sediment. Then, the sediments were collected by centrifugation and 
filtration. At last, the BaSO4 was washed three times with pure water and dried in 120 °C for 
12 hours. Through weighting the BaSO4 sediment, we could calculate dissolved sulfur content 
in the electrolyte.

Soft package battery assembly 
The cathodes were prepared by mixing the C/S composites with sodium carboxymethyl 
cellulose at a weight ratio of 9:1 in water, and coated onto carbon coated Al foil in room 
temperature. The electrodes were further dried at 70 °C under vacuum overnight. The sulfur 
loading on the aluminum is about 4.0 mg cm−2.  
Firstly, the lithium foil with the length of 1.5 m was spread on to a glass plate; then, the 
Celgard 2325 membrane with the length of 3 m was used to wrap the lithium foil; third, 
dozens of the cathodes with the size of 5 cm × 8 cm was stacked between the Celgard 2325 
separators and lithium folded in the Z type; the tabs of cathodes and anodes were melted 
together under ultrasonic spot welding machine; then, the electrodes stacks were pressed by 
hand and packed into aluminum plastics shells; last, the electrolyte was added into the 
aluminum plastics shells before sealed. As to be noted that, the soft package stacks were 
pressed tightly to ensure no more electrolyte was remained in the corners of the aluminium 
plastics shells. The volume of electrolyte in coin Li-S cells is 12 mL and the volume/mass 
ratios of electrolyte and sulfur is 4. The assembly process illustration of soft package Li-S cell 
is shown in Figure S2.
