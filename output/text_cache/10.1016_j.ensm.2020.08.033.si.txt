An organic nickel salt-based electrolyte additive boosts homogeneous catalysis for lithium-sulfur batteries 








Figure S1. The UV-Vis spectra of Li2S6 solution and the upper-level Li2S6 solution after added NiDME. The peaks in the yellow line of Li2S6 solution demonstrate the existence of polysulfide ions. After NiDME was added into Li2S6 solution, the intensities of these peaks in the collected upper-level solution were dramatically decreased, suggesting the LiPSs were captured by NiDME and participated.
