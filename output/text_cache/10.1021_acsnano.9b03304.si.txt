Figure S10. Different products and additive in electrolyte can help to form the dense SEI layer at 
different times. (a) C 1s 0s, C 1s 700s, C 1s 1400s, and (b) S 2p 0s, S 2p700s, S 2p 1400s indicate 
the depths at which the C 1s or S 2p spectra are acquired. Compared with the traditional 
compositions of SEI layer for Li-S batteries, two extra compositions of LiNPH and organic 
carbonates are introduced by BNC-added electrolyte. The benzene ring in the LiNPH endows the 
SEI layer with enhanced rigidity. All of these promote the formation of dense SEI layers with the 
excellent rigidity and flexibility during the cycles, which can suppress growth of Li dendrites.
