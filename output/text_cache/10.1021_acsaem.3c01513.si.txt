Figure S1  Solubility tests of KFSI and LiFSI in D2 solvent.  
We attempted to add the KFSI or LiFSI in the range of 0.1–10.0 wt.% to 10g of the SL electrolyte. 
The results indicated that LiFSI dissolved slightly more than 1 wt.%, whereas KFSI dissolved 
above 10 wt.%, as mentioned at the beginning of the results and discussion section.

Figure S3  Solubility tests of KFSI and LiFSI in SL containing LiTFSI.  
We attempted to add the KFSI or LiFSI in the range of 0.1–10.0 wt.% to 10g of the SL electrolyte. 
The results indicated that LiFSI dissolved slightly more than 1 wt.%, whereas KFSI dissolved 
above 10 wt.%, as mentioned at the beginning of the results and discussion section.  
 
In this test, the same amout of KFSI or LiFSI as that used in the SL electrolyte was put into LiTFSI-
containing SL that constituted the SL electrolyte. 
 
Results: KFSI dissolved in all cases, whereas LiFSI dissolved only at 0.1 and 1.0 wt.%. These 
results are exactly the same as those obtained in the solubility tests of KFSI and LIFSI in the SL 
electrolyte, which also contains D2.
