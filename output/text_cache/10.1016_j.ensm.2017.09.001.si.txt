Supporting information to
The synergetic interaction between LiNO3 and lithium polysulfides for suppressing shuttle effect of lithium-sulfur batteries 

Cathode fabrication
Sulfur powder (Sigma-Aldrich), Super C45 (TIMCAL) are added into the PVDF binder solution to form a cathode slurry. The composition of cathode slurry is sulfur:C45:PVDF = 60:30:10 (wt%). The slurry is mixed by a ball-milling method for 48 hours to achieve a homogeneous distribution. Then the slurry was coated onto an aluminum current collector with a doctor blade using an Elcometer motorized film applicator. The coated slurry was kept in air to dry overnight. Finally, 1.26 cm2 cathodes with a sulfur loading of 2.53 mg/cm2 were punched out and dried in a vacuum oven for 2 days at 50 ºC before transferred into the argon-filled glove box for coin cell assembly. 
Cell assembly and testing
Coin cells (CR2325, National Research Council Canada) were assembled in an argon-filled glove box with O2 and H2O content less than 0.5 ppm. Lithium metal foil was used as anode and polypropylene celgard 2400 was used as separator. The electrolyte was 1M lithium bis(trifluoromethanesulfonyl)imide (LiTFSI) dissolved in a mixture of 1,3-dioxolane (DOL) and dimethoxyethane (DME) (1:1 in volume) with and without 2 wt% LiNO3 as an additive. The separator is polypropylene film (Celgard 2400). 16 µL/mg-S of electrolyte is added into each cell. The electrochemical performance was measured galvanostatically in a voltage window of 1.8-2.6 V on a Maccor series 4000 cell tester. The specific capacities were calculated based on the mass of sulfur in the electrodes. Cells used for SEM characterization were tested at 0.05C for 1 cycle, and stopped at fully lithiated or fully delithiated state. These cells were then disassembled in argon-filled glove box. After that the cathodes were flushed using DME/DOL(1:1 in volume) solvent and dried before SEM study.
X-ray absorption spectra
The operando sulfur K-edge XAS spectra were measured at beamline 5.3.1 at Advanced Light Source, Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory. The XAS spectra were collected in total fluorescence yield mode and calibrated using elemental sulfur spectra by setting the position of white line to 2472.2 eV.22 All the XAS spectra were measured under constant helium flow in the sample chamber and acquired continuously during the discharge/charge processes at a 0.1C rate. The XAS spectra were background subtracted and normalized to the absorption pre- and post-edges. The cells used to perform in-situ and operando XAS experiments were adapted from the CR2325 coin cells: a 2×1 mm2 hole was drilled at the sulfur (cathode) side of the cell housing using a high precision laser system; the hole was then sealed with a 20-µm thick kapton film to avoid leaking and allow X-ray beam penetration. To investigate the species evolution in the electrolyte (including the separator) and lithium anode during the charge/discharge process, a 3×1.5 mm2 hole was also drilled on sulfur cathode to make X-ray directly probe into the electrolyte.  The ex-situ XAS spectra were performed at beamlines 6.3.1.2 and 8.0.1.3 at Advanced Light Source of Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory. The XAS spectra were collected by surface-sensitive total electron yield (TEY, probing depth of about 5 nm).
