2.Computation Methods
MD simulations were carried out using Forcite. For the EDHV-112 electrolyte, initially, 545 EC,432 DMC,555 HFME,228 VC, 200 Li+, 200 TFSI- were packed into an 73.67×73.67×73.67 Å3 box; For the EDHV-114 electrolyte, initially, 364 EC,288 DMC,740 HFME,228 VC, 200 Li+, 200 TFSI- were packed into an 74.11×74.11×74.11 Å3 box; For the EDHV-116 electrolyte, initially, 272 EC,215 DMC,832 HFME,228 VC, 200 Li+, 200 TFSI- were packed into an 72.13×72.13×72.13 Å3 box; For the ED-11 electrolyte, initially, 1200 EC,950 DMC, 200 Li+, 200 TFSI- were packed into an 73.67×73.67×73.67 Å3 box;
The systems were first geometry optimizated until the maximum energy converged into 10-4 kcal·mol-1. After heated to their respective target temperatures (298.15K for RT),the systems were followed by 10 ns equilibration under isothermal–isovolumetric ensemble (NVT).
