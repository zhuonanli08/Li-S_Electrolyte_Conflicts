Figure S13: Cluster size distributions derived from MD simulations for LT0-PS410, LT0-PS210, 
LT0-PS205-NO05, and LT05-PS205 in a log-lin representation. The clustering behavior of LT0-

large PS cluster (panels c and d). The presence of LiNO3 or LiTFSI reduces the sizes of clusters 
(see panel d). Panel d is a zoomed-in area of panel (c).
