Experiment section
Raw materials
All raw materials were purchased through commercial channels without further processing. Holmium chloride hexahydrate (HoCl3·6H2O, Aladdin), Carbon nanotubes (CNT, Nanjing XFNANO Materials Tech Co., Ltd), acetylene black (AB), polyvinylidene difluoride (PVDF, HSV-900, Shenzhen Kejing Eectronic Co., Ltd.), N-Methylpyrrolidone (NMP, Aladdin), Li2S (Aladdin), Sulfur powder (Aladdin), Lithium-sulfur electrolyte (1M LiTFSI in DOL/DME (vol 1:1) with 1wt% LiNO3, DoDoChem.), Lithium (Li, China Energy Lithium Co., Ltd.)
Preparation of the electrolytes
The modified electrolyte involves adding HoCl3 (0.1 M) to the traditional base electrolyte (1M LiTFSI in DOL/DME (vol 1:1) with 1wt% LiNO3) and a certain amount of molecular sieve balls are added for water removal. The prepared electrolyte is fully dissolved for 24 hours before use.
Preparation of the sulfur cathode  
Firstly, sulfur powder and CNT were mixed in a ratio of 7:3 and heated at 155°C for 12 hours to obtain CNT/S. Then, CNT/S was mixed with AB and PVDF in an 8:1:1 (240 mg:30 mg: 30 mg) weight ratio, and NMP (about 1.5 mL) was added as a solvent to obtain a black cathode slurry. Coating the cathode slurry with a scraper on the carbon-coated aluminum (Al) foil, then dry overnight under vacuum at 60°C and punch the cathode electrode plate into a diameter of 12 mm.
Electrochemical measurements  
Li–S batteries
The CR2025 (Guangdong Canrd New Energy Technology Co., Ltd) Li–S coin battery was assembled in the Ar-filled glove box (O2<0.01 ppm, H2O<0.01 ppm), with CNT/S as the cathode, lithium as the anode, and coating CNT on separator-coated polypropylene (PP) 2500 separator to physically limit the LiPSs shuttle. The loading of CNT was about 0.3 mg cm−2. The sulfur load was 1.3-1.5 mg cm−2 and 50 μL electrolyte for every coin battery. For batteries with higher sulfur loading (11.43 mg cm−2), the Electrolyte/Sulfur (E/S) is 6 μL mg−1. Aluminum mesh was employed as the current collector (100 mesh, Kunshan Guangjiayuan New Materials Co., LTD), and the cathode had a diameter of 10 mm. The constant current charging and discharging experiments were conducted between 1.7-2.8 V (Wuhan Land Electronics Co., Ltd.). Other electrochemical tests were conducted on the Chenhua CH660E electrochemical workstation. The CV test measurements were conducted in the same potential range of (1.7-2.8 V,) at a scanning speed rate of 0.1 mV s−1, and. The EIS test frequency was conducted in the range of 0.01-100000 Hz.

Li2S6 symmetrical cell
0.5 M Li2S6 for the LiPSs catalytic experiment. Sulfur powder and Li2S in a 5:1 weight ratio in the glove box were mixed and added to 1 M LiTFSI with 1wt% LiNO3 in DOL/DME (vol 1:1), and continuously stirred at 60°C for 48 hours to obtain brownish red Li2S6. The Li2S6 symmetrical battery used carbon paper as the counter electrode, with 10 μL Li2S6 and 10 μL modified electrolyte added to both sides of the separator. The CV test range was chosen between -1 to 1 V, and the scanning speed ranges from 1 mV s−1 to 9 mV s−1. The frequency range for EIS testing was 0.01 to 100000 Hz.
Li2S8 battery
0.2 M Li2S8 for the Li2S nucleation and decomposition. Sulfur powder was mixed with Li2S in a 7:1 ratio in the glove box, added to tetraethylene glycol dimethyl ether, and stirred at 60°C for 24 hours to obtain brownish red Li2S8. For the Li2S deposition experiments, the Li2S8 battery were assembled using carbon paper as the cathode, and lithium as the anode. The electrolytes were 20 μL of 0.2 M Li2S8 and 20 μL modified electrolyte was added. The batteries were first discharged to 2.05 V at a constant current of 0.112 mA, and then at a constant voltage of 2.02 V for 10 hours.
The decomposition of Li2S was evaluated using a cell similar to that used for the Li2S deposition. First, the batteries were discharged at 0.113 mA constant current to 1.7 V and then discharged to a voltage less than 1.7 V at a constant current of 0.011 mA to ensure complete Li2S deposition. Subsequently, they were charged at a constant voltage of 2.4 V for 10 hours, and the decomposition curve of Li2S was measured.
Li||Li symmetrical cell and Li||Cu cell
Li||Li symmetrical cell was assembled in the Ar-filled glove box (O2<0.01 ppm, H2O<0.01 ppm), Li metal as the electrode, and 60 μL electrolyte for every coin battery. Li||Li symmetrical cell tested under 0.5 mA cm−2 with 0.5 mAh cm−2. The Li||Cu cell was discharged and then charged to 1.0 V with 0.5 mA cm−2 with 1.0 mAh cm−2.
Characterization technologies
A Field emission scanning electron microscope (FE-SEM, Gemini SEM 500, Zeiss) determined the morphology of the Li2S deposited/decomposition on carbon paper, Li anode, after cycling. The Raman spectroscopic measurements of the electrolytes were conducted through Thermo Fisher DXR2xi with an argon ion laser (532 nm). The fourier transform infrared spectroscopy (FTIR) was employed for the analysis of the molecular structures and functional groups of additives and electrolytes (VERTEX70). Surface characterization of the SEI composition of the Li anode after cycling was performed by X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) (ESCALAB Xi+, Thermo Fisher Scientific).
