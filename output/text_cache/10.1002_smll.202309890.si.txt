Preparation of cathode materials and electrolytes: Firstly, the carbon nanotubes-
sulfur (CNTs-S) composites were prepared by typical fusion-diffusion method. CNTs 
and S were fully mixed and ground at a mass ratio of 3:7 (~69 wt% S within the cathode, 
Figure S1), and a certain amount of CS2 was added to the mixture for sufficient fusion. 
The mixture was heated at 155℃ for 12 h in an oven to form CNTs-S composites. Then, 
the as-synthesized CNTs-S composite, polyvinylidene fluoride (PVDF) and CNTs were 
added to 1-methyl-2-pyrrolidinone (NMP) solvent at a mass ratio of 80:5:15, forming 
a mixed slurry. The mixed slurry was coated on clean aluminum (Al) foil and dried at 
55℃ for 12 h. The areal S loading of cathode was controlled at 1, 4 or 4.5 mg cm−2 for 
the routine and harsh condition measurements. Extra BzCl was dissolved in commercial 
standard Li–S electrolyte with varying concentrations for tests. 
 
Fabrication of Li–S and Li/Li symmetric cells: Electrochemical measurements were 
conducted using CR2025-coin-type cells, which were assembled inside an Ar-filled 
glove box (O2<0.1 ppm, H2O<0.1 ppm). In a Li–S battery, Celgard 2400 separator was 
employed as the separator, CNTs-S composites were applied as the working electrode 
and Li metal was used as the count electrode. The amount of electrolyte injected is kept 
at 60 μL for the common cell and 62 μL for the high-S-loading cell with low 
electrolyte/S ratio (E/S) (4 mg cm–2). For the assembly of the Li/Li symmetric battery, 
2 pieces of Li metal were applied as the electrodes. The electrolyte is prepared by 
adding 5 mM Li2S8 in the standard Li–S electrolyte to simulate the true environment of 

<<PAGE 4>>
 
3 
Li–S battery.  
 
Electrochemical measurements: The cycling performances of the cells were cycled 
between 1.6–2.8 V on a Neware multichannel battery cycler under different rates (1 
C=1675 mA g–1 based on the mass of S). Cyclic voltammetry (CV) and electrochemical 
impedance (EIS) tests were recorded at room temperature using an electrochemical 
workstation (CHI 760E). The scan rate of CV tests was 0.1 mV s–1. EIS test was tested 
under frequency range of 100 kHz~0.01Hz and AC amplitude of 5 mV. For the visible 
H-type cell tests, the cathode with a S mass of 1.4 mg was used, and the cells were 
discharged at 0.1 mV s–1 from 2.8 V to 1.6 V on an electrochemical workstation (CHI 
760E). 
 
7Li NMR spectra: The 7Li NMR spectra were recorded at 600 MHz using Varian NMR 
system at 25℃. The solution with 0.3 M Li2S8 in DME/DOL (1:1 vol%) was used as 
the reference (0 ppm). And the Li2S8-d-THF solution was prepared by mixing 1 mL of 
tetrahydrofuran-D8 (d-THF) in 2 mL of Li2S8 solution. As a contrast, Li2S8-d-THF-BzCl 
solution was prepared by mixing 40 μL of BzCl in 3 mL of Li2S8-d-THF solution. 
 
The GC–MS spectra: The spectra of gas chromatography-mass spectrometry (GC–
MS) were run on QP-2010 Ultra to confirm the content in the electrolyte at 130℃. 
Firstly, the solution with 20 μL of BzCl in 2 ml of DOL was used as the reference. Then, 
the solution of experimental group was prepared by mixing 20 μL of BzCl and 2 mL of

4 
Li2S8 solution (5 mM in DOL). The GC–MS was also used for the radical trapping 
experiment. The electrolytic cell was equipped with carbon cloth (0.5×0.5 cm2) and 
platinum as electrodes, where 2,2,6,6-ramethylpiperidine oxide (TEMPO, 2 mmol), 
BzCl (20 mmol) and the standard electrolyte (5 mL) were added. The above electrolyte 
was taken out for the GC–MS test after the cell was electrolyzed at a constant current 
of 1 mA at room temperature for 5 h with constant stirring.[1]  
 
The Raman spectroscopic measurements: Raman spectra were acquired using an 
inVia Raman spectrometer (Renishaw, UK) at 532 nm with a 50 mW Ar-ion laser. The 
spectra of the electrolyte were recorded from 80 to 3150 cm−1, and all liquid samples 
were sealed in capillary glass tubes. Laser power was set at 500 mV, and 400 scans 
were accumulated with a resolution of 4 cm−1.  
 
XPS measurements: X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) spectra were recorded 
using a PHI ADEPT 1010 with a monochromatic Al Kα X-ray source (150 kV, 10 mA 
emission current) with the beam size of 200 μm. The binding energies were corrected 
by setting the binding energy of the hydrocarbon C 1s feature to 284.8 eV. In situ Li 
sputtering was conducted using an Ar+ ion gun rastered over a 2 mm × 2 mm area and 
the accelerating voltage of the ions was adjusted between 2 kV and 4 kV.  
Scanning electron microscopy (SEM) images were acquired with a JSM-6700F field 
emission scanning electron microscope. UV–vis spectra were recorded on a UV-1800 
UV–vis spectrophotometer (Shimadzu, Japan). The timelapse video with 1.5 wt%
