GITT experiments were conducted at 0.1 C as a sequence of pulses for twenty minutes, 
and relaxed for an hour to make the cells restore to equilibrium until the cells are fully 
discharged or charged.3 GITT curves of G/S-NBP and G/S-blank cells seem to exhibit 
different polarization characteristics (Figure S15a, b). In order to clearly show the difference 
in polarization characteristics, the overpotential at the end of each current pulse is plotted in a 
time-dependent histogram (Figure S15c, d). The overpotential of G/S-NBP cell shows 
fluctuant variation, which is fundamentally different from G/S-blank cell that has a typical 
polarization behavior of sulfur cathode kinetics.3
