#：Accumulative charge pass (unit: mAh cm-2) is the total discharge capacity during the SPAN cells lifespan.
S：This is calculated by sulfur loading rather than SPAN loading.
*：Following Reference[3], N/P is calculated as N/P = (Ca-Cir-Cr) / Cr, where Ca, Cir, Cr, denote the negative electrode capacity, first-cycle irreversible capacity, and reversible cyclic capacity, respectively. Here, discharge capacities at specific cycles are used to approximate these values: N/P ≈ (Ca-C1st) / C3rd, with C1st and C3rd being the first- and third-cycle discharge capacities. This approach enables straightforward comparison of N/P with lithium-containing cathode-based metal lithium batteries (e.g., Li-LFP or Li-NCM).
