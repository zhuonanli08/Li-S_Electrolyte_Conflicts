BASIS–DFT energetic and geometry benchmarks
for adsorption and reaction in modeled lithium clus-
ters
To supplement the RDF comparison, we performed BASIS–DFT benchmarks for adsorp-
tion and reaction in modeled lithium clusters using Li13 and Li20, intact PC adsorption,
PC decomposition into propene plus a carbonate-like fragment bound to the lithium clus-
ter, and DIO, S8, and fragmented-S4 adsorption. All benchmark structures contain at
most 31 atoms. BASIS was evaluated with the r2SCAN and ωB97X readout heads after
DFT geometry optimization at the corresponding levels of theory. The electronic reac-
tion energies and geometry metrics below are reported without applying an agreement
filter.
The expanded adsorption set supports the same qualitative trend as the PC decompo-
sition set: BASIS-r2SCAN gives the most consistent agreement with the matching DFT
level, including close adsorption energies for Li13–DIO, Li13–S8, Li13–S4, and Li20–S4.
The ωB97X readout shows larger energetic deviations for several sulfur-containing and
PC decomposition cases, so those results are used as a readout-sensitivity check rather
than as quantitative thermochemistry.

RDF benchmark against VASP r2SCAN
The radial distribution function (RDF) benchmark compares the basis-model r2SCAN
readouts with a DFT-MD simulation performed at the r2SCAN level as a reference trajec-
tory over the aligned overlap window. The overlap contains 660 configurations sampled
every 10 fs, corresponding to a 10-ps trajectory segment.
This benchmark validates local structural correlations for the selected lithium elec-
trolyte interface model over the comparison window. It should not be interpreted as a
complete validation of reaction energetics, electron-transfer events, or long-time dendrite
growth pathways. Those aspects require additional system-specific electronic-structure
calculations that are beyond the scope of the present revision.
