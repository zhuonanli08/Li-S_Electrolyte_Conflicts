EXPERIMENTAL METHODS 
Preparation of the various LiTFSI-DOL electrolyte solutions 
LiTFSI powder (molecular mass = 287.1 g·mol-1 ; Sigma-Aldrich 99.95% purity) was dried 
for at least 48 hours in a tubular glass vacuum oven (Buchi®, Pressure < 10-3 Bar) at a 
temperature of 130 °C. The salt was then transferred into an Ar–filled glovebox (O2 level < 
0.3 ppm and H2O level < 1.0 ppm) together with the as-received DOL bottle (Sigma-Aldrich, 
99.8 % purity, anhydrous). Subsequently, appropriate amounts of LiTFSI and DOL were 
weighed inside the glovebox (by means of an OHAUS Adventurer AR0640 electronic 4–digit 
balance), and mixed. The prepared electrolyte solutions were left stirring until complete 
dissolution of the salt occurred. For the electrolyte concentrations less than 1 × 10-3 molal, 
serial dilution method was applied in order to reach the desired concentrations. 
Electrochemical tests 
The various LiTFSI-DOL electrolyte solutions were studied by means of Potentiostatic 
Electrochemical Impedance Spectroscopy (PEIS) technique using a specific conductivity cell, 
i.e., High Temperature Conductivity Cell – HTCC from Biologic®. The HTCC allowed the 
measurements of electrolyte conductivities ranging approximatively from 1×10-6 S·cm-1 to 1 
×10-1 S cm-1. The HTCC consists of a two-electrode setup were each electrode is comprised 
of a platinum plate, placed in parallel to each other, covered with a black platinum layer in 
order to increase the effective surface area of the electrode. The glass tube (used to contain 
and isolate the Pt-cell) of the HTCC was filled with 1 mL of electrolyte solution. The Pt-cell 
was subsequently placed inside the electrolyte-filled glass tube. The whole procedure was 
carried out inside an Ar–filled glovebox (O2 level < 0.3 ppm and H2O levels < 1.0 ppm). The 
electrolyte-filled HTCC was subsequently placed inside a cooled incubator (Gallenkamp®) in 
order to maintain a fixed temperature of 25 °C ± 0.2 °C and connected to a VMP2 multi-

solution (HACH UK, 7455 ± 75 mg·L-1 KCl (0.1 M), 12.890 ± 0.129 mS·cm-1 at 25°C). 
Dynamic Viscosity Measurements 
The dynamic (shear) viscosity was measured using a rheometer (Discovery HR-2, TA 
instruments) at 25 °C. For the various LiTFSI in DOL electrolyte solutions, the viscosity was 
measured through flow sweep tests over shear rates of 20–150 rotation·s-1 as well as through 
peak hold tests at a fixed shear rate of 10 rotation·s-1. Each test was performed using 1 mL of 
electrolyte solution and repeated 3 times for reproducibility.
