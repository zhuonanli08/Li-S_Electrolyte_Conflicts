Figures S1-2 and S6-8 depicts the time sequences of the operando Raman spectra obtained 
for Li-S battery cells with different electrolytes (DME, TFEE and TFEME based). For each cell 
the set of spectra is shown as a heatmap, which is a graphical representation of spectra where 
intensities (heights) are colour coded, and as a stacked plot representation. All spectra have 
been normalized to the TFSI peak intensity at ca. 740 cm‒1 and when necessary the 
background has been subtracted for clarity. 
Figure S1. Operando Raman spectra of a Li-S battery cell upon cycling: a. Potential profile of the C/S 
composite cathode using a 1 M LiTFSI in DME:DOL electrolyte at C/20 rate with 16 µl/mg electrolyte to sulfur 
ratio. D and C are the discharge and charge processes, respectively, and b. A heatmap representation of the 
evolution of the corresponding Raman spectra. * = electrolyte (Fig. S3).
