1. Experiment section 
1.1 Preparation of electrolyte 
Two types of electrolytes were prepared in an argon (Ar)-filled glovebox as following: 1 mmol lithium 
bis(trifluoromethanesulfonyl)imide (LiTFSI) in 1 mL dioxolane (DOL)/dimethoxyethane (DME) (1:1, in volume) 
mixed solvents with 2 wt.% lithium nitrate (LiNO3) was chosen as blank electrolyte, denoted as DOL/DME 
electrolyte. Typically, 1 mmol LiTFSI in 1 mL DOL/DME/hexamethylphosphoramide (HMPA) (1:1:1, in volume) 
mixed solvents with 0.5 mmol LiNO3 was denoted as DOL/DME/HMPA electrolyte. The solvents were mixed in the 
specified volume ratio before salts were added.  
1.2 Solubility, ultraviolet-visible light (UV-vis), Raman and electron paramagnetic resonance (EPR) 
measurements 
Both sulfur and lithium sulfide (Li2S) were added in either DOL/DME (1:1, in volume) or DOL/DME/HMPA 
(1:1:1, in volume) in stoichiometric molar ratio of 1:1 (Li2S2), 3:1 (Li2S4), 5:1 (Li2S6), and 7:1 (Li2S8). Polysulfides 
solutions with certain concentrations of 20 mmol or 2 mmol were obtained by stirring for 24 h at 60 °C. The 2 mmol 
polysulfides solutions was sealed within a quartz glass cuvette in Ar-filling glove box and then measured in UV2700 
spectrometer (Shimadzu). The 2 mmol polysulfides solutions were further diluted 10 times and sealed in a quartz 
glass cuvette in Ar-filling glove box for Raman measurement (Ocean optics QE65 Pro) with a laser excitation 
wavelength of 532 nm. The solution of 0.2 mmol LiPSs (i.e., Li2S8, Li2S6, Li2S4, and Li2S2) in DOL/DME/HMPA 
(about 40 μL) was loaded into capillary tubes (1 mm outer diameter) for EPR spectrometer (JES-FA200). One end 
of capillary tube was heat sealed, the other end was sealed using plasticine and Parafilm in turn. 
1.3 Assembly and measurements of Li||CC/Li2S8 coin cells. 
In this work, all Li foil with silicane-based artificial solid−electrolyte interphase (SEI) was prepared according to 
the reported work as following: Li foil was immersed in a mixed solution of triethoxy(3,3,3-trifluoropropyl)silane

and bis[(3-methyldimethoxysilyl)propyl]polypropylene oxide (1:1 in volume) in tetrahydrofuran (THF) for 4 h, after 
which the residual solution was wiped off. Li||CC/Li2S8 coin cells with DOL/DME or DOL/DME/HMPA electrolytes 
were assembled using carbon cloth (CC) as the cathode current collector, Li foil with artificial SEI as the anode, PP 
separator, and 30 uL electrolyte. CC was cut to disks with a diameter of 12 mm, washed with ethanol, and completely 
dried before using. The CC/Li2S8 cathode was prepared by dripping a certain concentration of Li2S8 in DME on CC 
and dried at 60°C for 12 h. The active material in each cell was equivalent to 1.5 mg based on S. 
Galvanostatic charge−discharge (GCD) cycle was conducted using a LAND battery system at various areal current 
densities ranging from 1.7 V to 2.7 V (including 2.8 V, 2.9 V, or 3.0 V). In the galvanostatic intermittent titration 
technique (GITT) test, the cell was held at open circuit for 10 h after 100 mAh gsulfur-1 of both discharge and charge.

at 2.14 V at the current density of 0.1 C, and then kept at constant potential (2.04 V) for 200,000 s. Four classical

1.4 In-situ Raman spectroscopy of Li||CC asymmetrical cell with the Li2S8-containing electrolyte.  
Li||CC asymmetrical cell was assembled using CC (a size of 10×10 mm) as the cathode current collector and Li 
foil with artificial SEI as the anode. This assembly was sealed in a quartz glass cuvette filling with DOL/DME/HMPA 
electrolyte containing 0.1 mmol Li2S8.  
1.5 Assembly and measurements of Li||S coin cells. 
The CNT/sulfur composite was prepared by blending CNT and sulfur in a weight ratio of 3:7 and heated the 
mixture at 155°C for 12 h. Then, CNT/sulfur cathodes were prepared by CNT/sulfur, conductive agent, and LA133 
in a mass ratio of 8:1:1. The resulting slurry was cast on the carbon-coating aluminum-foil, dried at 60°C, and cut 
into 12 mm disks. The S content in CNT/sulfur cathode was about 56%. The GCD cycle of Li||S coin cells was same 
as Li||CC/Li2S8 coin cells 
2. Materials characterization 
The cycled CC cathodes in Li||CC/Li2S8 cells were disassembled in Ar-filling glove box, washed with pure DME, 
dried in glove box, and subsequently characterized by a scanning electron microscopy (SEM, MAIA3, TESCAN 
Brno) and X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS, Axis Supra, Shimazu).
