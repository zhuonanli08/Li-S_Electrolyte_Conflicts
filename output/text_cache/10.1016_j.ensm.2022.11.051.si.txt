Experimental Section/Methods
Preparation of SeS2@KB compositions and SeS2 cathodes: SeS2@KB compositions were prepared by a melting-diffusion method. The sublimed sulfur powder (99.5% purity, Aladdin), selenium powder (99.99% purity, Adamas reagent, ltd.), and Ketjenblack (ECP-600JD, Lion Specialty Chemicals) were first mixed at a mass ratio of 11 : 9 : 12 by grinding for 1h. Then, the composition was transferred into a sealed glass bottle and heated to 260°C for 6h at 5 °C min−1 at argon atmosphere and the SeS2@KB composition with a content of 62.5 wt% of SeS2 was collected after the powder was cooled to room temperature. With the same method, the S@KB powder with a content of 62.5 wt% of S was also prepared. The SeS2 cathodes composed of 80 wt% SeS2@KB powders (62.5 wt% SeS2), 12 wt% Super P carbon (DodoChem), and 8 wt% aqueous polyacrylic latex binder (LA133, Chengdu Indigo Power Sources Co., Ltd., Chengdu, China) were blended into an automatic mixer (AR-100, THINKY). The slurry obtained was blade-cast onto a carbon-coated aluminum foil and dried at 60 °C for 12 h. SeS2 cathodes with an active material loading of 0.6 to 0.8 mg cm−2 were obtained. The high loading SeS2 cathodes is composed of 90 wt% SeS2@KB powders (80 wt% SeS2) and 10 wt% LA133.
Preparation of electrolytes: The ANx electrolytes were prepared by solving lithium bis(trifluoromethane sulfonyl)imide (LiTFSI, DodoChem) in acetonitrile (AN, 99.9%, Adamas reagent, ltd.) with a molar ratio of x and 1,1,2,2-tetrafluoroethyl 2,2,3,3-tetrafluoropropyl ether (HFE, Adamas reagent, ltd.) , and the volume ratio of the ANx to HFE is 1 : 1. The above procedure was done in a argon-filled glove box. The DOL/ DME electrolytes were synthesized by solving 1.0 M LiTFSI in dioxolane (DOL, Adamas reagent, ltd.) and dimethoxyethane (DME, Adamas reagent, ltd.) with a volume ratio of 1: 1.
Characterizations: The Raman spectra (HORIBA, LabRAM HR Evolution) of AN2 and AN3 electrolyte were performed by sealing the solutions in capillary tubes in the range of 100-4000 cm−1. The conductivity of the electrolytes was measured with a conductivity tester (FE38, METTLER TOLEDO) and a matched platinum electrode (Inlab 710, METTLER TOLEDO) at 25 °C. The contact angles between the electrolytes and the separator/electrode were measured using a contact angle measuring instrument (JC2000D1). The photos taken 1/12 seconds after each droplet fell onto the separator/electrode surface were used to measure the contact angles. X-ray diffraction (XRD) on a multipurpose X-ray diffractometer (SmartLab SE, Rigaku) with a scanning speed of 15º min−1 in the range of 10 to 80º. The content of Se-S in the SeS2@KB composites was determined by thermogravimetric (TG) analyses on a STA 8000 (Perkin Elmer) from 30 to 800 °C at a speed at 5 °C min−1 under a nitrogen atmosphere. The UV quartz cuvette cells were assembled in an argon filled glovebox and tested in a Lambda 35 UV-vis spectrometer with the transparent window facing to the UV light in the wavelength range of 200 to 800 nm. The cycled lithium electrodes were disassembled from the cycled cells and washed by DME solvents to remove residual electrolytes and then dried in the vacuum. The morphologies of fresh and cycled lithium electrodes were characterized by using a field emission scanning electron microscope (SEM, JEOL, JSM-7610FPLUS). X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS, ESCALAB 250Xi) of the cycled lithium electrodes was carried out using Al Kα (1,486.7 eV) source.
Electrochemical Measurements: The electrochemical test of Li-SeS2 batteries was carried out in 2025-type coin cells at 30 °C. 40 μL electrolyte was added for each cell. The coin cells were assembled with the SeS2@KB cathodes and lithium metal anodes (15.6 mm in diameter, 450 µm in thickness, China Energy Lithium Co., Ltd., Tianjin China) separated by polypropylene (PP) separators (Celgard 2500) in an Argon-filled glove box. Galvanostatic measurements were conducted in the voltage range of 1 to 3 V vs. Li/Li+ at a current density of 0.1 mA cm−2 on a Neware Battery test station (CT-4008T) and all the specific capacity values were calculated based on the weight of SeS2 in the cathodes. Electrochemical impedance spectroscopy (EIS) analyses were carried out in the frequency range of 100 kHz to 0.01 Hz with an amplitude of 10 mV at the open-circuit voltage. Cyclic voltammetry (CV) was performed at a scan rate of 0.1 mV s−1. CV and EIS were performed on an electrochemical station (VMP3, BioLogic). For the analysis of the dissolution of the intermediates, the assembled cells were firstly discharged to 2.11 V at 0.1 C, and subsequently potentiostatically discharged at 2.10 V for the transformation of active materials into soluble intermediates. The potentiostatic discharge was stopped when the discharge current was below 10−5 A. 
Fabrication of pouch cell: The well-mixed slurry was single-side coated on Al foil and dried at 60°C for 12 h. Then, 4×5 cm-2 electrodes with a SeS2 loading of around 20 mg. The Li anode sheets were also cut with the same size as sulfur cathodes. The thickness of the Li anode was 100 μm on Cu current collectors. A Celgard 2500 membrane was used as the separator. The composition of the electrolyte was the same as the coin cells, and the electrolyte/active material ratio (E/A) is around 25 µL mgSeS2-1. The cells were galvanostatically cycled between 1–3 V on the NEWARE CT-9040 battery test system.
