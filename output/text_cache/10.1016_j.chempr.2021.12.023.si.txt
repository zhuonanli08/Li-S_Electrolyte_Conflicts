S6 
 
 
Figure S6. Optical images of the color change of the DOL/DME with 3.0 mM Li2S8, 
in which Li foils were pretreated by DOL/DME (left) and DIPS-EPSE (right) for 50 h, 
respectively. The scale bar is 1.0 cm. 
Fresh Li foils were immersed in DIPS-EPSE or DOL/DME for 50 h, respectively. 
The pretreated Li foils in the two electrolytes were then both transferred into 
DOL/DME electrolyte containing 3.0 mM Li2S8. The Li2S8 solution is initially 
brownish-red which is related to the concentration of Li2S8. After immersing 18 h, 
both the two solutions fade to light yellow, indicating that the SEI formed in DIPS-
EPSE cannot block the parasitic reactions of LiPSs on Li metal anodes. 
 
 

<<PAGE 8>>
 
S7 
 
 
Figure S7. (A) Electrochemical performance of Li | Li symmetric batteries with 
DOL/DME and DIPS-EPSE. (B) Electrochemical performance of Li | S batteries with 
the pre-cycled Li metal anode in DOL/DME and DIPS-EPSE. 
To further verify the function of SEI induced by DIPS-EPSE, Li | Li cells were 
firstly cycled in DIPS-EPSE or DOL/DME at 1.0 mA cm−2 and 1.0 mAh cm−2 for 5 
cycles. Then, the pre-cycled Li metal anode was disassembled and matched the S 
cathode to construct a new Li–S battery with DOL/DME electrolyte. The Li–S battery 
employed with pre-cycled Li in DIPS-EPSE is overcharged at 0.1 C, similar to that in 
DOL/DME, demonstrating that the electrochemically formed SEI in DIPS-EPSE 
cannot inhibit the parasitic reactions of LiPSs.
