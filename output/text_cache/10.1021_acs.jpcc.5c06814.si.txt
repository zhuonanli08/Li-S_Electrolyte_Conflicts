S3. Supporting Method 
S3.1. Region-dependent mean square displacement 
In a previously study we proposed a method for calculating the region(R)-dependent mean 
square displacement (MSDR)1. In this study, we calculated the MSDR in the following manner 
by analyzing trajectory segments, as schematically illustrated in Figure 10 in main text. Using 
Voronoi polyhedral analysis, we defined the clusters to which Li ions belong. Li ions that are 
part of the microphase are assigned a state of 0, whereas those belonging to HFE-rich phase 
are assigned a state of 1. The region of the microphase is denoted as V0, and that corresponding 
to state 1 is denoted as V1. When a Li ion returns to V0, we start (or restart) collecting a new 
segment of its trajectory, and we continue to collect trajectory data until the Li ion exits V0. 
This sampling procedure is repeated throughout all MD simulation trajectories. Suppose that 
we collected Nseg trajectory segments; then, the MSDR can be calculated using equation,
