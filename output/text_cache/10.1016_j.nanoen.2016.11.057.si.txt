Figure S1. 1H NMR spectra of conventional electrolyte with added DMTS (10 mg DMTS in 2.5 mL electrolyte) discharged with a sulfur-free OMC cathode, showing the disappear of DMTS peak (~2.50 ppm) and formation of a new peak at 2.34 ppm ascribed to dissolved CH3SSLi, similar to the position of DMDS, at 50% depths of discharge (DOD). The intensity of this new peak does not change much even at the end of discharge, indicating the saturation and low solubility of CH3SSLi in the conventional electrolyte. Although CH3SLi is slightly soluble in the 50 vol% DMDS-containing electrolyte and shows a singlet peak at 1.93 pm in Figure S1 and Figure 3d, it is not observed here, suggesting that CH3SLi is insoluble in conventional electrolyte.
