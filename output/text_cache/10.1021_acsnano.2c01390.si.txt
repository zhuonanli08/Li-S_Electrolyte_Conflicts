Figure S9 The cycling performance of Li-S batteries with different amount of TFP at 
0.2 C. The capacity is calculated by using the mass of both sulfur and TFP. It 
indicated that the addition of 50 wt% of TFP resulted in lower capacity and 10 wt% of 
TFP (relative to the mass of sulfur, i.e. 5.6% relative to the total amount of electrode 
materials) had the higher capacity. However, it should be noted that the addition of 50 
wt% of TFP (23% relative to the total amount of electrode materials) resulted in high 
stability and higher CE.
