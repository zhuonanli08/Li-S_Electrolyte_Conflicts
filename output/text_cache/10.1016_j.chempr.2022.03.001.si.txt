1. Small-angle scattering measurements of the cell components 
 
Figure S1. Small-angle neutron scattering measurements of the cell components on the absolute scale. The 
thickness and packing density should be considered when comparing the contributions from the 
components. The total thickness of the Al, Be, C-coated Al, Kapton, Li, films and the separator are 40, 500, 
20, 50, 125 µm, respectively. The Ketjenblack powder, KB, was measured in a quartz cuvette with a packing 
density of 0.0105 g cm-3, which is quite different from the packing state in the S/C composite electrodes.

Figure S2. Azimuthally averaged small-angle scattering data from the first charge of the cells SANS-H (left) 
and SAXS (right). The intensity (I) is plotted against the momentum transfer (q).

3. Variation of the scattering length densities of the main components of the positive 
electrodes as a function of the state of charge 
 
 
Figure S3. Schematic diagram of scattering length density (SLD) of the main components of the positive 
electrode with neutron (left) and X-ray (λ = 1.54 Å, right) at various states of discharge (SoD). The voltage 
curve (E) of the second cycle of the SANS-D and SAXS cells are also plotted in light brown as a reference. 
The value for the electrolyte on the lower plateau is estimated with the density measurement of the Li2S6 
solution in the respective electrolyte according to the electrolyte/sulfur ratio specified in Table 1. The dashed 
lines for the electrolyte near the end of discharge show the scattering length density inferred from the fitting 
results of the “larger spheres” in Figure 3 and 4. The scattering length density of the hydrogenous electrolyte 
only changes slightly, so the variation is not presented on the left plot. 
4. Expanded plot of the data in the higher q-range shown in Figure 2B 
 
Figure S4. The intensity difference, ∆I, between the data from selected states of discharge in the first cycle 
and the first measurement in the SAXS cell is plotted against the momentum transfer, q. Note that the y-
axis is linear. The plot is expanded of the higher q-range in Figure 2B.

7. Comparison between the particle radius obtained from SAXS and WAXS 
 
Figure S7. Comparison of the radius of the smaller sphere from the SAXS results and the radius calculated 
from the Scherrer equation with a shape factor of 0.9 and the full-width at half-maxima of the 111 peaks of 
Li2S in WAXS data. As explained in the main text, differences are expected because effects of polydispersity 
are not included in the Scherrer equation analysis.

8. Comparison between operando and commercial coin cells 
 
Figure S8. The potential, E, internal resistance, R, and diffusion resistance coefficient, k, of the positive 
electrode of the in operando cells (SANS-D, SANS-H and SAXS) compared to those of the commercial coin 
cells with DME:DOL (DME:DOL CC) and DME:THF (DME:THF CC) as the electrolyte solvent. The sulfur 
loadings in the cells DME:DOL CC and DME:THF CC are 3.47 and 3.82 mg cm-2. Note that the SANS-D 
and SANS-H cells switched to C/5 from the 3rd cycle. 
 
Figure S9. The specific discharge capacity, Qd, and Coulombic efficiency, Qd/Qc, where Qc is the specific 
charge capacity, of the operando cells (SANS-D, SANS-H and SAXS) compared to those of the commercial 
coin cells with DME:DOL (DME:DOL CC) and DME:THF (DME:THF CC) as the electrolyte solvent. Note 
that the SANS-D and SANS-H cells switched to C/5 from the 3rd cycle.
