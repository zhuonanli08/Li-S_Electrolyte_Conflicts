Figure S7. FT-IR spectra of Li anode harvested after 100 cycles from Li-S cells with 1.0 M 
LiTFSI DOL/DME, baseline electrolyte DOL/DME + 0.1 M LiNO3, and baseline + 3wt%, 
10wt%, and 20wt% HFB, respectively.
FT-IR spectra of Li anodes harvested from cells that were cycled in the presence of various HFB 
percentages was shown in Figure S7. C-H stretching peaks between 3000 to 2800 cm-1 were 
observed for lithium anode samples cycled with DOL/DME electrolyte and the high HFB 
concentration electrolytes. The lack of C-H stretching for the low HFB cells indicates that the 
SEI layer is composed primarily of inorganic components or fully fluorinated species. 
The characteristic peaks in the range of 1600 to 1400 cm-1 is typically originated from the SEI 
component from decomposition product of DOL. However, when HFB was added, this broad 
peak became smaller or disappeared, indicating HFB might participate the new SEI formation 
process. The intensity of peak at 1200 cm-1 also has the similar tendency. Additionally, the peaks 
located at 1370 and 1200 cm-1 might be related to the byproducts from the reaction of HFB and 
LiNO3 because both 15wt% and 20wt% HFB cells suffered from rapid capacity fading than the 
baseline cell.
