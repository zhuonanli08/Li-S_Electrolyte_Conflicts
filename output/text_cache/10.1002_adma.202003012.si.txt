I. Experimental Section 
1.1. Raw materials 
Thick lithium (Li) foils (600 μm) and ultrathin Li foils (50 μm) were purchased 
from China Energy Lithium Co., Ltd. 1,3-diamethoxyethane (DOL) and 1,2-
dimethoxyethane 
(DME) 
were 
purchased 
from 
DoDoChem. 
Lithium 
bis(trifluoromethanesulfony)imide (LiTFSI) and 3,5-bis(trifluoromethyl)thiophenol 
(BTB) were purchased from TCI (Shanghai) Development Co., Ltd. Lithium nitrate 
(LiNO3), lithium sulfide (Li2S), and sulfur (S) were purchased from Alpha Aesar. All 
raw materials were used without further purification. 
 
1.2. Materials characterizations 
The Li anode disassembled from Li–S batteries after cycles was cleaned by DME 
for three times to guarantee no residual electrolyte and then dried in glove box. During 
transferring process, the samples were placed in an argon (Ar)-filled container to avert 
contacting with air before characterizations.  
The morphologies of Li metal were characterized by a JSM 7401F (JEOL Ltd., 
Tokyo, Japan) scanning electron microscopy (SEM) operated at 3.0 kV. 
X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) experiments were carried out on 
scanning X-ray microprobe (Quantera SXM, ULVAC-PHI. INC) operated at 250 kV, 
with monochromated Al Kα radiation. Ar+ sputtering rate for the XPS depth-profiling 
calibrated on SiO2 surface was ~20 nm min–1. The sputtering time is the time of Ar+ 
sputtering on SEI to investigate the components at the different depths in SEI. 
 
1.3. Electrolyte and polysulfide preparation 
1.0 M LiTFSI dissolved in DOL/DME (v/v=1:1) with 2% LiNO3 was used as the 
blank electrolyte. 80 mM BTB was added into the blank electrolyte. The mixture of S 
and Li2S with a molar ratio of 1:7 was added into the blank or BTB electrolyte to 
prepare the Li2S8 electrolyte.

1.4. SEI preformation and visualized test 
For the SEI preformation of Li foils, electrolyte with 0. 4 M [S] Li2S8 was used 
(no LiNO3). Thick Li foil was soaked into 0.2 mL Li2S8 electrolyte with or without BTB 
(80 mM) for 1 hour. Then, the obtained Li foil was washed by DME for three times to 
remove residual electrolyte and dried in glove box. 
For the visualized test, the obtained Li foils after SEI preformation with different 
electrolytes were soaked into 4.0 mL electrolyte (no LiNO3 additive) with a Li2S8 
concentration of 0.6 mM (5 mM [S]) in sealed bottles. Then, the change of color of the 
bottle was recorded for 16 hours. 
 
1.5. Cell assembly and electrochemical performance 
All cells were assembled in 2025-type coin cells and tested on a LAND 
multichannel battery cycler at 25℃. For Li–S cells, the carbon/sulfur composite (30 
wt.% multi-walled carbon nanotubes and 70 wt.% S) was ball-milled with 
polyvinylidene fluoride at a weight ratio of 9:1 in NMP solvent, and a slurry was 
obtained. The slurry was casted onto Al foil and dried for 12 h at 60°C. The coated Al 
foil was cut into round disks with a diameter of 13 mm, and the S cathodes with areal 
sulfur loading of 4.5 mg cm−2 were prepared. PP separator was used. The amount of 
electrolyte was controlled with an electrolyte/sulfur ratio of 5.0 μL mgS−1 precisely. The 
galvanostatic cycling of cells was between 1.7–2.6 V. Symmetric Li–Li cells (ultrathin 
Li foils as electrodes) were tested at 2.0 mA cm2 and a capacity of 2.0 mAh cm2 with 
40.0 μL electrolyte composed of 0.25 M (2.0 M [S]) Li2S8 (with 2% LiNO3 additive). 
Electrochemical impedance spectroscopy tests were conducted on a Solartron 1470E 
electrochemical workstation with a frequency range from 0.1 Hz to 10 kHz and a 
voltage amplitude of 5 mV.

Figure S2. The stability of symmetric Li–Li batteries with 50 µm-thick bare or 
pretreated Li foils in the presence of 2.0 M [S] LiPSs in electrolyte (without LiNO3 
additive) at a current density of 1.0 mA cm−2 and a capacity of 1.0 mAh cm−2. 
The pretreatment is as same as the procedure of SEI preformation in visualized test. 
Symmetric Li–Li batteries assembled by pretreated Li metal (with an organosulfur-
containing SEI) or bare Li metal (with a routine SEI) were tested at a current density of 
1.0 mA cm2 and a capacity of 1.0 mAh cm2 with 40.0 μL electrolyte composed of 
0.25 M (2.0 M [S]) Li2S8 (without LiNO3 additive). The pretreated Li delivers a stable 
polarization voltage of about 20 mV for more than 80 hours. The polarization voltage 
of bare Li keeps around 20 mV during the first 40 hours, then the polarization voltage 
increases to over 50 mV rapidly till 80 hours. Because the organosulfur-containing 
component can suppress the side reactions between Li and LiPSs, the utilization of Li 
deposition improves and the internal resistance decreases even without LiNO3 additive.

Figure S4. The cycling performance of Li–S batteries under practical and mild 
conditions with a routine SEI. The practical conditions include a high loading sulfur 
cathode (4.5 mgS cm−2), an ultrathin Li anode (50 μm), and a low E/S ratio (5.0 µL 
mgS−1) at 0.1 C. The mild conditions employed in most previous reports include a low 
loading sulfur cathode (1.0 mgS cm−2), a thick Li anode (600 μm), and a high E/S ratio 
(20.0 µL mgS−1) at 0.5 C.  
The large difference between practical and mild conditions illustrates that the 
cycling performance of Li–S batteries changes significantly when the evaluation 
conditions approach to practical conditions. The practical evaluation conditions are 
advocated to further promote the development of Li–S batteries.
