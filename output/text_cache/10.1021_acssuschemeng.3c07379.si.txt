Experimental 
Preparation of cathode
CNT and sulfur were mixed with a weight ratio of 2.5:7.5 and ground evenly in a 
mortar. The mixture was sealed in a glass tube and then placed inside a tubular furnace. 
It was heated at a rate of 1 ℃ per minute under air atmosphere, reaching a temperature 
of 155 °C and maintained for 12 hours. The CNT/S compound was obtained by cooling 
it to room temperature. Cathode slurry was prepared by mixing CNT/S compound,

Preparation of electrolyte
Figure.S1 presents the schematic diagram for the synthesis of the electrolytes with 
1% LiNO3 & 2% Y(NO3)3. To obtain anhydrous Y(NO3)3, the Y(NO3)3·6H2O 
compound was subjected to freeze-drying, which involved removing the bound water. 
1.0 M lithium bis (trifluoromethylsulfone) imide (LiTFSI) was used as the standard 
electrolyte in the mixture of 1,3-dioxolane (DOL) and dimethoxymethane (DME) with 
the volume ratio of 1∶1. For the preparation of the electrolyte, Y(NO3)3 and LiNO3 
were accurately weighed at 20.03 mg and 10.02 mg, respectively. These amounts were 
then added to 2 ml of the standard electrolyte solution. After magnetic stirring for 6 
hours, a 1% LiNO3-2% Y(NO3)3 electrolyte was obtained.
 Similarly, by following the same process, a 2% Y(NO3)3 electrolyte and a 1% 
LiNO3 electrolyte could be obtained. 1% LiNO3 was also called basic electrolyte. 
Electrochemical Measurements
Galvanostatic charge–discharge tests were carried out within the cutoff voltages 
of 1.7−2.8 V, using LAND CT2001A cell test instruments (LANHE, China). 
Electrochemical impedance spectroscopy (EIS) was measured in the frequency range 
from 0.01 Hz to 100 kHz at 5 mV s-1 on a Chenhua CHI-604E electrochemical

Characterization
The microstructure of the lithium anode surface after cycling was observed by 
SEM (EM-30-Plus COXEM Korea). The elemental mapping of composition was 
applied by energy-dispersive spectroscopy EDS (Ultim Max Oxford English). The 
bonding energy of the lithium anode surface was analyzed by X-ray photoelectron 
spectroscopy (XPS, Thermo-Fisher-Scientific, USA). The composition was phased by 
X-ray diffraction XRD (Bruker-AXS D8 Advance, Germany). 
Theoretical Calculations
All density functional theory (DFT) calculations were performed by the Vienna 
Ab-initio Simulation Package (VASP) with the projector augmented wave (PAW) 
method. The plane wave energy cut-off was set to 500 eV, and the process was carried 
out until the EDIFF was less than 10−7 and EDIFFG was less than −0.01. The charge 
transfer between molecules was analyzed through Bader charge analysis. HOMO-
LUMO energy bandgap (Eg) was calculated as follows
