S1. Benchmark Calculation
In order to validate the use of the BMK density functional1 and the cc-pVTZ basis set,2-3 
we also compared reduction potentials of several electrolyte molecular systems from other DFT 
functionals, including B3LYP,4-5 B3PW91,4, 6 and M06-2X7 together with cc-pVDZ, aug-cc-
pVDZ, and aug-cc-pVTZ basis sets.2-3, 8 Reduction potentials obtained from the CBS-QB39 
calculations were considered reference values, and the root mean squared error (RMSE) from the 
CBS-QB3 potentials were calculated. In this benchmark test, DME_ttt, DME_tgt, DOL, FSI-, 
TFSI-, FEC, BOB-, and DFOB- were selected from our system together with carbonate 
electrolyte molecules, including ethylene carbonate (EC), propylene carbonate (PC), dimethyl 
carbonate (DMC), diethyl carbonate (DEC) and vinylene carbonate (VC). Because we are 
interested in reduction potentials of ionic complexes in this study, the electrolyte/Li+ complex, 
which is the simplest ionic complex, was employed for reduction potential calculations in this 
benchmark. 
The calculated reduction potentials from DFT functionals and basis sets are tabulated in 
Table S1, and the reduction potentials from CBS-QB3 method are also shown for comparison. 
Overall, B3LYP produces the worst RMSE of over 0.30 V and the worst 0.51 V, compared to 
other density functional methods. As expected, the triple  basis sets, cc-pVTZ and aug-cc-
pVTZ, have lower RMSEs than the double  basis sets, cc-pVDZ and aug-cc-pVDZ. However, 
the augmented basis sets do not outperform the non-augmented basis sets except the RMSEs 
between BMK/cc-pVDZ and BMK/aug-cc-pVDZ in Table S1. Even B3LYP/aug-cc-pVDZ 
yields the largest RMSE with 0.51 V in the benchmark calculation. M06-2X and BMK 
functionals produces comparable RMSEs from CBS-QB3 reduction potentials. The combination 
of the BMK DFT method and the cc-pVTZ basis set, however, provides the smallest RMSE of 
0.12 V. From this result, the BMK/c-pVTZ theoretical method is our tool to calculate reduction 
potentials of our electrolyte systems.
