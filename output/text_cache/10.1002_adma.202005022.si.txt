Experimental section 
Materials. Elemental sulfur, 1,3-dioxolane (DOL), 1,2-dimethoxyethane (DME), dimethyl 
sulfoxide (DMSO), N,N-dimethyl acetamide (DMAc), N,N-dimethyl formamide (DMF), 1,3-
dimethyl-2-imidazolidinone (DMI), poly(vinylidene fluoride) (PVDF, Mw = 560000), lithium 
bis(trifluoromethane)sulfonamide (LiTFSI), ethylene carbonate (EC), dimethyl carbonate 
(DMC), and lithium nitrate (LiNO3) were purchased from Sigma-Aldrich, USA. N-methyl-2-
pyrrolidone (NMP) was purchased from Junsei Chemical, Japan. All chemicals were used 
without further purification. 
UV–Vis Spectrophotometry: The stoichiometric amounts of elemental sulfur and Li2S were 
added into individual solvents to prepare the Li2S6 solutions with 0.1 M. These solutions were 
diluted to 1 mM for UV-Vis spectroscopy analysis. The UV quartz cuvettes were charged 
with the prepared solutions in an argon filled glovebox. The sealed samples were analyzed 
on a Lambda 35 UV/vis spectrometer in the wavelength range of 400–700 nm. 
Electrochemical characterization. The electrochemical performance was evaluated by 
fabricating CR2032-type coin cells. The carbon-sulfur composite was prepared by blending 
activated carbon (C-Novel) and elemental sulfur in a weight ratio of 3:7 and firing the blend 
at 155 °C for 12 h. Cathodes were prepared by dispersing the carbon-sulfur composite with 
conductive agent (Super C) and PVDF in NMP in a mass ratio of 7:2:1. The as-prepared 
slurry was cast onto aluminum foil (20 μm, Hohsen, Japan) using the doctor-blade technique. 
The electrodes were then dried in a convection oven at 60 °C for 12 h. The areal sulfur 
loading was fixed at 1 mgsulfur cm−2. Coin cells were fabricated in an Ar-filled glove box by 
pairing with Li metal discs as both counter and reference electrodes. A polypropylene 
membrane (Celgard 2400) was used as a separator, and solvents (mixture of DOL/DME in 
1:1 (v/v), DMF, DMAc, and DMI, respectively) containing LiTFSI with and without LiNO3 
additive were used as the electrolytes. For the cells based on DMSO, a PE membrane (SK

stripped at a current density of 0.2 mA cm−2 until the voltage reached 1.0 V. All 
electrochemical characterizations were conducted using a WBCS 3000 battery cycler 
(Wonatech, South Korea) and PEBC050.01 (PNE Solution, South Korea). Galvanostatic 
measurements were carried out in the voltage range of 1.7−2.7 V vs. Li/Li+, and EIS analyses 
were conducted in the frequency range of 1 MHz−0.1 Hz with an amplitude of 10 mV at 
open-circuit voltage. Linear sweep voltammetry (LSV) was carried out using an aluminum 
current collector with a scan rate of 0.05 mV s−1. Cyclic voltammetry (CV) was also 
conducted at a scan rate of 0.05 mV s−1. The specific capacities and current densities from all 
the electrochemical measurements were calculated based on the weight of sulfur only. 
Experiments with the high loading electrodes (5 mgsulfur cm–2), presented in Figure S16, were 
conducted by using lithium polysulfide catholyte infiltrated in a carbon paper as a cathode. 
The catholyte was prepared by blending 0.78 M Li2S8, 1 M LiTFSI, and 0.5 M LiNO3 in DMI 
and the electrolyte was 1 M LiTFSI and 0.5 M LiNO3 in DMI. 
Physicochemical characterization. UV-vis spectra were recorded on a Lambda 35 UV/vis 
spectrometer (Perkin Elmer Instruments, U. S. A.). Field-emission scanning electron 
microscopy (FE-SEM, JSM-7600F, JEOL, Japan) was used to obtain images of the 
morphologies of electrodes. To capture the SEM images of Li2S, VGCF was used as a 
conductive agent for the cathode and 30 μL catholyte comprising 0.08 M Li2S6, 1 M LiTFSI, 
and 0.2 M LiNO3 in DOL/DME (v:v=1:1) or 0.08 M Li2S6, 1 M LiTFSI, and 0.5 M LiNO3 in
