1. Experimental
1.1 Materials
Lithium bis(trifluoromethanesulfonyl)imide (LiTFSI), 1,2-Dimethoxyethane (DME), 1,3-dioxolane (DOL) were bought from the Sigma-Aldrich Corporation. 1,1,2,2-Tetrafluoroethyl methyl ether (TME) was bought from the Aladdin Corporation. Li foil was purchased from the China Energy Li Corporation. Copper foil and aluminium foil was obtained from the HF-Kejing Corporation. For the 1M LiTFSI-DME/DOL electrolyte, 1.0 mmol of LiTFSI was dissolved in 0.5 mL DME and 0.5 mL DOL. For the 1 LiTFSI-3 DME/DOL electrolyte, 1.0 mmol of LiTFSI was dissolved in 1.5 mmol DME and 1.5 mmol DOL. For the 1 LiTFSI-3 DME/DOL-6TME electrolyte, additional 6.0 mmol TME was added to 1 LiTFSI-3 DME/DOL electrolyte. The amount of electrolyte was about 30 μL per battery in Li-S batteries and about 60 μL in other batteries. The cathode electrode was prepared via mixing S@KB (with 71.2% of sulfur content), Acetylene black (AB) and poly (vinylidene fluoride) (PVDF) in N–methylpyrrolidone (NMP) solution, with a mass ratio of 80:10:10. And then spread the obtained slurry onto a piece of aluminum current collector. The electrode was then dried under vacuum at 60°C for 24 h. The mass loading of S was about 0.5 mg cm−2. Polypropylene (Celgard, 2400) were used as the separators. Li/Li symmetrical battery was fabricated by using a Li foil as the working electrode, another Li foil as the counter electrode and reference electrode. Li-Cu batteries was fabricated by using a Li foil as anode, Cu foil as cathode. Assemble processes were all operated in an argon-filled glove box with oxygen and water contents below 1 ppm.
1.2 Electrochemical measurements
Li-Li batteries were used to evaluate the Li deposition and stripping characteristics. Li-SS batteries were used to evaluate the oxidative stability of the electrolytes. Li-Cu batteries were used to evaluate the CE of Li metal anode. The ratio of the charge capacity to the discharge capacity is the CE of the battery. The current density used in the Li-S full battery was 0.5 C. In the electrochemical impedance spectroscopy test, the frequency ranged from 1 MHz to 0.1 Hz and the amplitude was 10 mV. The anodic stability of the electrolyte was evaluated by Linear Sweep Voltammetry test, and the scan rate was 1.0 mV s−1.
1.3 Characterizations
The obtained electrodes were washed with DME before next analysis. Raman spectra were recorded on microscope spectrometer (NRS-1000DT) with 633 nm laser. The scanning electron microscopy (SEM) images were received from SU8010 (Hitachi). Argon ion etching-assisted X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) was conducted in a Thermo Fisher Scientific Model K-Alpha spectrometer equipped with Al Kα radiation (1486.6 eV).
