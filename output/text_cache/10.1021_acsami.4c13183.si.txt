3. Supporting Notes 
 
Supporting Note 1 
Discussion of EIS data during the first discharge: 
The Nyquist plot of the first discharge displays an initially large arc at low-frequencies around 20 Hz and 
below. As this high-impedance feature occurs specifically during the beginning of the first discharge, we 
speculate that its origin is related to dissolved polysulfides and CEI formation2,3. As the CEI forms, the low-
frequency arc becomes smaller, and the low-frequency trend changes its slope from a linear behavior 
(approximately a 45° slope) to a capacitive behavior (low-frequency trend close to a vertical line) at the end 
of the first discharge where the lithiation completes and Li2S forms4 (Figure S10c, FigureS11c).
