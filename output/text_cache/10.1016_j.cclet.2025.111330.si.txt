1. Experimental section
1.1. Raw materials
1,3-dioxolane (DOL, battery grade, DoDoChem), 1,2-dimethoxyethane (DME, battery grade, DoDoChem), lithium bis(trifluoromethanesulfony)imide (LiTFSI, battery grade, DoDoChem), lithium nitrate (LiNO3, metals basis, Aladdin), polypropylene (PP) separator (Celgard 2500), aluminum (Al) foil, lithium (Li) foil (50, 75, or 600 μm thick), elemental sulfur (S), lithium sulfide (Li2S), carbon nanotubes (CNTs), N-methyl-2-pyrrolidinone (NMP, Sinopharm), poly(vinylidene fluoride) (PVDF, HSV-300) were purchased from commercial sources and used without further purification. 1-methyl-1-propylpyrrolidinium bis(fluorosulfonyl)imide (Py13FSI, 99.5%) was purchased from DoDoChem and purified by molecular sieve for over 24 h. All the materials were stored in an argon-atmosphere glove box with H2O and O2 levels below 0.01 ppm.
1.2. Preparation of sulfur cathodes
S/C composite was prepared by blending S and CNTs at a 7:3 weight ratio. This composite was then ball-milled with a PVDF binder at a ratio of 9:1 (by weight) in NMP. The slurry was spread onto Al foil and dried at 60 °C for 12 h. The resulting S/C cathodes with 63% S content were cut into 13.0 mm diameter disks. The areal sulfur loadings were set at 0.95 and 4.1 mgS/cm2.
1.3. Preparation of electrolytes
The baseline electrolyte was obtained by dissolving 1.0 M LiTFSI and 2.0 wt% LiNO3 in DOL/DME (1:1, by vol.) mixed solvents (marked as DOL/DME electrolyte). For ionic liquid-containing electrolyte, Py13FSI was added to the baseline electrolyte at 2 and 5 vol%. To prepare the electrolyte with 1.0 mol/L [S] Li2S8, Li2S and S8 were mixed with a molar ratio of 8:7 in the above electrolytes. All the electrolytes were prepared in an argon-atmosphere glove box with H2O and O2 levels below 0.01 ppm.
1.4. Battery assembly and electrochemical measurement
Under mild conditions, Li–S batteries were assembled with Li metal anodes (600 μm), regular-loading S cathodes (0.95 mgS/cm2), and 25 μL electrolyte in the configuration of 2025-type coin cell. Under demanding conditions, an ultrathin Li anode (50 μm), a high-loading S cathode (4.1 mgS/cm2), and electrolyte (35 μL) were employed to assemble Li–S batteries in the configuration of 2032-type coin cell. For batteries under demanding conditions, cycle performance was tested between 1.7–2.6 V at 0.1 C (1 C = 1672 mA/g) after 2 activation cycles at 0.05 C. 
The rate performance of Li–S batteries was evaluated at 0.1 C, 0.2 C, 0.3 C, 0.4 C, and 0.5 C under demanding conditions. The batteries were cycled for 5 cycles at each rate after 2 activation cycles at 0.05 C.
Li | Li symmetric cells were assembled with 600 μm Li as electrodes, PP separator, and 50 μL electrolyte containing 1.0 mol/L[S] Li2S8 and tested at a current density of 1.0 mA/cm2 and an areal capacity of 1.0 mAh/cm2.
For the shuttle current test, Li–S batteries under mild conditions without LiNO3 were galvanostatically charged to 2.90 V and then discharged at the same current to various voltages (2.60, 2.45, 2.43, 2.40, 2.39, 2.38, 2.37, 2.36, 2.35, 2.34, 2.25, and 2.20 V) step by step, and then switched to a potentiostatic mode. The shuttle current was determined by the steady-state current at different potentiostatic voltages.
All the batteries were tested on a LAND multichannel battery cycler at 25 °C. 
1.5. Material characterizations
Cycled anodes from Li–S batteries were rinsed with DME three times to remove remaining electrolyte solution and then dried in a glove box with H2O and O2 content below 0.01 ppm. During the transfer process, the samples were stored in a container saturated with argon (Ar) to avoid any air exposure, ensuring their integrity for subsequent analysis. The morphologies of Li metal anodes were observed by a scanning electron microscope (SEM, FEI-Apreo C FEI Ltd., Hillsboro state, United States) operated at 3.0 kV. X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) experiments were carried out on Axis Supra+ (Kratos Analytical) using Ar+ gun (5 kV) to obtain the desired depth profile. The Ar+ sputtering rate was ~130 nm/min calibrated on Ta2O5 surface. The XPS profiles were recorded after Ar+ sputtered for 0 and 60 s. The elements tested by XPS include Li, C, N, O, S, and F. Raman spectra were recorded with a He–Ne laser excitation at 532 nm using a Horiba Jobin Yvon Lab-RAM HR800 Raman spectrometer.
1.6. Li–S pouch cell assembly and test 
A 7 × 4 cm2 Li–S pouch cell was fabricated in a dry room with a dew point below −40°C. Ultrathin Li foils (75 μm on a single side), high-sulfur-loading cathodes (8.1 mgS/cm2 on a single side), and a low electrolyte to sulfur (E/S) ratio (3.0 gele/gS) were employed. The cathodes and anodes (double-layer coated with active materials) were stacked layer-by-layer with PP separators. The pouch cell was tested at 0.05 C (1 C = 1000 mA/g) and cycling voltage range from 1.70 V to 2.55 V. The energy density was evaluated based on all of the cell components including core, electrolyte, and cell package.
