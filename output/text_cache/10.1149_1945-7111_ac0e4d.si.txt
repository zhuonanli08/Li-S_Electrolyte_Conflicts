Synthesis of CIPs 1
The process for industrial production of carbonyl iron powders is high-pressure gas-phase synthesis method, which is used in the production of BASF in Germany. The synthetic pressure is over 20 MPa, the reaction temperature is 150-200 ℃, and the reaction time is over 120 h, shown as Eq. (S1). Sponge iron or oxide scales, as synthetic raw materials, are ground into powder by ball and then reduced in the atmosphere of hydrogen gas. Put them into the synthesis reactor, and carbon monoxide gas is pressurized by a high pressure compressor, transformed into the reactor. At the same time, the reactor was heated to produce a synthetic reaction to produce carbonyl iron powders.

DFT calculation details
Spin-polarized calculations were performed by VASP. The PAW-PBE function was used to describe the ion−electron interaction and electron exchange−correlation. A cutoff energy of 500 eV was used for the plane-wave basis set. Grimme’s method (DFT-D3) was employed to account for the van der Waals’ interactions. For structural optimization, a convergence threshold of 0.02 eV Å−1 was set in force. The binding strength of the surface adsorbate was evaluated by the calculating of binding energy (EBE):
EBE = E*+EX − EX*                             (S2)
where *, X, and X* represent the substrate, adsorbate, and adsorption systems, respectively.


Synthesis of Li2S6 in DOL and DME
The synthesis of 0.6 M Li2S6 electrolyte was performed to a reported method 2. Firstly, lithium sulfide (Li2S2, 99.9%) and sulfur powder (S8, 99.9%) were mixed with a molar ratio of 4:3 in a bottle of glove box. Then, a mixed solvent (lithium-sulfur electrolyte, DOL and DME (1:1 vol) was dropped in the bottle. The above mixture was stirred at 50 °C for 12 h to make the Li2S and S8 react completely by magnetic stirring apparatus. Finally, the resultant mixture was centrifuged at 5000 rpm for 8 min and a red-brown Li2S6 was obtained.
