All molecular dynamics simulations were performed using the GROMACS software 
package. The equations of motion were integrated using the leap-frog algorithm with a 1 fs time 
step. All bonds were constrained using the LINCS algorithm (order 4, 1 iteration, with a warning 
angle of 30°). 
Non-bonded interactions were treated as follows: for electrostatics, the Particle Mesh Ewald 
(PME) method was employed with a real-space cutoff of 1.4 nm, Fourier grid spacing of 0.12 nm, 
PME interpolation order of 4, and a relative tolerance of 1×10⁻⁵. Van der Waals interactions were 
handled using a plain cutoff scheme with a distance of 1.4 nm, and both energy and pressure 
dispersion corrections were applied. 
Neighbor searching was conducted using a Verlet list cutoff scheme with a buffered list 
cutoff of 1.4 nm, updated every 40 steps, based on a grid search. Temperature was maintained at 
303 K using the Nose–Hoover thermostat with a time constant of 0.2 ps, coupling the entire system. 
Pressure was controlled at 1 bar using the Berendsen barostat (isotropic), with a time constant of 
2.0 ps and an isothermal compressibility of 4.5×10⁻⁵ bar⁻¹.
