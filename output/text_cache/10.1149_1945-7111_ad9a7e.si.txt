Figure S1. Charge–discharge curves of a Li–Li symmetrical cell containing G3 electrolyte. The blue curve shows the voltage profile during charging and discharging of the cell at a current density of 0.4 mA cm−2. The orange and green curves show the voltage profile during resting for 10 min after charging (Li deposition) or discharging (Li dissolution), respectively, followed by potential-controlled impedance measurements.


Figure S2. Nyquist plots (1 MHz – 100 or 1 mHz) of a Li–Li symmetrical cell with G3 electrolyte. 
(a) Potential-controlled impedance before and after each charging process. (b) Enlarged view of (a) after the 1st charge. (c) Enlarged view of (a) after the 6th charge. (d) Potentially controlled impedance before cycling and current-controlled impedance during each charging process. (e) Enlarged view of (d) during the 1st charge. (f) Enlarged view of (d) under the 6th charge. The measured impedance was divided by 2 to obtain the normalized impedance of a 1 cm2 Li metal electrode. The filled circles indicate the data points for each decade, and the red plot indicates the data points at 10 Hz. The Nyquist plots of the 10th charge were the result of a short circuit.



Figure S3. Nyquist plots (1 MHz – 1 mHz) of a Li–Li symmetrical cell with G3 electrolyte after the 5th charge from 25 to −25 °C and (inset) enlarged view of the plots from 25 to −5 °C. The measured impedance was divided by 2 to obtain the normalized impedance of a 1 cm2 Li metal electrode. The filled circles indicate the data points for each decade, and the red plot indicates the data point at 10 Hz.


Figure S4. Profiles of fitting parameters of a Li metal anode with G3 electrolyte. (a) charge transfer resistance (Rct); (b) enlarged profile from (a), (c) double layer capacitance (Cdl), (d) time constant of the charge transfer process (τct), (e) SEI2 resistance (RSEI2), (f) enlarged profile from (d), (g) capacitance of the SEI2 layer (CSEI2), (h) time constant of SEI2 (τSEI2), (i) SEI1 resistance (RSEI1), (j) capacitance of the SEI1 layer (CSEI1), and (k) time constant of SEI1 (τSEI1). The blue line indicates the cycle in which an internal short-circuit occurs. The orange plots indicate the results obtained before and during the charging. The green plots indicate the results obtained during the discharge.
