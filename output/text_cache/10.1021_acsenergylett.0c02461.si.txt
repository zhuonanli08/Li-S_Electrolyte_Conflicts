Experimental Methods:
Polysulfide Solution Preparation:
Both low concentration and high concentration polysulfide solution samples were prepared in a 
similar manner. Inside an argon filled glovebox, an appropriate amount of Li2S and sulfur 
powder stoichiometrically equivalent to either 0.1 M or 1 M Li2S6 were mixed in a 1:1 volume 
mixture of DOL and DME solvents. For the samples containing CH3TFA, a mass equivalent to 
0.2 M CH3TFA was then added and stirred until the reaction went to completion (typically 
within 1 minute). The low concentration solutions were subsequently used for UV-Vis 
experiments. 
Ultraviolet-Visible (UV-Vis) Spectroscopic Studies:
Polysulfide samples at a concentration of 0.1 M were prepared as described above. Then, 15 µL 
of each sample was placed in a 0.01 mm demountable short-path-length cuvette (White Bear 
Photonics, LLC) in an argon-filled glovebox. This served to seal the sample and ensured it 
conformed to the 0.01 mm path length. Each cuvette was then placed in a short-path-length cell 
holder (White Bear Photonics, LLC) and brought outside of the glovebox where it was scanned 
with a Cary 5000 UV-VIS NIR Spectrometer. A wavelength range of 200 to 800 nm was 
scanned in the single beam configuration, with baseline corrections performed beforehand.
1H and 13C-Nuclear Magnetic Resonance (NMR) Spectroscopy:
Samples were prepared in an argon filled glovebox consisting of either 0.2 M CH3TFA or 0.2 M 
CH3TFA and 0.1 M Li2S6 in D6-DMSO. 10 µL of each solvent was drawn from each vial and 
placed in an NMR tube with 700 µL of D6-DMSO. This sample was sealed in an argon filled 
glovebox and then brought out of the glovebox for characterization. Both 1H and 13C-NMR 
spectroscopy was performed via a Bruker Avance III 500 MHz NMR spectrometer. 32 scans of

Li-S Coin Cell Fabrication: 
Li-S cells were assembled in CR-2032 type coin cells, consisting of a sulfur cathode, lithium-
metal foil anode, electrolyte, and a Celgard 2325 separator. The sulfur cathodes had areal 
loadings of 3.2 mg cm-2 and E/S = 12 µL mg-1 or 4.6 mg cm-2 with E/S = 10 µL mg-1  for, 
respectively, the galvanostatic cycling and rate-capability tests. The slurry-cast sulfur cathode 
consisted of 67.5% sulfur, 23.5% carbon, and 9% PEO-PVP (4:1, by mass, dissolved in H2O) 
binder cast onto carbon-coated aluminum foil. 
Electrolyte preparation: 
Inside an argon-filled glovebox, three different electrolytes were prepared: 1.0 M LiTFSI and 0.2 
M LiNO3 in DOL/DME (1:1, by vol.); 0.8 M LiTFSI, 0.2 M LiNO3, and 0.2 M LiTFA in 
DOL/DME; or 0.8 M LiTFSI, 0.2 M LiNO3, and 0.2 M CH3TFA in DOL/DME. All electrolytes 
were stirred overnight prior to being used in cells.
Li-S Pouch Cell Fabrication: 
Soft-packaging pouch cells were constructed inside an argon-filled glovebox. Slurry-cast sulfur 
cathodes (same constituents as coin cell cathodes described above) with an area of 39 cm2 and 
areal sulfur loading of 4.8 mg cm-2 were used. Li-foil was attached to a Ni-foam to form the 
anode. The cathode and anode tabs were, respectively, welded on to the Al and Ni backings. The 
pouch cells were injected with 840 µL of electrolyte (E/S = 4.5 µL mg-1) before being sealed.

Electrochemical Cell Testing:
An Arbin battery cycler was used for all electrochemical studies. For the galvanostatic coin-cell 
cycling, a rate of C/10 was employed after 2 formation cycles at C/20 rate, with 1C = 1,672 mA 
g-1 of sulfur in the cathode. A voltage window between 2.7 and 1.7 V was used. For the rate 
studies, cells were cycled between C/20 to C/3 rate, with 8 cycles at each rate. Voltage ranges of 
1.8 – 2.8 V, 1.75 – 2.9 V, 1.7 – 3.0 V, 1.65 – 3.1 V were used, respectively, for cycling at rates 
of C/20, C/10, C/5, and C/3. Pouch cells were cycled at a C/20 rate for the duration of cycling. 
Pouch cells were initially cycled at 1.65 – 2.6 V. An oxidation of another cell constituent (likely 
a contaminant in the electrolyte) occurred on cycle 20 in the additive-containing cell (indicated 
by an increased charge length and an aberrant voltage profile), after which the upper cutoff 
voltage for the CH3TFA pouch cell was changed to 2.45 V.  Cyclic voltammetry (CV) and 
electrochemical impedance spectroscopy (EIS) were conducted on a BioLogic VSP potentiostat. 
In the CV tests, potential was swept from open circuit voltage to 1.8 V and then back to 2.8 V at 
a rate of 0.05 mV s-1. For EIS studies, a scan range from 106 to 1 Hz was used with a 10 mV 
amplitude perturbation. For cycling studies with Li-Li symmetric cells, a 1 mA cm-2 rate was 
applied for 1 h, after which the current was reversed.
Computational Methods:
The computational resources of the Texas Advanced Computing Center were used for running 
all simulations (URL: http://www.tacc.utexas.edu). All calculations were performed using 
Gaussian 16 Rev. A.03.1 The hybrid DFT method B3LYP was used to optimize structures and 
for all calculations, along with the Pople basis set 6-311++G(d,p). All input molecular structure 
files were prepared with Avogadro Version 1.2.0.2 During calculations, the influence of solvent 
was implicitly modelled via the polarizable continuum model (PCM) using THF solvent, whose
