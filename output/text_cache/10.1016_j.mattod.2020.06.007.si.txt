Figure S1. Snapshots of AIMD simulations for electrolytes after a relaxation for 13 ps. (a) Baseline electrolyte (BE, 6 M LiFSI in DME). (b) TFTFE mixed (HE-2). Green, yellow, red, brown, light blue, and ivory colors represent Li, S, O, C, F, and H, respectively.














Figure S2. Radial distribution function (RDF) between Li and O from salt and solvent molecules. (a) Highly concentrated BE indicates Li+ binds more with oxygen from FSI anion than that of DME. (b) No blue peak was observed at 3 Å or lower, indicating that TFTFE acts as a spectator and does not engage in the formation of Li+-coordination structure.

Figure S4. (a-f) The potential profiles of galvanostatic plating/stripping of LMA in the electrolyte of BE, where the CE exceeds 100%. A capacity of 1.0 mAh cm-2 was plated and subsequently exhaustively stripped from the Cu foil current collector in every cycle (Li||Cu asymmetric cells) at 0.5 mA cm-2. 









Figure S5. Wettability test by measuring a contact angle of single droplet on the carbon coated Al current collector for (a) HE-2 and (b) BE.




Figure S6. GCD potential profiles of a half-cell test with HE-5 as the electrolyte at an E/S ratio of 5 and S@PAN as a cathode. The initial and following discharge capacity values were significantly lower than both BE and HE-2, presumably due to the limited contact of Li+ and sulfur by over-dilution. 












Figure S7. Electrochemical performance of CMK-3/S cathode with HE-2 electrolyte at the E/S ratio of 5 µL mg-1 with the active mass loading of 2.0 mgs/cm2.








Figure S8. Digital photo of BE with S and Li2S in a stoichiometric molar ratio of 5:1 after 48 hours of rest at room temperature inside a glovebox. 










Figure S9. Electrochemical performance of the VS2 cathode. (a) GCD profiles of the VS2 electrode using HE-2 electrolyte. (b) Cycling test up to 100 cycles. 






Figure S10. GCD potential profiles of the 14th cycle of the hybrid cathode under full cell conditions. The erratic and overcharging oxidation profile can be a symptom of electrolyte drying or polysulfide shuttling, which disturbed the following cycles.







Figure S11. Digital image of cycled separator from the hybrid cathode, i.e., VS2 mixed ZnS-coated Li2S@graphene, with HE-2 electrolyte after 14 cycles. Yellow color represents polysulfide dissolution from the cathode.
