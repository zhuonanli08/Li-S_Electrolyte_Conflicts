S1.  Calculating properties of mixed electrolytes 
Table S1: Properties of solvents used in this study 
 
 
Table S1 shows the properties of solvents that were used in this study. HOMO and LUMO energies 
were calculated using DFT with 6-31G* basis set and B3LYP functional. It is worthwhile to note that 
for mixed electrolytes, all properties except dielectric constant and viscosity were calculated by 
volume averaging the properties of the individual solvents present in the mixture.
