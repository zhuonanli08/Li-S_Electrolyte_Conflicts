Experimental 
General: If not noted otherwise, all manipulations were performed in an argon-filled (99.999%) glovebox (GS E-
Line) with < 3 ppm O2 and < 1 ppm H2O gas residual content. Syntheses were done using Schlenk techniques 
under Argon. Solvents were dried using CaH2, followed by a molecular sieve. 
Synthesis of chemicals: DMT was synthesized according to a previously reported method.[1] 
Preparation of electrolytes: If not stated otherwise, the required amount of conductive salt LiTFSI (E-lyte) was 
weighed into a volumetric flask and then dissolved in the solvent. After complete dissolution the flask was filled to 
the respective volume with electrolyte. All electrolyte mixtures can be found in SI Table 1. 
(LiTFSI in TMP, saturated):TTE 1:3 (v:v): LiTFSI was added to TMP until no more dissolution was observed. The 
saturated solution was then filtered and mixed with TTE in a ratio of 1:3 (v:v). 
Electrochemistry: All measurements were performed using a Biologic SP-150 potentiostat.  
(1064 nm), 500 scans, and a step width of 4 cm−1. 
Data Evaluation: Linear regressions were performed using OriginPro 2024.[2] 
CV measurements of Li against Fc and Me10Fc were done by dissolving Fc or Me10Fc in the electrolyte and 
measuring using a Pt-working electrode (WE), a Li auxiliary electrode (AE), and a Li reference electrode (RE) at a 
scan rate of 20 mv/s. They were done in a single container with a lid that had passages for all three electrodes to 
hold them in place during measurement as shown in SI Figure 1.

Raman Measurements: Raman spectroscopy was performed using a Bruker Vertex 70 with an Nd-YAG Laser
