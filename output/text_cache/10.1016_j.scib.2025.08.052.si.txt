To eliminate the influence of the substrate surface, a discharge-charge cycle of 0.5 mA cm−2 
and 5.0 mAh cm−2 was conducted before the test. Then, discharging was performed using 0.5 
mA cm−2 and 5.0 mAh cm−2, followed by 20 cycles of charging and discharging with 0.5 mA 
cm−2 and 0.5 mAh cm−2, and finally charging to 1.0 V under 0.5 mA cm−2.
