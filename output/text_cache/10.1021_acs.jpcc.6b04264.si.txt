UV-vis Spectra Peak Fitting and Parameters 
UV-vis spectra obtained for polysulfide solutions were fit using a series of Gaussian functions in Igor 
Pro’s MultipeakFit peak fitting program.  An example of the peak fitting is shown below in Figure S1, 
where the raw spectrum of the TEGDME, xmix = 4, 50 mM sample is plotted along with the calculated fit 
and the individual Gaussian functions comprising the calculated fit.
