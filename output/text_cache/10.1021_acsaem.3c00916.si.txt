Measurement of ionic conductivity：
The σ of the prepared electrolytes solution at different temperatures was measured using a high-
temperature conductivity cell (HTCC), a sealed 2-pole conductivity cell (two platinum electrodes) 
with a cell constant of 1 cm-1. To measure the EIS of the cell 0.6 mL electrolyte solution was used.
σ= K/R                                                                   (S1)
Where K is the cell constant of the cell and R is the resistance of the electrolyte.

S10
Experimental details of the solubility test:
The elements C, H, N, S (weight %) were detected by the ThermoFisher Flash Smart Elemental 
analyzer. The detection limit was 0.01%. Various polysulfides solution was prepared by adding 
and mixing Li2S, S in T5FDLiTFSI electrolyte (0.3ml, accurately measured by micropipette) based 
on the stoichiometric ratio (i.e. Li2S+S >Li2S2, Li2S+3S >Li2S4, etc.) The total concentration of S 
added was fixed at 5M for all samples. In order to overcome the slow kinetic dissolution process, 
the solution was stirred at an elevated temperature (60 ºC) for two days and followed by continuous 
stirring at R.T. for another two days. The solution was syringe filtered (<0.45 m pore sizes) to 
𝜇
remove the undissolved solids (Li2S and S). The clear supernatant sample was sealed. The 
electrolyte sample was believed to reach the saturation of polysulfide dissolution evidenced by a 
large amount of solid S and Li2S remaining undissolved. After that, the weight % of C, H, N, S 
elements was collected by using the ThermoFisher Flash Smart Elemental analyzer. The adsorbed 
S concentration [S] was determined by molar S/N and subtraction of baseline S concentration.
