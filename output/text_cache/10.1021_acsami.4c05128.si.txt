Computational details 
The present study employed Density Functional Theory (DFT) based calculations using the Linear 
combination of atomic orbitals (LCAO) approximation as incorporated in deMon2k software. The 
geometries for both PyDPP and PhDPP molecules have been generated using GaussView06 
software.1 All the geometries are optimized using deMon2k code2 employing Becke’s three-
parameter hybrid (B3LYP) exchange and correlation functional3 with double zeta-valence 
polarisation (DZVP)4 as a basis set with the dispersion corrections. Vibrational frequencies have 
been calculated to validate the local minima of all the optimized geometries. Every frequency is 
discovered to be positive. The GEN-A2 auxiliary functions5 are incorporated to fit the charge 
density. The convergence criterion for the optimization of geometries and SCF cycles is set as 10−5 
au and 10−8 au. All the clusters have been optimized at their lowest spin state. The Restricted Open 
Shell Kohn Sham (ROKS) formalism is applied to clusters with an odd number of electrons, which 
are thought to have a multiplicity of 2. Clusters with an even number of electrons, however, have 
a spin multiplicity of 1 which are handled using the formalism of Restricted Kohn Sham (RKS). 
Consequently, the RKS formalism is used to optimize DPP molecules while the lithium 
polysulfides are also optimized with the lowest spin multiplicity of 1. Once the DPP molecules are 
optimized, different lithium polysulfides are adsorbed on PyDPP and PhDPP molecules 
considering the electronegative sites in DPP molecules, and the adsorption energies of different 
polysulfides are evaluated as Eads = E(DPP+Li2Sy) – E(DPP) – E(Li2Sy), where E(DPP+Li2Sy) corresponds to 
DPP and Li2Sy complex. E(DPP) corresponds to the energy of a DPP molecule and E(Li2Sy) 
corresponds to the energy of lithium polysulfides.
