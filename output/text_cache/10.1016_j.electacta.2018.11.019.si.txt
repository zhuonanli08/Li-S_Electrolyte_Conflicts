1. Calculation of amount of “free” sulfolane in the of electrolyte 
N = [(*1000) – (Csalt * ММsalt) – (n * Csalt * MМsolvent)] / MМsolvent 
(1.S) 
 
N – amount of “free” sulfolane in the of electrolyte, mol/l 
 – density of electrolyte, g/cm3 
Csalt – concentration of support lithium salt (LiClO4) in electrolyte, mol/l 
ММsalt – molecular weight of support lithium salt (LiClO4), g/mol 
n – average solvate number of the lithium ion (Li+)  
MМsolvent – molecular weight of electrolyte solvent (sulfolane), g/mol
