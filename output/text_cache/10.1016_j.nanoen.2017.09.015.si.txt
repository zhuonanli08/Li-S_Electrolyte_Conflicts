Figure S2. 7Li SS-NMR spectra for the residual SEI from Cu foil electrodes of Cu||Li pouch cells after 100 cycles (stopped in stripped state) at 0.4 mA/cm2 to a 1 mAh/cm2 deposition capacity. The residue formed in [0.5 M LiTFSI + 3 M LiNO3]/G2 is shown in (a) and the residue formed in 1 M LiTFSI/G2 is shown in (b). The asterisk denotes a spinning side band. The overall 7Li NMR spectra in (a) and (b) show peaks are centered at 264.9 and ~0 ppm, corresponding to metallic and non-metallic SEI components, respectively. The non-metallic SEI components were fitted as shown in (c) and (d) for [0.5 M LiTFSI + 3 M LiNO3]/G2 and 1 M LiTFSI/G2, respectively: Li2O (2.8 ppm, red), LiOH (0.7 ppm, gray) and LiTFSI (-1.2 ppm, blue) and unclassified components (green lines) centered at -0.7~-0.8 ppm, which possibly contain LiF (-1.0 ppm) and Li2CO3 (~0 ppm). The Li-containing species of the SEI from 1 M LiTFSI/G2 are mainly composed of LiOH and LiTFSI.













Figure S3. 13C, 1H and 19F SS-NMR spectra for the residual SEI from Cu foil electrodes of Cu||Li pouch cells after 100 cycles (stopped in stripped state) at 0.4 mA/cm2 to a 1 mAh/cm2 deposition capacity. The electrodes for (a), (c) and (e) were cycled in [0.5 M LiTFSI + 3 M LiNO3]/G2 and (b), (d) and (f) were cycled in 1 M LiTFSI/G2 electrolytes. The asterisks in the 19F spectra of (e) and (f) denote a spinning side band. A picture of a Cu||Li pouch cell (Cu surface area = 20.8 cm2) used to obtain enough sample for the NMR analysis is shown in (g).
