Supplementary figure S5. Scatter plots of average chemical contributions to DME versus other physical properties
Scatter plots were drawn when the x-axis is contribution to DME and the y-axis is molecular weight, atom polarizability, bond
polarizability and smr, respectively. In the (a),(b) and (c), contributions to DME is extracted from the model trained with all
chemicals. The respective pearson correlation values versus molecular weight, atom polarizability, bond polarizability and smr
are -0.5, -0.42, -0.20, and -0.36. On the other hand, in the (d),(e) and (f), contributions to DME is extracted from the model
trained with some chemicals. During the training, seen chemicals and unseen chemicals are denoted as blue dots and oragne
dots, respectively. The respective pearson correlation values versus molecular weight, atom polarizability, bond polarizability
and smr are -0.47, -0.47, -0.36, and -0.37.
