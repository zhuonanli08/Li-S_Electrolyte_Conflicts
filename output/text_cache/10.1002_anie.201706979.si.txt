Experimental Section 
Preparation of Materials. 1,2-Dimethosyethane (DME), 1,3-dioxolane (DOL) and dimethyl 
carbonate (DMC) were purchased from Sigma-Aldrich. Lithium bis(fluorosulfonyl)imide (LiFSI) 
was acquired from CoorsTek Fluorochemicals, Inc. Li2S and LiNO3 were from Alfa-Aesar. All 
the materials were used as received. In the electrochemical cell, both the counter and 
reference electrodes were lithium strips. To form the passivation layer on the surface, the 
lithium strips were soaked in 0.5 M LiFSI in DOL/DME solution (1:1) with 1wt% LiNO3 additive 
for 1.5 hours. 
The Li/PS semi-liquid was studied in our research, and the lithium polysulfide (Li2S8) was 
acted as the liquid cathode. The Li2S8 was prepared by mixing stoichiometric amounts of Li2S 
and sulfur in 0.5 M LiFSI in DOL/DME (1:1) at 60 ̊C overnight. 5 mM solution was used in this 
study. Li/PS coincell was also tested with the same Li2S8 catholyte for CV comparison, 
cathode of which is the 8:1 weight ratio mixture of carbon (Ketjenblack) and poly(vinyl 
difluoride) binder, mixed as slurry in N-methyl-2-pyrrolidone and dried at 60 ̊C overnight. In 
addition, the freshly cleaved HOPG (ZYH type, Bruker Corp.) was used as the working 
electrode for the in situ AFM cell. 
Electrochemical AFM. In situ electrochemical AFM experiments were realized by a 
potentiostat/galvanostat coupled with the commercial AFM systems (Bruker Multimode 8 and 
Icon with Nanoscope V controllers) in argon filled gloveboxs owning <0.1 ppm of H2O and <0.1 
ppm of O2. Temperature of the in situ cell can be tested by the heater, and experiments were 
performed when it stabilized. All potentials were referred to Li+/Li. The CVs were recorded in 
the range 1.5 V - 3.0 V at a sweep rate of 1 mV s-1. During the discharge process, the potential 
was swept toward negative potential from OCP (about 2.44 V) to 1.5 V and positive to 3.0 V for 
the subsequent oxidation. HOPG surface was scanned by EC-AFM with an insulating 
triangular silicon nitride AFM tip (force constant of 3 - 26 N m-1) in peak force tapping mode. In 
this study, each of the images needs 3 min 15 s. O ring was used to seal the cell. All 
experiments were carried out in an argon filled glovebox to avoid air contamination. 
Material Characterization. HOPG electrodes were separated from their respective cells via 
constant potential control at specific stages and rinsed by DMC solution to remove the residual 
electrolyte on the surface. It was dried in argon filled glovebox before the ex situ analyses. The 
Raman spectroscopy (Thermo Scientific DXR, 532 nm laser wavelength) and the X-ray 
photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) were applied to characterize the composition of the 
deposited products. The XPS measurements were performed on an ESCALab220i-XL 
electron spectrometer (VG scientific) using 300 W Al Kα radiation (hν = 1486.6 eV). The base 
pressure was about 3×10-9 mbar, and the binding energies were referenced to the 
hydrocarbon C 1s peak at 284.8 eV. Cluster-based calculations were conducted using the 
Gaussian (G09) suite of programs, Becke's three-parameter hybrid method using the 
Lee–Yang–Parr correlation functional (B3LYP) was chosen in this study.
