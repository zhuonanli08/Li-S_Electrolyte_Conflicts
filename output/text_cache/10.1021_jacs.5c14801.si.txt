Methods
2
Preparation of Co-CQD
3
1 g Vitamin B12 (VB12, Beijing J&K Co., Ltd., China) was placed in the center of a quartz boat and 
4
pyrolyzed in a tube furnace under an Ar atmosphere at 250 ℃ for 2 h with a heating rate of 5 ℃ min−1. 
5
The resulting carbon material was hydrolyzed with 10 mL 0.1 M NaOH solution at 80 ℃ for 24 h, 
6
filtered, dialyzed for 3 d, and freeze-dried to obtain Co-CQD.
7
Preparation of CQD
8
1 g 5,10,15,20-Tetraphenylporphyrin (TTP, Bide Pharmatech Co., Ltd., China) was placed in the 
9
center of a quartz boat and pyrolyzed in a tube furnace under an Ar atmosphere at 350 ℃ for 2 h with 
10
a heating rate of 5 ℃ min−1. The resulting carbon material was hydrolyzed with 4 mL concentrated 
11
H2SO4/HNO3 (v/v 3:1) solution at 80 ℃ for 24 h, filtered, dialyzed for 3 d, and freeze-dried to obtain 
12
CQD.
13
Preparation of electrolyte
14
Conventional Li-S electrolyte composed of 1.0 mol L−1 LiTFSI in DOL/DME (1:1, v/v) with 1.0 wt% 
15
LiNO3 was purchased from DoDoChem as the blank sample. Co-CQD or CQD was added to the 
16
electrolyte to prepare homogeneous solutions with concentrations of 20, 100, and 200 ppm, 
17
respectively.
18
Preparation of sulfur cathodes
19
The sulfur (99.99%, Aladdin) and multiwalled carbon nanotube (MWCNT, Chengdu Jiacai 
20
Technology Co., Ltd.) composite was prepared using a simple melt-diffusion method, denoted 
21
S/MWCNT. Specifically, MWCNT and sulfur powder were ground in a mass ratio of 2:8, and then 
22
heated in a 100 mL Teflon-lined stainless-steel autoclave at 155 °C for 12 h. The sulfur content in the 
23
S/MWCNT composite was quantified by thermogravimetric (TG) analysis as ~80 wt% (Figure S49). 
24
The sulfur cathodes were prepared by adding 20 wt% conductive agent (Ketjenblack, purchased from 
25
Guangdong Canrd New Energy Technology Co., Ltd.) and 70 wt% S/MWCNT powder and 10 wt% 
26
PVDF binder in NMP to form a slurry, which was then coated onto an Al foil and dried at 60 °C for 
27
12 h.

Battery Assembly and Electrochemical Measurements
2
The electrochemical performance was evaluated using a CR2032 battery with a sulfur cathode, a 
3
lithium foil, a Celgard 2500 separator, and 1.0 mol L−1 LiTFSI in DOL/DME (1:1, v/v) with 1.0 wt% 
4
LiNO3 as the electrolyte. The sulfur loadings of the cathodes were 1.0, 5.0, 6.0, 9.0 and 13.0 mg cm−2. 
5
Note that the E/S ratios were 5 µL mgS−1 for the sulfur loadings of 6.0, 9.0 and 13.0 mg cm−2. EIS was 
6
measured on the Ivium workstation with a frequency range of 0.01-105 Hz. A Neware battery test 
7
system was used to conduct the discharge/charge measurements in the potential range of 1.7-2.7 V (vs 
8
Li+/Li).
9
Li-S Pouch Cell Assembly
10
S/CNT composites (70 wt%), conductive CNT (20 wt%, purchased from Nanjing/Jiangsu XFNANO 
11
Materials Tech Co., Ltd.) and LA133 binder (10 wt%) in a solid content of 1 g 5 mL−1 (H2O) were 
12
well ground and subsequently dispersed. The obtained slurry was coated on both sides of a carbon-
13
coated aluminum foil by the Heating Tape Cast Coater (Shenzhen Kejing Star Technology Company, 
14
MSK-AFA-HC100), which was then dried at 60 °C for over 24 h, resulting in a sulfur cathode for use 
15
in the pouch-cell assembly. Lithium foils (100 µm) were used as the anodes. PP films (Celgard 2500) 
16
were used as the separators and Al-plastic film was used as the packaging material. The electrolyte 
17
was DOL/DME (1:1, v/v) with 1.0 M LiTFSI, 5.0 wt% LiNO3 and 100 ppm Co-CQD. The assembly 
18
process was a multi-layer superposition, and both sides of the cell were lithium foil because of the 
19
double-coated cathode.
20
Li2S Nucleation Characterization
21
Li2S nucleation was characterized by potentiostatic discharge experiments with the coin cell composed 
22
of carbon paper as the cathode, lithium foil as the anode, 0.2 M Li2S8 and 1.0 M LiTFSI in DOL/DME 
23
(1:1, v/v) with CQD/Co-CQD and without additives as the catholyte. The assembled cells were first 
24
galvanostatically discharged under a current of 0.112 mA until the voltage was 2.1 V, and then 
25
discharged potentiostatically at 2.02 V for Li2S deposition.

DFT Calculations
17
DFT calculations were conducted by the Vienna Ab Initio Simulation Package (VASP) with the 
18
generalized gradient approximation (GGA) method1-3. Electron-ion interactions were treated by the 
19
projector augmented wave (PAW) method and the Perdew-Burke-Ernzerhof (PBE) functional was 
20
used to describe the exchange and correlation energies4. A cutoff energy of 400 eV was applied, and 
21
the Brillouin zone was sampled using a Γ-centered k-point mesh of (1 × 1 × 1). Additionally, van der 
22
Waals interactions (vdWs) between base materials and sulfur species in the LiPSs adsorption 
23
simulations were considered by the DFT+D3 method5. The energy convergence criterion was set to 
24
10–5 eV, and optimization of system structures before and after LiPSs adsorption was conducted until 
25
the Herman-Feynman force of each atom reached below 0.01 eV Å–1.
