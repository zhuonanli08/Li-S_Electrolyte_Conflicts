## 实验部分
2.1. Materials and electrode preparations The high conductive graphene (HCG) was purchased from Suzhou Tanfeng Graphene Technology Co. Ltd. For the HCG/S preparation, the S was infiltrated into HCG using a melt-diffusion strategy at 160 ◦C for 24 h, forming well-contacted and homogeneous HCG/S composites. The cathode materials were prepared by mixing HCG/S, acetylene black, and polyvinylende fluoride (PVDF) binder in a weight ratio of 8: 1: 1 in N- methyl-2-pyrrolidone (NMP). The slurry was then coated onto the aluminum foil and dried at 50 ◦C for 12 h. Afterwards, the dried coated aluminum foil was punched into disks with a diameter of 14 mm for Li-S coin cell use, and the loading of the active-material was ~ 1.1 mg cm-2. 2.2. Preparation of electrolyte Conventional electrolyte (CE), i.e., 1.0 M LiTFSI in 1,3-dioxolane (DOL)/ dimethoxyethane (DME) (1: 1, by vol.), and STE, i.e., 1.0 M LiTFSI in MPE/DOL/DME (4: 3: 3, by vol.), and 2 wt% LiNO3 was employed. The electrolyte was prepared in an Ar-filled glove box.

- Results and discussion With an aim to screen a target solvent with relatively low solvation ability and relatively higher reduction stability, one of the O atoms in the dimethoxyethane (DME) solvent was tentatively substituted with a C atom to eliminate the chelation effect and provide the electron feedback to the H atoms, which results in the conceptual molecule of methyl butyl ether (MBE) (Fig. 1a) [32,33].
- Comparing with the traditional solvent of DME and 1,3-dioxolane (DOL), the MPE solvent carries a shorter chain and the decrease in O atom endows the H atom with a better ability to avoid the nucleophilic attack of PS with high reduction reactivity (Fig. 1a), which are highly favorable for propelling the reac tion kinetics of Li-S chemistry (vide infra).
- Meanwhile, the shrinkage in molecular volume would endow MPE with less steric effect to accelerate Li+ de-solvation process and block the Li+-ether complex, thus increasing the reaction kinetics [32].
