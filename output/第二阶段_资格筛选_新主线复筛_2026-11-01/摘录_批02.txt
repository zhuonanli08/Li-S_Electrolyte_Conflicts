摘要：Abstract In this work, we explore the surface chemistry of Li metal electrodes used in Li–S batteries when employing a very thin (30 μm) lithium metal foil. X‐ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) was used as the main characterisation tool to follow the evolution of the solid electrolyte interphase (SEI) layer on the Li electrode, complemented by scanning electron microscopy. Based on the XPS analyses, the premature capacity fading observed for the cell with thin Li metal electrode could be ascribed to changes in the composition of the surface layer on the Li electrode, due to that the resulting rougher surface more rapidly consume the LiNO3 electrolyte additive.
